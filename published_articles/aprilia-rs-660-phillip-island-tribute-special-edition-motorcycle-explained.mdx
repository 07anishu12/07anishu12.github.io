## 1. Introduction

Aprilia has celebrated its glorious international road racing heritage at EICMA 2026 by unveiling the exquisite Aprilia RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute. Commemorating the legendary Australian circuit where Aprilia has clinched historic World Superbike and Grand Prix victories across three decades, this special edition pays homage to Noale's iconic racing champions. Built around the critically acclaimed RS 660 platform—which redefined the middleweight sportbike category by balancing accessible street ergonomics with authentic racetrack capability—the Phillip Island Tribute features an exclusive competition livery inspired by Aprilia's historic racing liveries, a factory single-seat racing cowl, a bi-directional quickshifter with inverted GP-shift capability, and upgraded Brembo braking hardware.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Aprilia RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute was officially presented to global media on November 3, 2026, at the Aprilia stand at EICMA in Milan. Production will follow as an individually numbered limited production series for the 2027 model year. European and Australian showroom deliveries will commence in March 2027, followed by North American availability in May 2027. For the Indian market, Piaggio Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. confirmed that the Phillip Island Tribute will be available via CBU import through Motoplex dealerships in the second half of 2027.

## 3. Engine and Performance

At the heart of the RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute is Aprilia's forward-facing 659cc, liquid-cooled, DOHC parallel-twin engine derived directly from the front bank of the RSV4 1100 superbike. Featuring a 270-degree crankshaft configuration that delivers V-twin-like exhaust rumble and tractive drive, the engine produces an exhilarating 100 hp (73.5 kW) at 10,500 rpm and 67 Nm of peak torque at 8,500 rpm. The power delivery is characterized by an eager midrange, with over 80 percent of maximum torque available from just 4,000 rpm. Power is managed via a six-speed transmission paired with an assist-and-slipper clutch and the Aprilia Quick Shift (AQS) bi-directional quickshifter, which can be configured electronically for standard road shift or reverse GP-style race shifting.

## 4. Design and hardware

The aesthetic execution of the Phillip Island Tribute is a tribute to Italian racing passion. The bodywork is finished in a vibrant tribute livery combining Aprilia racing purple, red, and matte black, punctuated by the stylized Southern Cross constellation and historic Aprilia racing emblems. The front fairing features twin aerodynamic layered walls that function as integrated downforce wings, channeling high-pressure airflow to stabilize the chassis at speeds approaching 240 km/h. The chassis utilizes an aluminum dual-beam frame with the engine acting as a stressed member. Suspension comprises 41mm inverted Kayaba forks and an adjustable rear monoshock. Brembo four-piston radial calipers clamping 320mm front discs ensure immense, fade-free braking power.

## 5. Features and technology

The technological loadout is powered by the comprehensive APRC (Aprilia Performance Ride Control) electronic package governed by a six-axis IMU. Features include multi-level Cornering ABS, Aprilia Traction Control (ATC) with 8 selectable levels, Aprilia Wheelie Control (AWC), Aprilia Cruise Control (ACC), and three road riding modes (Commute, Dynamic, Individual) alongside two dedicated track modes (Challenge and Time Attack). Cockpit instrumentation features a 4.3-inch full-colour TFT display compatible with Aprilia MIA smartphone connectivity, while an aerodynamic solo seat cowl replaces the pillion perch for track days.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the Aprilia RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute is priced at €12,999, commanding a modest €800 premium over the standard RS 660. In India, where it is imported under CBU tax structures, the estimated ex-showroom price is expected to range between Rs 13.80 lakh and Rs 14.80 lakh. Aprilia positions the Phillip Island Tribute as an aspirational, highly collectible sportbike for riders who want an agile, characterful middleweight with authentic racing pedigree and stunning visual appeal.

With middleweight sportbike popularity booming in India, the tribute model is expected to sell out its allocated domestic units swiftly.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Aprilia RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute competes in the middleweight sportbike segment against the Yamaha YZF-R7 / YZF-R9, the Triumph Daytona 660, the Kawasaki Ninja 650, and the Honda CBR650R. Compared to the Yamaha R7, the RS 660 offers a massive 27 hp power advantage (100 hp vs 73 hp), a comprehensive sixaxis IMU electronics suite, and superior Brembo braking hardware.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What does the Phillip Island Tribute celebrate?

It celebrates Aprilia's historic World Superbike and Grand Prix racing victories at Australia's world-famous Phillip Island circuit.

### Q: How much power does the RS 660 Phillip Island Tribute produce?

The 659cc parallel-twin engine produces 100 hp at 10,500 rpm and 67 Nm of peak torque at 8,500 rpm.

### Q: Can the quickshifter be reversed for GP race shifting?

Yes, the Aprilia Quick Shift (AQS) software allows seamless switching between standard road pattern and reversed GPstyle shift patterns.

### Q: When will this special edition be available in India?

Piaggio India has confirmed domestic availability starting in the third quarter of 2027 through Motoplex dealerships.
