## 1. Introduction

Aprilia has showcased its refreshed entry-level luxury maxi-scooter, the 2027 Aprilia SXR 125, at EICMA 2026 in Milan. Originally conceived to bring Italian touring grandeur and aerodynamic elegance to the high-volume 125cc commuter segment, the SXR 125 has carved a loyal following among urban commuters who desire the commanding presence, generous wind protection, and expansive footroom of a maxi-scooter without the steep purchase and operating costs of a large-displacement motorcycle. For 2027, Aprilia has updated the SXR 125 with refined Euro 5+ engine calibrations, enhanced digital instrumentation, contemporary matte colorways, and upgraded seat foam ergonomics, reinforcing its standing as the most stylish and comfortable 125cc commuter in its class.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Aprilia SXR 125 was presented at the Piaggio Group pavilion during EICMA on November 3, 2026. Manufactured at Piaggio's modern production facilities in Baramati, India, and exported to select international emerging markets and southern Europe, the scooter is scheduled for commercial domestic rollout in India in the first quarter of 2027. International European market deliveries will commence in spring 2027 through Aprilia's global urban scooter distribution network.

## 3. Engine and Performance

Powering the Aprilia SXR 125 is a refined 124.45cc, air-cooled, single-cylinder, four-stroke engine featuring a three-valve (3V) cylinder head and electronic fuel injection. Engineered for bulletproof daily reliability and smooth, progressive acceleration, the engine produces 9.52 hp (7.0 kW) at 7,600 rpm and 9.2 Nm of peak torque at 6,250 rpm. The three-valve architecture (two intake valves and one exhaust valve) improves combustion chamber breathing, delivering crisp throttle pickup from low speeds while minimizing fuel consumption. Power transfers to the rear wheel through an ultra-smooth continuous variable transmission (CVT). The scooter achieves a verified fuel efficiency of over 42 km/l on standard commuter test cycles, yielding an operating range exceeding 290 km from its 7.0-litre fuel cell.

## 4. Design and hardware

The design of the SXR 125 is celebrated for its imposing maxi-scooter silhouette. The front apron is dominated by an aggressive split-LED headlight cluster inspired by the RS 660, crowned by a wide smoked aerodynamic flyscreen. Broad, sculpted body panels flow back into an expansive dual-tier contour seat, redesigned for 2027 with high-density memory foam and contrast red stitching that provides supreme lumbar support for the rider and comfortable passenger seating. Wide, ergonomic floorboards feature front foot pegs that allow the rider to stretch their legs forward in a cruiser-style stance during long highway commutes. The scooter rides on wide 12-inch alloy wheels fitted with 120/70 tubeless tires front and rear.

## 5. Features and technology

The SXR 125 is equipped with an informative 5.7-inch fully digital LCD instrument cluster. The large display provides a digital speedometer, tachometer bar, ambient temperature, battery voltage, twin trip meters, fuel economy readouts, and top speed recorder. The dash is compatible with the Aprilia MIA Bluetooth system, enabling smartphone pairing for turn-by-turn navigation alerts and incoming call management. Practical amenities include a lockable front split glovebox with an integrated 5V USB mobile charger, a retractable luggage hook on the front apron, and a 21-litre underseat storage compartment fitted with a courtesy light.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In India, where the scooter is manufactured with 100 percent local sourcing, the 2027 Aprilia SXR 125 is expected to retail at an ex-showroom price of approximately Rs 1.35 lakh to Rs 1.42 lakh. In European export markets, retail pricing is set around €3,200. Aprilia positions the SXR 125 as a premium, design-led maxiscooter for urban executives, working professionals, and design-conscious riders who prioritize touring comfort, Italian brand prestige, and weather protection over pure sporty firmness.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

In the Indian domestic marketplace, the Aprilia SXR 125 competes directly against the Suzuki Burgman Street 125, the TVS Ntorq 125 XT, and the Honda Grazia 125. Compared to the Suzuki Burgman Street, the Aprilia SXR 125 offers wider 120-section front and rear tires for superior cornering stability, a larger and more comprehensive digital LCD console, and iconic Noale superbike styling.
