## 1. Introduction

Ducati has unveiled the ultimate embodiment of its seventh-generation superbike platform at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the all-new 2027 Ducati Panigale V4 SP (Sport Production). Taking the breathtaking technological advancements of the redesigned 2025/2026 Panigale V4—including its revolutionary double-sided hollow symmetrical swingarm—the 'SP' badge elevates the machine into an uncompromising track weapon. Featuring lightweight five-split-spoke carbon fiber wheels, an authentic STMEVO SBK dry clutch, Brembo Hypure monobloc front brake calipers, an exposed brushed aluminum fuel tank, and an aggressive matte carbon Winter Test livery, the Panigale V4 SP is engineered to deliver professional racing performance directly into the hands of track-day enthusiasts.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Ducati Panigale V4 SP was officially unveiled on November 3, 2026, during Ducati's premiere showcase at EICMA in Milan. Production will take place at Borgo Panigale in limited, individually numbered series with the production number laser-engraved onto the billet aluminum steering head. European showroom deliveries will commence in April 2027. For the Indian market, Ducati India has confirmed that the Panigale V4 SP will be available via bespoke customer pre-orders in late 2027, with extremely limited allocations allocated to the country.

## 3. Engine and Performance

At the heart of the Panigale V4 SP is the 1,103cc Desmosedici Stradale 90-degree V4 engine, derived directly from Ducati's dominant MotoGP machinery. Equipped with a counter-rotating crankshaft and Twin Pulse firing order, the engine produces an astonishing 216 hp (158.9 kW) at 13,500 rpm and 120.9 Nm of peak torque at 11,250 rpm. Installing the included full titanium Akrapovič racing exhaust system increases power to an earthshattering 228 hp while shaving 5 kg off the kerb weight. The SP variant is distinguished by an authentic 9disc STM-EVO SBK dry clutch crafted from billet aluminum, which provides superior mechanical anti-hopping slipper control under aggressive trail-braking and emits the legendary, intoxicating Ducati dry-clutch acoustic chatter.

## 4. Design and hardware

Visually, the Panigale V4 SP is finished in Ducati's revered 'Winter Test' livery: matte black bodywork paired with exposed matte carbon fiber aerodynamic winglets and an unpainted, hand-brushed aluminum fuel tank. The five-split-spoke carbon fiber wheels reduce moment of inertia by 26 percent at the front and 46 percent at the rear compared to cast wheels, resulting in lightning-fast corner transitions. Suspension is commanded by third-generation Öhlins Smart EC 3.0 semi-active suspension with an NPX 25/30 pressurized fork. Braking is executed by Brembo's revolutionary Hypure radial calipers, which are lighter and dissipate heat faster than Stylema units, controlled by a Brembo MCS radial master cylinder with remote span adjustment.

## 5. Features and technology

The electronic brain of the Panigale V4 SP is governed by the latest six-axis Bosch IMU and Ducati's cuttingedge Ducati Vehicle Observer (DVO) algorithm. DVO calculates 70 physical parameters in real-time to optimize Cornering ABS, Ducati Traction Control (DTC) EVO 3, Ducati Slide Control (DSC), and Ducati Wheelie Control (DWC). The 6.9-inch wide-aspect TFT display features a dedicated 'Track Evo' display mode modeled after Ducati Lenovo MotoGP telemetry screens. A GPS module with automatic lap timer, Ducati Data Logger (DDL), adjustable billet aluminum Rizoma footpegs, and carbon-fiber heel guards come standard in the track delivery crate.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Ducati Panigale V4 SP will retail at approximately €41,500. In India, with CBU import duties, the estimated ex-showroom price is expected to sit between Rs 42.00 lakh and Rs 45.00 lakh. Ducati positions the Panigale V4 SP at the very zenith of track-day exotica, targeting passionate collectors, gentleman racers, and track-day perfectionists who demand racing components without compromises.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati Panigale V4 SP rivals the most exotic track superbikes in existence, including the BMW M 1000 RR, the Aprilia RSV4 Factory 1100, and the Honda CBR1000RR-R Fireblade SP. Compared to the BMW M 1000 RR, the Panigale V4 SP offers the emotional drama of an authentic STM dry clutch, the weight advantage of full carbon wheels, and the sublime cornering agility of Ducati's new chassis.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What makes the Panigale V4 SP different from the standard V4 S?

The SP adds carbon fiber wheels, an STM-EVO SBK dry clutch, Brembo Hypure calipers, brushed aluminum tank, Rizoma billet footpegs, GPS lap timer, and the matte Winter Test livery.

### Q: How much lighter are the carbon fiber wheels on the SP?

The carbon fiber wheels reduce moment of inertia by 26% front and 46% rear, dramatically improving turn-in agility.

### Q: How much power does the Panigale V4 SP make?

It produces 216 hp in road-legal trim, increasing to 228 hp with the included full Akrapovič titanium racing exhaust.

### Q: When will the Panigale V4 SP launch in India?

Ducati India will open pre-orders for limited CBU allocations starting in late 2027.
