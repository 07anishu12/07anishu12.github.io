## 1. Introduction

BMW Motorrad has officially introduced the all-new BMW F 450 R naked roadster at EICMA 2026 in Milan, expanding its acclaimed 'Dynamic Roadster' lineage into the rapidly expanding sub-500cc twin-cylinder category. While BMW's single-cylinder G 310 R established an accessible gateway into Bavarian motorcycling, urban riders and canyon enthusiasts demanded the acoustic character, mid-range punch, and electronic sophistication of a multi-cylinder roadster. Built around the brand's newly engineered 420cc parallel-twin engine, the F 450 R inherits the athletic, aggressive styling cues of the flagship S 1000 R, featuring a sharp angular LED headlamp, muscular tank extensions, an exposed tubular trellis frame, and an upright streetfighter cockpit engineered for sharp urban agility and weekend backroad carving.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The BMW F 450 R made its official world debut on November 3, 2026, at the BMW Motorrad stand in Milan. BMW executives confirmed that series production will begin in early 2027 through the company's longstanding strategic manufacturing cooperation with TVS Motor Company in India. European retail availability is scheduled for April 2027, targeted specifically at European A2 licence holders and urban commuters. For the Indian domestic market, BMW Motorrad India plans an official commercial launch in the second half of 2027, with bikes distributed across its nationwide dealership network.

## 3. Engine and Performance

Powering the F 450 R is BMW's newly developed 420cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke parallel-twin engine. Utilizing a 270-degree crankshaft offset for a pulsing V-twin-like exhaust note and tractive power delivery, the engine features double overhead camshafts and four valves per cylinder. It produces an exact 48 hp (35 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 43 Nm of peak torque at 6,750 rpm, hitting the maximum legal limit allowed for European A2 licensing without requiring restrictor kits. The motor is paired with a slick-shifting six-speed gearbox with an anti-hopping slipper clutch and BMW's optional Shift Assistant Pro bi-directional quickshifter, providing seamless clutchless gear shifts during aggressive street riding.

## 4. Design and hardware

In terms of design, the F 450 R embodies pure modern streetfighter aesthetics. It adopts a crouching stance with a compact, low-set LED headlight assembly flanked by sculpted fuel tank fairings that channel cooling air across the radiator. The exposed steel trellis frame is powder-coated in signature BMW motorsport finishes, contrasting against a lightweight cast aluminum swingarm. Ergonomics strike an optimal balance between aggressive intent and day-long urban comfort, featuring a wide aluminum tapered handlebar and moderately rearset foot controls. With a lightweight kerb weight of just 162 kg and an accessible seat height of 805mm, the F 450 R promises effortless low-speed maneuverability through dense city congestion.

## 5. Features and technology

The technological suite on the F 450 R sets a high benchmark for the middleweight naked class. Centered on a brilliant 6.5-inch TFT display, the interface offers comprehensive Bluetooth connectivity, turn-by-turn arrow navigation, call handling, and media controls managed via BMW's intuitive handlebar multi-controller wheel. Rider safety electronics include lean-angle sensitive Cornering ABS, Dynamic Traction Control (DTC), and Engine Drag Torque Control (MSR). Riders can customize throttle response across three standard riding modes: Rain, Road, and Dynamic. Full-LED lighting and a USB-C charging outlet come standard.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European markets, the BMW F 450 R is expected to retail between €6,400 and €6,800, positioned as a premium yet accessible urban roadster. In India, local manufacturing at TVS's Hosur facility guarantees competitive pricing against imported twins. The anticipated ex-showroom price in India is estimated between Rs 3.30 lakh and Rs 3.70 lakh. This positions the F 450 R as a formidable step-up for current single-cylinder naked bike owners who desire multi-cylinder refinement without paying exorbitant import duties.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The BMW F 450 R enters a fiercely contested market segment, going head-to-head with the KTM 390 Duke, the Yamaha MT-03, the Kawasaki Z500, and the Triumph Speed 400. Compared to the KTM 390 Duke, the F 450 R offers twin-cylinder acoustic smoothness and superior high-speed composure. Against the Yamaha MT-03 and Kawasaki Z500, the BMW counters with superior electronic rider aids, a standard 6.5-inch TFT display, and lean-sensitive cornering safety systems.
