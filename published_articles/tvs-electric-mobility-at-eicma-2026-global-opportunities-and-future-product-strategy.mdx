## 1. Introduction

TVS Motor Company delivered an unequivocal statement of global electric ambition at EICMA 2026, using the world's premier two-wheeler stage to unveil its comprehensive international electric mobility roadmap. While European legacy manufacturers have approached electrification cautiously with niche boutique offerings or concept studies, TVS arrived in Milan with battle-tested commercial validation: over 350,000 electric scooters manufactured and sold in India. Anchored by the European commercial debut of its flagship TVS X electric performance crossover and the presentation of a next-generation modular EV platform, TVS demonstrated that it possesses the technological maturity, software capability, and industrial scale required to capture significant market share across Europe, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.

## 2. Launch and Availability

TVS Motor Company's electric mobility presentation was hosted on November 3, 2026, at the TVS pavilion in Hall 11 at Fiera Milano. Managing Director Sudarshan Venu officially announced that commercial European sales of the TVS X will commence in the second quarter of 2027, backed by newly established distribution subsidiaries in Italy, Germany, France, and the UK. Production of international EV models is anchored at TVS's dedicated EV manufacturing plant in Hosur, Tamil Nadu. Furthermore, TVS announced that its mass-market iQube family will expand into southern European and Latin American commercial fleets starting in mid-2027.

## 3. Engine and Performance

The technological vanguard of TVS's electric showcase was the high-performance powertrain of the TVS X. Powered by an advanced permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) producing an impressive 11 kW (15 hp) of peak power and 40 Nm of maximum torque, the machine accelerates from 0-40 km/h in a blistering 2.6 seconds, reaching a top speed of 105 km/h. Energy is supplied by a 4.44 kWh high-density lithium-ion battery pack featuring an integrated aluminum extrusion casing that acts as a structural chassis member. The TVS X delivers a verified real-world range of 140 kilometers on a single charge. Advanced thermal management utilizes ram-air cooling ducts to maintain optimal battery temperatures during high-speed highway cruising.

## 4. Design and hardware

The design language displayed by TVS reflected a radical departure from conventional commuter scooters. The TVS X is built around a cast aluminum perimeter frame (X-Tilt design) paired with an aluminum singlesided swingarm and an offset rear monoshock, achieving a pristine 50:50 front-to-rear weight distribution. Aerodynamic LED lighting includes a quad-LED headlight cluster and integrated daytime running lights. Hardware specifications rival premium motorcycles: 220mm front and 195mm rear disc brakes governed by single-channel ABS, inverted telescopic front forks, and 12-inch alloy wheels shod in high-grip compound tires.

## 5. Features and technology

Technology is TVS's decisive competitive advantage. The TVS X features an astonishing 10.2-inch HD tiltadjustable touchscreen instrument console running the TVS NavPro operating system. The interface offers multi-window widgets, live gaming and video streaming during charging stops, turn-by-turn navigation, and live vehicle telemetry sharing. Electronic safety aids include TVS Smart Xhield, which provides crash detection alerts, geofencing, anti-theft immobilization, and speed limiters. Regenerative braking is customizable across four intensity levels, and the vehicle features cruise control, reverse assist, and keyless smart-card unlocking.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the TVS X will retail at approximately €5,999, positioned as a high-tech, performance alternative to the BMW CE 02 (€8,500). In India, where local manufacturing provides deep economies of scale, the TVS X retails at an ex-showroom price of Rs 2.50 lakh. TVS positions its electric division not as a low-cost alternative, but as an avant-garde technology brand delivering superior performance, connectivity, and design compared to legacy Japanese and European competitors.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

TVS's global electric offensive directly challenges established international EV players. The TVS X rivals the BMW CE 02, the Silence S01 Plus, and the Yamaha NMAX E. In India, it competes against the Ather 450 Apex and Ola S1 Pro, over which it claims superior cast-aluminum chassis engineering and an unmatched 10.2-inch touchscreen display.
