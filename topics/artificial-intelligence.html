Moving Beyond the Single-Model Wrapper

The initial wave of generative AI applications was dominated by thin API wrappers around single commercial models. In enterprise production, however, this paradigm introduces severe risks: sudden model deprecations, unpredictable latency spikes, unformatted hallucinations, and unsustainable token economics.

Modern AI engineering requires a modular, distributed architecture:

  • Multi-Model Orchestration: Allocating lightweight tasks (classification, entity extraction) to fast, cost-effective models (Gemini Flash, DeepSeek), while reserving expensive frontier models (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) for high-complexity synthesis.
  • Deterministic Output Validation: Guaranteeing that model outputs conform exactly to TypeScript/Pydantic schemas before touching relational databases or client interfaces.
  • Model Distillation: Fine-tuning compact models on constrained internal data to cut inference costs by 90%+ while eliminating vendor lock-in.
