Fintech Systems & Product Execution

Fintech product management requires the intersection of software architecture, regulatory compliance, risk modeling, and conversion funnel optimization. In consumer and commercial lending, every friction point directly impacts loan sanction rates, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and non-performing asset (NPA) delinquency ratios. Building successful digital finance products requires engineering trust, verifiable audit trails, and deterministic decision engines.

Core architectural pillars in digital lending and financial product ownership:

  • Loan Origination Systems (LOS) & Rule Engines: Structuring multi-stage credit approval workflows that orchestrate bureau pulls (CIBIL/Experian), bank statement parsing (Account Aggregator), automated rule-based policy checks, and sanction letter generation.
  • Automated KYC & Verification Pipelines: Integrating Aadhaar XML/e-KYC, PAN verification APIs, and bank penny-drop validations into seamless zero-dropoff onboarding flows.
  • Eval-Driven Product Development: Applying rigorous hypothesis testing, experiment tracking, and user retention cohorts rather than intuitive feature additions, tying engineering velocity directly to business outcomes.
  • Financial Risk & Liquidity Alignment: Aligning product-level loan disbursal pacing with underlying institutional balance-sheet liquidity, Net Stable Funding Ratios (NSFR), and cost of capital constraints.
