The Architecture of Modern Lending Platforms

At Drivio Technologies, my day-to-day focus as an Associate Product Manager centers on the operational reality of digital asset financing and two-wheeler loans. A resilient fintech product is not merely a slick consumer UI; it is an orchestrated distributed system spanning:

  • Loan Origination System (LOS): Customer identity onboarding (eKYC, DigiLocker), credit bureau ingestion (CIBIL, Experian), automated underwriting rule engines, and dealer portal workflows.
  • Loan Management System (LMS): Amortization scheduling, NACH/eNACH mandate management, EMI collections, overdue penalty calculation, and foreclosure ledgers.
  • Banking & NBFC Gateway Integrations: Real-time co-lending API protocols, escrow settlement accounts, and core banking system (CBS) reconciliations.

Structural Liquidity: Why NSFR Trumps LCR

Beyond operational software, financial stability depends on structural duration matching. Basel III established the Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) to ensure financial institutions hold sufficient Available Stable Funding (ASF) to match the liquidity risk profiles of their long-term assets (Required Stable Funding, RSF):

\text{NSFR} = \frac{\text{Available Stable Funding (ASF)}}{\text{Required Stable Funding (RSF)}} \ge 100\%

While the short-term Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) protects against 30-day liquidity runs, NSFR diagnoses structural 1-year duration risks, exposing institutions that rely on volatile wholesale commercial paper to fund long-tenure consumer or vehicle loans.
