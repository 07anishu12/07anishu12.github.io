Quick Answer: Between the Suzuki Access 125 and Honda Activa 125, the Suzuki Access 125 delivers higher real-world fuel economy. In mixed Indian conditions, the Suzuki Access 125 (124.0cc, 103 kg) returns 47–52 km/l (benchmark 50 km/l, or ₹2.10/km at ₹105/litre), while the Honda Activa 125 (124.0cc, 110 kg) returns 47–51 km/l (benchmark 49 km/l, or ₹2.14/km), saving ₹43 per 1,000 km on the Suzuki Access 125. Suzuki Access 125 vs Honda Activa 125: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Suzuki Access 125 and Honda Activa 125 requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Suzuki Access 125 pairs a 124.0cc engine (8.58 bhp @ 6,750 rpm, 10.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a 103 kg chassis and claims 52.5 km/l.

The Honda Activa 125 uses a 124.0cc powertrain (8.19 bhp @ 6,250 rpm, 10.4 Nm @ 5,000 rpm) weighing 110 kg with a claimed 60.0 km/l. Their 7 kg mass difference and gearing (CVT Automatic vs V-Matic CVT Automatic) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Suzuki Access 125 features Light 103 kg kerb weight paired with brisk 8.58 bhp SEP engine, Eco Drive Illumination ring, and external tail-mounted fuel filler, returning 48–52 km/l in urban use. The Honda Activa 125 counters with Idling Stop System (auto engine cut-off at signals), real-time fuel economy display, and low-rpm torque peak at 5,000 rpm, achieving 46–50 km/l. Related reading: Suzuki Access 125 city and highway mileage guide.

On open highways, the Suzuki Access 125 reaches 90 km/h and cruises cleanly at 50–65 km/h (51–55 km/l), whereas the Honda Activa 125 tops out at 88 km/h and cruises comfortably at 50–65 km/h (50–54 km/l).

Conditional Verdict: Which Model Fits Your Commute?

Choose the Suzuki Access 125 (₹80,700 – ₹91,300) if you value its 47–52 km/l real-world efficiency, 103 kg weight, and Light 103 kg kerb weight paired with brisk 8.58 bhp SEP engine, Eco Drive Illumination ring, and external tail-mounted fuel filler. Choose the Honda Activa 125 (₹89,256 – ₹96,122) if your riding prioritizes its 124.0cc performance character, 47–51 km/l fuel economy, and 50–65 km/h highway composure.
