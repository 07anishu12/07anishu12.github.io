For any car carrying family members in India, 6 airbags is the minimum configuration you should buy because a 2-airbag car offers zero airbag protection in side-impact (T-bone) collisions and zero airbag coverage for rear-seat passengers. In a 6-airbag car, you get 2 Frontal airbags (steering wheel and dashboard), 2 Side Thorax airbags (built into the front seat bolsters to protect the driver and co-driver's ribs/pelvis), and 2 full-length Roof Curtain airbags that drop down across both front and rear side windows to protect front and back-seat passengers' heads.

Clearing Up the Biggest Misconception: Where Are All 6 Airbags Located?

When a car brochure says '6 Airbags Standard', many buyers look around the rear seats expecting to see four separate airbags in the back. Here is how a 6-airbag (and 7/8-airbag) system is actually engineered inside the cabin:

  1. Airbags 1 & 2 — Dual Frontal Airbags (Driver Steering Hub + Co-Driver Dashboard): Deploy only in moderate-to-severe frontal or near-frontal impacts to keep the front occupants' heads and chests from striking the steering wheel or dash.

  3. Airbags 5 & 6 — Full-Length Side Curtain Airbags (Inside Left & Right Roof Headliner Above Doors): Each curtain airbag is a long continuous cushion spanning from the A-pillar all the way back to the C-pillar.

Why Side-Impact Protection (Airbags 3 to 6) Saves Lives at Indian Intersections

In Indian city and highway driving, un-signalled median cuts, U-turns, and crossroad junctions frequently lead to side-impact (T-bone) crashes. In a side impact, your 2 Frontal Airbags do NOT deploy (because forward deceleration is minimal).

3 Mandatory Safety Rules So Airbags Never Injure Your Family

An airbag is inflated by a pyrotechnic gas generator at 250 to 320 km/h in 30 milliseconds. It is designed as a Supplemental Restraint System (SRS)—meaning it works only when the seatbelt holds you back until the bag has finished expanding:

  1. Never Ride Without a Seatbelt (Front or Rear): If an unbelted passenger flies forward while the airbag is still exploding outward at 300 km/h, the bag strikes their face and neck with devastating force.
  2. Never Put Feet on the Dashboard or Hold a Child on Your Lap: A co-driver resting their feet on the dashboard or holding a baby on their lap in the front seat risks fatal injury if the passenger airbag deploys.
  3. Never Put Rear-Facing Infant Seats in Front of an Active Airbag OR Use Non-Compatible Seat Covers: Standard aftermarket seat covers stitched over front seats trap Airbags 3 & 4 inside the upholstery.

Clear Buying Verdict

  • Treat 6 Airbags as Your Family Floor: Now available from ₹5.60–₹6.30 lakh ex-showroom on models like the Grand i10 Nios, Swift, Baleno, Dzire, Exter, Venue, Sonet, Brezza, Nexon, and XUV 3XO.
  • 7th (Driver Knee) and 8th (Front-Centre) Airbags: Useful bonuses found on ₹22+ lakh SUVs/MPVs (XUV700 AX7L, Fortuner, Carnival), but 6 airbags + ESC + a stable body shell already covers 95% of real-world crash scenarios.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Why didn't the front airbags open when a car was hit from behind or from the side?

Airbag crash sensors only deploy the specific airbags positioned to cushion the occupant along the vector of impact. In a rear-end collision, your body is pushed backward into the padded seat and headrest first—firing the front airbags when you aren't moving forward nicely wastes them for a secondary frontal hit.

Q2. Do airbags protect the middle passenger sitting in the centre of the rear bench?

Curtain airbags (#5 and #6) protect the heads of the two outer window-side rear passengers. The middle rear passenger is furthest from side-door intrusion, so their primary protection is wearing the three-point middle seatbelt.

Q3. Do airbags expire or need replacement after 5 or 10 years if the car never crashes?

Modern solid-propellant airbag modules and clock-spring assemblies are engineered to last the 15-year design life of the vehicle without scheduled replacement, provided the red 'SRS Airbag' warning light on your speedometer turns off normally 4 seconds after starting the car.

Q4. How much does it cost to replace deployed airbags after an accident in India?

If frontal or curtain airbags deploy in a crash, the airbag modules, dashboard skin/headliner, seatbelt pretensioners, and SRS control module must all be replaced together—typically costing ₹65,000 to ₹1,80,000, which is fully covered if you carry a comprehensive Zero-Depreciation insurance policy.

Q5. Can I install a Ganesh idol or mobile phone holder on top of the passenger dashboard?

Never glue any idol, perfume bottle, or phone mount onto the right half of the passenger dashboard where the 'SRS AIRBAG' stamp is embossed. When the passenger airbag explodes outward at 300 km/h, any object glued to the cover turns into a high-velocity projectile.
