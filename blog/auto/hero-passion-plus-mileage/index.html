Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Hero Passion Plus

Evaluating hero passion plus mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Hero Passion Plus is certified at 70.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: Hero Splendor Plus city and highway mileage guide. Related reading: Hero Glamour 125 claimed vs real-world fuel efficiency.

Driven by a 97.2cc (Air-cooled, 4-stroke, single-cylinder OHC with XSens FI and i3S) motor producing 7.91 bhp @ 8,000 rpm and 8.05 Nm @ 6,000 rpm through a 4-speed constant mesh transmission, the Hero Passion Plus weighs 115 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes 11-litre fuel tank, digi-analogue cluster, side-stand engine cut-off, and plush single-piece seat. Across variants (Standard Drum Self-Start Alloy i3S (OBD2B compliant)), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Hero Passion Plus to 60–64 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (11-litre fuel tank, digi-analogue cluster, side-stand engine cut-off, and plush single-piece seat) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 50–60 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 63–67 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 11.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.
