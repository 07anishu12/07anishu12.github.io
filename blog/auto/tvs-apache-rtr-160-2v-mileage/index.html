Speed-Wise Fuel Economy, Top Speed (107 km/h) and Gearing

Aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed, rapidly increasing fuel demand above 65 km/h. At 35–45 km/h in 4th or top gear, the TVS Apache RTR 160 2V operates under light load and returns 47–49 km/l unless traffic forces frequent downshifts.

Its peak thermal efficiency window is 70–85 km/h in top gear, delivering 49–51 km/l on open roads. Pushing toward its 107 km/h flat-road top speed triggers rich fuel mapping, dropping economy to 42–44 km/l at 80 km/h and 36–38 km/l near full throttle.
