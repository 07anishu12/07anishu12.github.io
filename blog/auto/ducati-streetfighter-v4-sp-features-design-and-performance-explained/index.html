1. Introduction

Ducati has unleashed the most extreme, technologically advanced naked hyperbike in its history at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the 2027 Ducati Streetfighter V4 SP. Stripping the fairings from the revolutionary seventh-generation Panigale V4 and fitting high, wide handlebars, the Streetfighter V4 SP injects the 'Fight Formula' with track-bred 'Sport Production' hardware. Powered by a screaming 1,103cc Desmosedici Stradale V4 engine delivering 208 hp, the SP edition cuts rotating mass with split-spoke carbon fiber wheels, incorporates an authentic STM-EVO SBK dry clutch, and integrates third-generation Öhlins Smart EC 3.0 semi-active suspension. Wrapped in an ominous matte black Winter Test livery with an exposed brushed aluminum fuel tank, the Streetfighter V4 SP represents the absolute zenith of naked motorcycle performance.

2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Ducati Streetfighter V4 SP made its public debut on November 3, 2026, at the Ducati stand during EICMA in Milan. Production will take place at Borgo Panigale in limited, individually numbered series, with the production sequence engraved onto the billet handlebar clamp. European showroom deliveries will begin in May 2027. For the Indian market, Ducati India has confirmed that the Streetfighter V4 SP will arrive in late 2027 on a strictly allocated customer pre-order basis through its exclusive dealership network.

3. Engine and Performance

Propelling the Streetfighter V4 SP is the 1,103cc Desmosedici Stradale 90-degree V4 engine with desmodromic valvetrain and a counter-rotating crankshaft. In road-legal specification, the engine unleashes a ferocious 208 hp (153 kW) at 13,000 rpm and 123 Nm of peak torque at 9,500 rpm. Fitting the optional full titanium Akrapovič racing exhaust increases power to an astonishing 220 hp while reducing weight by 6 kg. The SP edition features an authentic 9-disc STM-EVO SBK billet aluminum dry clutch, ensuring positive mechanical slipper engagement during brutal downshifts into tight hairpins while producing the unmistakable mechanical cadence revered by Ducatisti worldwide.

4. Design and hardware

The visual menace of the Streetfighter V4 SP is dominated by biplane carbon fiber aerodynamic wings mounted on the radiator shrouds, which generate 28 kg of downforce at 270 km/h, preventing front-wheel loft under hard acceleration and increasing high-speed braking stability. The five-split-spoke carbon fiber wheels reduce rotational inertia by 26 percent at the front and 46 percent at the rear, allowing instant sideto-side directional changes. Suspension is handled by Öhlins Smart EC 3.0 semi-active suspension with an Öhlins steering damper. Braking is commanded by track-grade Brembo Stylema R calipers with racing-spec ventilated pistons, actuated by a Brembo MCS 19/21 radial master cylinder with remote span adjustment.

5. Features and technology

Electronics on the Streetfighter V4 SP are governed by a six-axis IMU utilizing the advanced Ducati Vehicle Observer (DVO) algorithm. DVO coordinates Cornering ABS EVO, Ducati Traction Control (DTC) EVO 3, Ducati Slide Control (DSC), and Ducati Wheelie Control (DWC) EVO. The 5.0-inch TFT display features an exclusive Track Evo display mode, showing live lean angle, G-meter readouts, and split lap times recorded by the standard GPS module. Adjustable billet aluminum Rizoma foot controls with carbon heel guards and carbonfiber front mudguards complete the bespoke package.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Ducati Streetfighter V4 SP carries a retail price of approximately €37,500. In India, where luxury CBU import duties apply, the estimated ex-showroom price is projected between Rs 38.00 lakh and Rs 41.00 lakh. Ducati positions the Streetfighter V4 SP as the ultimate collector-grade hypernaked motorcycle for riders who demand unrestricted MotoGP performance in an upright, aggressive streetfighter stance.

7. India launch possibility

8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati Streetfighter V4 SP competes at the extreme summit of the naked hyperbike category against the BMW M 1000 R, the KTM 1390 Super Duke R Evo, and the MV Agusta Brutale 1000 RR. Against the BMW M 1000 R, the Streetfighter V4 SP counters with lighter carbon fiber wheels, an authentic dry clutch, biplane carbon wings, and the emotional visceral soul of its Desmosedici Stradale V4.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much horsepower does the Streetfighter V4 SP make?

The 1,103cc V4 engine generates 208 hp at 13,000 rpm, increasing to 220 hp with the full titanium Akrapovič racing exhaust.

Q: What are the advantages of carbon wheels on the Streetfighter V4 SP?

The carbon wheels reduce moment of inertia by 26% front and 46% rear, drastically quickening corner turn-in and acceleration.

Q: Does it feature an authentic dry clutch?

Yes, the SP edition comes fitted with an STM-EVO SBK 9-disc billet aluminum dry clutch that provides superior slipper action and classic acoustic chatter.

Q: When will the Streetfighter V4 SP arrive in India?

Ducati India will open pre-orders for limited CBU allocations starting in late 2027.

10. Conclusion

The 2027 Ducati Streetfighter V4 SP unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an absolute tour de force in the hyper-naked motorcycle universe. By uniting 208 hp of Desmosedici Stradale power with featherweight carbon wheels, an authentic dry clutch, Öhlins Smart EC 3.0 suspension, and biplane downforce wings, Ducati has created an adrenaline-pumping naked machine that redefines the outer boundaries of speed and control.
