1. Introduction

Yamaha has unleashed a seismic shockwave across the maxi-scooter and adventure touring categories at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the all-new 2027 Yamaha RW700. Challenging the uncontested hegemony of the Honda X-ADV, the RW700 represents Yamaha's audacious entry into the heavy crossover adventure-scooter segment. Built around Yamaha's legendary 689cc crossplane CP2 parallel-twin engine, the RW700 combines authentic dual-sport ergonomics, long-travel suspension, wire-spoked tubeless wheels, and rugged rally styling with the sheer touring comfort of a luxury maxi-scooter. The headline innovation is the standard fitment of Yamaha's newly refined Y-AMT (Yamaha Automated Manual Transmission), giving riders full electronic control over clutchless manual finger-shifting or completely autonomous automatic riding.

2. Launch and Availability

The Yamaha RW700 made its global debut on November 3, 2026, at the Yamaha stand during EICMA in Milan. Yamaha confirmed that the RW700 will be offered in two distinct trims: the standard RW700 and the longdistance, fully accessorized RW700 Explore. Commercial rollouts across European and Australian markets will begin in May 2027, meeting strict Euro 5+ emissions standards. Regarding the Indian market, India Yamaha Motor has not officially confirmed domestic distribution, though the model could be imported in limited numbers as a halo lifestyle vehicle in late 2027.

3. Engine and Performance

Propelling the RW700 is Yamaha's acclaimed 689cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke, DOHC, eight-valve paralleltwin CP2 engine. Featuring a 270-degree crankshaft configuration, the engine is world-renowned for its torquey, linear power delivery and indestructible mechanical reliability. In the RW700, the motor develops 73.4 hp (54 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 68 Nm of peak torque at 6,500 rpm. The transmission is commanded by Yamaha's Y-AMT automated manual gearbox, which replaces traditional manual clutch levers and foot shifters with electro-mechanical actuators. Riders can toggle between fully automatic 'D' (Drive) and 'D+' (Sport) modes, or engage full manual 'MT' mode to shift gears instantaneously using handlebar-mounted seesaw paddle switches.

4. Design and hardware

Visually, the RW700 exudes a purposeful overland Dakar silhouette. The front fascia incorporates sharp twinLED projector lights beneath an electrically adjustable touring windscreen. Sturdy aluminum crash protectors, integrated handguards, and an aluminum bash plate shield mechanical components from trail debris. The chassis is built around a high-tensile steel tubular backbone frame paired with an aluminum swingarm. Suspension is handled by 43mm inverted KYB front forks delivering 160mm of travel, and a rear KYB monoshock supplying 150mm of travel. Wire-spoked tubeless rims (17-inch front and 15-inch rear) are fitted with dual-purpose Pirelli Scorpion Rally STR tires, providing 175mm of ground clearance.

5. Features and technology

The technological cockpit is dominated by a 7.0-inch full-colour TFT display equipped with full smartphone connectivity, Garmin turn-by-turn navigation, and music playback controls. The Y-AMT system is integrated with Yamaha Ride Control (YRC), allowing riders to tailor throttle response, engine braking, and traction control across three preset modes (Sport, Street, Rain) and two customizable User modes. Additional adventure luxuries include Smart Key keyless operation, an electronic central stand, cruise control, heated grips on the Explore variant, and a 34-litre lighted underseat compartment capable of housing an off-road helmet with peak.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the standard Yamaha RW700 is projected to retail at approximately €11,800, with the fully loaded RW700 Explore commanding €12,900, positioning it directly against the Honda X-ADV (€13,200). In India, if imported via the CBU route, the estimated ex-showroom price would range between Rs 12.50 lakh and Rs 14.00 lakh. Yamaha positions the RW700 as an avant-garde lifestyle explorer for affluent riders who want touring comfort without sacrificing rugged adventure capabilities.

8. Rivals and alternatives

The Yamaha RW700 faces its primary rival in the benchmark Honda X-ADV 750. While both offer multicylinder power and automated gearboxes, the Yamaha RW700 leverages the superior emotional character, visceral crossplane exhaust note, and lighter weight of its celebrated CP2 engine. It also provides an enticing alternative to conventional adventure bikes such as the Yamaha Ténéré 700 and Suzuki V-Strom 800DE for riders seeking step-through weather protection.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What engine is used in the Yamaha RW700?

The scooter uses Yamaha's acclaimed 689cc liquid-cooled CP2 parallel-twin engine producing 73.4 hp and 68 Nm of torque.

Q: How does the Yamaha Y-AMT transmission work on the RW700?

Y-AMT uses electronic actuators to manage the clutch and gear shifting, providing fully automatic driving or clutchless manual shifting via handlebar paddle switches.

Q: Does the RW700 have wire-spoked wheels?

Yes, it features 17-inch front and 15-inch rear cross-spoked tubeless wheels shod in Pirelli Scorpion Rally STR dualpurpose tires.

Q: Will the Yamaha RW700 be launched in India?

Yamaha has not officially confirmed an Indian launch, though limited CBU imports through Blue Square dealerships remain possible in late 2027.

10. Conclusion

The 2027 Yamaha RW700 unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an audacious, brilliantly engineered assault on the crossover adventure category. By uniting the soulful performance of the CP2 engine with cutting-edge YAMT automated shifting, authentic off-road chassis hardware, and sumptuous maxi-scooter luxury, Yamaha has created a machine that defies categorization. It stands as one of the most versatile, exciting motorcycles of the modern era.
