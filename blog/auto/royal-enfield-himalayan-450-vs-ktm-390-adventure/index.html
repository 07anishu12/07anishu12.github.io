Quick Answer: Comparing the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 and KTM 390 Adventure, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 delivers better real-world fuel efficiency. In mixed Indian conditions, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 (451.65cc, 196 kg) returns 27–31 km/l (benchmark 28 km/l, or ₹3.75/km at ₹105/litre), while the KTM 390 Adventure (373.2cc, 177 kg) returns 25–29 km/l (benchmark 27 km/l, or ₹3.89/km), saving ₹139 per 1,000 km on the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450. Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 vs KTM 390 Adventure: Engine Specs, Weight & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Evaluating Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 against KTM 390 Adventure requires separating official ARAI claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 uses a 451.65cc engine (39.47 bhp @ 8,000 rpm, 40.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) in a 196 kg chassis with a claimed 30.0 km/l. Related reading: Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 highway and mountain road mileage. Related reading: Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450 city vs highway fuel economy.

The KTM 390 Adventure features a 373.2cc powertrain (42.9 bhp @ 9,000 rpm (up to 45.3 bhp on 399cc gen), 37.0 Nm @ 7,000 rpm (39.0 Nm on 399cc gen)) weighing 177 kg and claiming 28.0 km/l. Their 19 kg weight difference and gearing (6-speed with Assist and Slipper Clutch vs 6-speed with PASC Slipper Clutch and Quickshifter+) drive their real-world efficiency gap.

City Traffic Tractability, Highway Touring Range and Performance

In urban traffic, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 leverages Sherpa 450 liquid-cooled engine, 6-speed gearbox, 17-litre tank, 43mm Showa USD forks (200mm travel), and Performance/Eco ride modes to return 25–28 km/l, while the KTM 390 Adventure uses Cornering ABS, Motorcycle Traction Control (MTC), Offroad mode, WP APEX suspension, and lighter 177–182 kg wet weight to achieve 24–27 km/l.

On open highways, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 reaches 151 km/h and cruises best at 95–115 km/h (29–33 km/l, ~476 km tank range), whereas the KTM 390 Adventure reaches 155 km/h and cruises cleanly at 100–120 km/h (28–31 km/l, ~392 km tank range). Conditional Verdict: Which Motorcycle Suits You Best?

Choose the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 (₹2,85,000 – ₹3,08,029) if you prefer its 451.65cc torque delivery, 17.0L tank range, and Sherpa 450 liquid-cooled engine, 6-speed gearbox, 17-litre tank, 43mm Showa USD forks (200mm travel), and Performance/Eco ride modes. Choose the KTM 390 Adventure (₹2,84,000 – ₹3,64,000) if your riding benefits more from its 177 kg weight, 25–29 km/l real-world efficiency, and Cornering ABS, Motorcycle Traction Control (MTC), Offroad mode, WP APEX suspension, and lighter 177–182 kg wet weight.
