TVS Apache RTR 180 vs TVS Apache RTR 160 4V: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing TVS Apache RTR 180 and TVS Apache RTR 160 4V requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The TVS Apache RTR 180 pairs a 177.4cc engine (16.78 bhp @ 9,000 rpm (Sport) / 14.3 bhp (Urban/Rain), 15.5 Nm @ 7,000 rpm) with a 140 kg chassis and claims 43.0 km/l.

The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V uses a 159.7cc powertrain (17.31 bhp @ 9,250 rpm (Sport) / 15.42 bhp (Urban/Rain), 14.73 Nm @ 7,250 rpm) weighing 146 kg with a claimed 45.0 km/l. Their 6 kg mass difference and gearing (5-speed vs 5-speed with GTT) shape their real-world fuel gap. Related reading: TVS Apache RTR 160 2V real-world efficiency and top speed. City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The TVS Apache RTR 180 features Stronger low-to-mid-range punch (15.5 Nm at 7,000 rpm) and 6 kg lighter chassis than RTR 160 4V, returning 38–42 km/l in urban use. The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V counters with 4-valve oil-cooled engine with O3C cooling, double-cradle Split Synchro Stiff frame, Showa monoshock, and 3 ride modes, achieving 40–44 km/l. Related reading: TVS Apache RTR 160 4V city and highway mileage guide.

On open highways, the TVS Apache RTR 180 reaches 113 km/h and cruises cleanly at 75–90 km/h (42–45 km/l), whereas the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V tops out at 114 km/h and cruises comfortably at 75–90 km/h (44–48 km/l).

Conditional Verdict: Which Model Fits Your Commute?

Choose the TVS Apache RTR 180 (₹1,33,220) if you value its 39–43 km/l real-world efficiency, 140 kg weight, and Stronger low-to-mid-range punch (15.5 Nm at 7,000 rpm) and 6 kg lighter chassis than RTR 160 4V. Choose the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V (₹1,24,870 – ₹1,39,990) if your riding prioritizes its 159.7cc performance character, 40–45 km/l fuel economy, and 75–90 km/h highway composure.
