Choose the Maruti Suzuki Baleno if you want a no-compromise family hatchback that retains a smooth four-cylinder engine, delivers 16–18 km/l in city traffic, costs ₹50,000–₹95,000 less variant-for-variant, and commands stronger resale value. Choose the Hyundai i20 if you want a genuinely upscale cabin experience with an electric sunroof, Bose sound system, superior plastic quality, and a stepless IVT (CVT) automatic—and you accept 11.5–13.5 km/l city mileage.

Why the Baleno Retained Its 4-Cylinder Edge While Cutting Running Costs

Unlike the smaller Swift (which moved to a 3-cylinder Z-Series engine), the Maruti Baleno continues to use the refined 1.2-litre four-cylinder DualJet K12N petrol engine (90 PS, 113 Nm) with idle start-stop. Paired with a kerb weight of 925–960 kg, it is one of the most efficient four-cylinder petrol cars in India.

The Hyundai i20 also uses a refined 1.2-litre four-cylinder Kappa petrol engine (83 PS on MT / 88 PS on IVT, 114.7 Nm). However, because the i20 is heavier and geared differently, its real-world city fuel economy trails the Baleno by a consistent 4 to 4.5 km/l.

Head-to-Head Sweet Spot Comparison: Baleno Zeta vs i20 Sportz

For manual buyers around ₹9.50 lakh on-road and automatic buyers between ₹9.50 lakh and ₹10.60 lakh, the Zeta and Sportz variants represent the peak value points in both lineups.

The Automatic Divide: Baleno AGS (AMT) vs i20 IVT (CVT)

If you are buying a manual, the Baleno's light clutch and slick 5-speed shifter match the i20 closely. If you are buying an automatic, the two cars diverge sharply:

  • Hyundai i20 Sportz/Asta IVT: Uses a belt-and-pulley Continuously Variable Transmission. In stop-start Bengaluru, Mumbai, or Delhi traffic, it accelerates smoothly with zero gear-shift pause. It costs roughly ₹1.10 lakh more than the manual i20.
  • Maruti Baleno Zeta/Alpha AGS: Uses a 5-speed Automated Manual Transmission costing only ₹50,000 more than the manual. While well-calibrated with standard Hill-Hold Assist, you will feel a slight pause between 1st and 2nd gear. In exchange, you save ₹65,000–₹85,000 upfront and ~₹18,000 per year on petrol.

Cabin Space, Ride Comfort & Safety

Both hatchbacks offer generous rear knee room that outperforms several sub-4m SUVs. The i20 has a 30 mm wider body (1,775 mm vs 1,745 mm) and a flatter rear bench, making it slightly more comfortable when three adults sit abreast. The Baleno counters with softer, more absorbent suspension damping over broken urban patches, UV-cut window glass (Zeta/Alpha), and a crisp 360-degree camera + Head-Up Display on the top Alpha trim (₹9.42 lakh ex-showroom). Both cars now provide 6 airbags, ESP/ESC, Hill-Hold Assist, and three-point seatbelts as standard; the Baleno scored 4 stars in Bharat NCAP (2025 test on 6-airbag spec: 26.52/32 AOP).

Clear Buying Recommendation

  • Buy the Maruti Baleno (Zeta MT or Alpha MT/AGS) if: You want 4-cylinder smoothness with 16–18 km/l city mileage, a 360-degree camera on top trims, lower purchase price, and high resale value.
  • Buy the Hyundai i20 (Sportz IVT or Asta (O)) if: Your priority is a true jerk-free CVT automatic in heavy traffic, an electric sunroof, Bose 7-speaker audio, and the most premium interior ambience under ₹11.5 lakh.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Did Maruti improve the Baleno's sheet metal and safety rating in the latest generation?

Yes. The second-generation Baleno uses thicker outer panels, heavier suspension components, and standard 6 airbags + ESP across all variants, earning a 4-star Adult Occupant Protection score under Bharat NCAP testing.

Q2. How much extra will I spend on petrol if I buy the Hyundai i20 IVT instead of the Baleno AGS?

If you drive 1,000 km per month in city traffic (11.5 km/l in i20 IVT vs 16.0 km/l in Baleno AGS at ₹102/L), the i20 IVT will cost roughly ₹9,040/month in fuel versus ₹6,375/month for the Baleno—a difference of ₹2,665 per month or ₹32,000 per year.

Q3. Is the Toyota Glanza identical to the Maruti Baleno?

Mechanically and structurally, yes—both are built on the same production line with identical engines, features, and dimensions. The Glanza offers a slightly different front bumper/grille design and a standard 3-year/1,00,000 km warranty (versus Maruti's 3-year/1,00,000 km updated warranty).

Q4. Does the Maruti Baleno offer a sunroof in any variant?

No. Maruti does not offer a sunroof on the Baleno; buyers who want a Maruti with a sunroof must step up to the Fronx/Brezza/Grand Vitara bracket, whereas the Hyundai i20 offers a factory sunroof starting from the Sportz (O) and Asta trims.

Q5. Which car has better headlights for night highway driving?

Both the Baleno Alpha (LED projector headlamps) and i20 Asta (LED MFR headlamps) offer strong night visibility, though on mid trims (Zeta vs Sportz), the Baleno Zeta already includes LED projector headlamps while the i20 Sportz uses halogen units.
