Quick Answer: Comparing the Royal Enfield Hunter 350 and TVS Ronin, the TVS Ronin delivers better real-world fuel efficiency. In mixed Indian conditions, the Royal Enfield Hunter 350 (349.34cc, 181 kg) returns 33–37 km/l (benchmark 35 km/l, or ₹3.00/km at ₹105/litre), while the TVS Ronin (225.9cc, 160 kg) returns 38–42 km/l (benchmark 40 km/l, or ₹2.62/km), saving ₹375 per 1,000 km on the TVS Ronin. Royal Enfield Hunter 350 vs TVS Ronin: Engine Specs, Weight & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Evaluating Royal Enfield Hunter 350 against TVS Ronin requires separating official ARAI claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Royal Enfield Hunter 350 uses a 349.34cc engine (20.2 bhp @ 6,100 rpm, 27.0 Nm @ 4,000 rpm) in a 181 kg chassis with a claimed 36.2 km/l.

The TVS Ronin features a 225.9cc powertrain (20.1 bhp @ 7,750 rpm, 19.93 Nm @ 3,750 rpm) weighing 160 kg and claiming 42.95 km/l. Their 21 kg weight difference and gearing (5-speed constant mesh vs 5-speed with Assist and Slipper Clutch) drive their real-world efficiency gap.

City Traffic Tractability, Highway Touring Range and Performance

In urban traffic, the Royal Enfield Hunter 350 leverages 14 kg lighter than Classic 350 (181 kg vs 195 kg), 17-inch front and rear wheels, shorter 1,370mm wheelbase, and brisk urban throttle map to return 32–35 km/l, while the TVS Ronin uses 160 kg kerb weight (21 kg lighter than Hunter 350), peak torque at just 3,750 rpm, 41mm Showa USD forks, and Glide Through Technology to achieve 38–42 km/l. Related reading: Royal Enfield Classic 350 vs Hunter 350 mileage comparison. Related reading: best bikes for office commuting in heavy traffic.

On open highways, the Royal Enfield Hunter 350 reaches 114 km/h and cruises best at 80–95 km/h (36–39 km/l, ~455 km tank range), whereas the TVS Ronin reaches 120 km/h and cruises cleanly at 80–95 km/h (41–45 km/l, ~560 km tank range).

Conditional Verdict: Which Motorcycle Suits You Best?

Choose the Royal Enfield Hunter 350 (₹1,49,900 – ₹1,74,655) if you prefer its 349.34cc torque delivery, 13.0L tank range, and 14 kg lighter than Classic 350 (181 kg vs 195 kg), 17-inch front and rear wheels, shorter 1,370mm wheelbase, and brisk urban throttle map. Choose the TVS Ronin (₹1,30,809 – ₹1,72,700) if your riding benefits more from its 160 kg weight, 38–42 km/l real-world efficiency, and 160 kg kerb weight (21 kg lighter than Hunter 350), peak torque at just 3,750 rpm, 41mm Showa USD forks, and Glide Through Technology.
