Ather 450X vs TVS iQube: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Ather 450X and TVS iQube requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Ather 450X offers 2.9 kWh / 3.7 kWh paired with a 6.4 kW PMSM mid-drive motor (26 Nm motor torque) (90 km/h top speed) and claims 111 km IDC (2.9 kWh) / 150 km IDC (3.7 kWh). Related reading: Ather 450X claimed vs real range across riding modes.

The TVS iQube provides 2.2 kWh (Base) / 3.4 kWh (Standard & S) driving a 4.4 kW BLDC hub-mounted motor (140 Nm wheel torque) (75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) top speed) with a claimed range of 75 km (2.2 kWh) / 100–114 km IDC (3.4 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Ather 450X highlights Hybrid aluminium perimeter chassis, 50:50 weight distribution, multi-level regenerative braking (Magic Twist), Traction Control, and Google Maps on 7-inch touchscreen, offering 22 litres of under-seat storage and a charging time of 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 700W Duo charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W charger).

Meanwhile, the TVS iQube counters with Silent hub motor with zero belt maintenance, 32-litre under-seat storage on 3.4 kWh, and broad pan-India TVS dealership service support, providing 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) of storage and charging in 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (4.15 kWh wall draw for 3.7 kWh on Ather 450X vs 3.85 kWh wall draw for 3.4 kWh on TVS iQube), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Conditional Verdict: Which Electric Scooter Should You Choose?

Choose the Ather 450X (₹1,40,599 – ₹1,54,999) if you prioritize its 85–90 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 105–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco/Eco) Eco range, 22 litres storage, and Hybrid aluminium perimeter chassis, 50:50 weight distribution, multi-level regenerative braking (Magic Twist), Traction Control, and Google Maps on 7-inch touchscreen. Choose the TVS iQube (₹94,999 – ₹1,29,444) if your routine benefits more from its 65 km (2.2 kWh) / 90–100 km (3.4 kWh) range, 75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) top speed, and Silent hub motor with zero belt maintenance, 32-litre under-seat storage on 3.4 kWh, and broad pan-India TVS dealership service support. Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Ather 450X or TVS iQube?

In Eco mode, Ather 450X delivers 85–90 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 105–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco/Eco) (benchmark 110 km) while TVS iQube delivers 65 km (2.2 kWh) / 90–100 km (3.4 kWh) (benchmark 90 km), giving Ather 450X the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Ather 450X vs TVS iQube?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Ather 450X costs ₹0.26/km and TVS iQube costs ₹0.30/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Ather 450X charges in 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 700W Duo charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W charger), whereas TVS iQube takes 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Ather 450X provides 22 litres compared to 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) on TVS iQube.

Which has a higher top speed?

Ather 450X reaches 90 km/h compared to 75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) for TVS iQube.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Ather 450X and TVS iQube?

Ather 450X costs ₹1,40,599 – ₹1,54,999 ex-showroom, while TVS iQube costs ₹94,999 – ₹1,29,444.
