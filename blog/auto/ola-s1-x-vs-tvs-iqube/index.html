Ola S1 X vs TVS iQube: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Ola S1 X and TVS iQube requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Ola S1 X offers 2.0 kWh / 3.0 kWh / 4.0 kWh paired with a 6.0 kW peak hub-mounted BLDC motor (Gen 2) / 7 kW mid-drive (Gen 3) (85 km/h (2 kWh) / 90 km/h (3 kWh & 4 kWh) top speed) and claims 95 km IDC (2 kWh) / 151 km IDC (3 kWh) / 190 km IDC (4 kWh).

The TVS iQube provides 2.2 kWh (Base) / 3.4 kWh (Standard & S) driving a 4.4 kW BLDC hub-mounted motor (140 Nm wheel torque) (75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) top speed) with a claimed range of 75 km (2.2 kWh) / 100–114 km IDC (3.4 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Ola S1 X highlights Physical key ignition on S1 X trims, segmented LCD display, twin telescopic forks, and 3 battery sizes tailored to daily commute budgets, offering 34 litres of under-seat storage and a charging time of 5 hr 00 min (2 kWh) / 7 hr 24 min (3 kWh with 500W charger; faster 750W optional).

Meanwhile, the TVS iQube counters with Silent hub motor with zero belt maintenance, 32-litre under-seat storage on 3.4 kWh, and broad pan-India TVS dealership service support, providing 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) of storage and charging in 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (3.40 kWh wall draw for 3.0 kWh on Ola S1 X vs 3.85 kWh wall draw for 3.4 kWh on TVS iQube), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Ola S1 X or TVS iQube?

In Eco mode, Ola S1 X delivers 70–75 km (2 kWh) / 100–115 km (3 kWh) / 125–135 km (4 kWh) (benchmark 105 km) while TVS iQube delivers 65 km (2.2 kWh) / 90–100 km (3.4 kWh) (benchmark 90 km), giving Ola S1 X the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Ola S1 X vs TVS iQube?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Ola S1 X costs ₹0.23/km and TVS iQube costs ₹0.30/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Ola S1 X charges in 5 hr 00 min (2 kWh) / 7 hr 24 min (3 kWh with 500W charger; faster 750W optional), whereas TVS iQube takes 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Ola S1 X provides 34 litres compared to 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) on TVS iQube.

Which has a higher top speed?

Ola S1 X reaches 85 km/h (2 kWh) / 90 km/h (3 kWh & 4 kWh) compared to 75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) for TVS iQube.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Ola S1 X and TVS iQube?

Ola S1 X costs ₹74,999 – ₹1,04,999 ex-showroom, while TVS iQube costs ₹94,999 – ₹1,29,444.
