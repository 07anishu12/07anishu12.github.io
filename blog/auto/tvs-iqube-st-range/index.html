Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 5.3 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 5.95 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 145 km per charge equals 3.66 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 4.10 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.

Charging time for the TVS iQube ST is 4 hr 18 min (0–80% on 5.3 kWh with 950W off-board charger). Keeping the lithium-ion pack between 20% and 80% State of Charge (SoC) maximizes battery life, meaning a scooter with 145 km real range comfortably supports a 94 km daily round trip with a healthy reserve.
