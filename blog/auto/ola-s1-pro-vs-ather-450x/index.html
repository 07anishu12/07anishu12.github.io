Ola S1 Pro vs Ather 450X: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Ola S1 Pro and Ather 450X requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Ola S1 Pro offers 4.0 kWh (Standard S1 Pro) / 5.2–5.3 kWh (S1 Pro+) paired with a 11.0 kW peak mid-drive IPM motor (Gen 2 4 kWh) / up to 13 kW (Gen 3 Pro+) (120 km/h (4 kWh) top speed) and claims 195 km IDC (4 kWh) / 242–320 km IDC (Pro+).

The Ather 450X provides 2.9 kWh / 3.7 kWh driving a 6.4 kW PMSM mid-drive motor (26 Nm motor torque) (90 km/h top speed) with a claimed range of 111 km IDC (2.9 kWh) / 150 km IDC (3.7 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Ola S1 Pro highlights 11 kW peak motor (0–40 km/h in 2.6 seconds), 120 km/h top speed, 4 ride modes (Eco, Normal, Sport, Hyper), and 34-litre flat-floor boot, offering 34 litres of under-seat storage and a charging time of 4 hr 50 min (0–80%) / 6 hr 30 min (0–100% with 750W portable home charger).

Meanwhile, the Ather 450X counters with Hybrid aluminium perimeter chassis, 50:50 weight distribution, multi-level regenerative braking (Magic Twist), Traction Control, and Google Maps on 7-inch touchscreen, providing 22 litres of storage and charging in 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 700W Duo charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W charger).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (4.50 kWh wall draw for 4.0 kWh on Ola S1 Pro vs 4.15 kWh wall draw for 3.7 kWh on Ather 450X), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Conditional Verdict: Which Electric Scooter Should You Choose?

Choose the Ola S1 Pro (₹1,29,999 – ₹1,59,999) if you prioritize its 130–145 km (4 kWh in Eco) / 160–185 km (5.3 kWh Pro+ in Eco) Eco range, 34 litres storage, and 11 kW peak motor (0–40 km/h in 2.6 seconds), 120 km/h top speed, 4 ride modes (Eco, Normal, Sport, Hyper), and 34-litre flat-floor boot. Choose the Ather 450X (₹1,40,599 – ₹1,54,999) if your routine benefits more from its 85–90 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 105–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco/Eco) range, 90 km/h top speed, and Hybrid aluminium perimeter chassis, 50:50 weight distribution, multi-level regenerative braking (Magic Twist), Traction Control, and Google Maps on 7-inch touchscreen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Ola S1 Pro or Ather 450X?

In Eco mode, Ola S1 Pro delivers 130–145 km (4 kWh in Eco) / 160–185 km (5.3 kWh Pro+ in Eco) (benchmark 130 km) while Ather 450X delivers 85–90 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 105–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco/Eco) (benchmark 110 km), giving Ola S1 Pro the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Ola S1 Pro vs Ather 450X?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Ola S1 Pro costs ₹0.24/km and Ather 450X costs ₹0.26/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Ola S1 Pro charges in 4 hr 50 min (0–80%) / 6 hr 30 min (0–100% with 750W portable home charger), whereas Ather 450X takes 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 700W Duo charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W charger).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Ola S1 Pro provides 34 litres compared to 22 litres on Ather 450X.

Which has a higher top speed?

Ola S1 Pro reaches 120 km/h (4 kWh) compared to 90 km/h for Ather 450X.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Ola S1 Pro and Ather 450X?

Ola S1 Pro costs ₹1,29,999 – ₹1,59,999 ex-showroom, while Ather 450X costs ₹1,40,599 – ₹1,54,999.
