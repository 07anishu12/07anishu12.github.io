1. Introduction

Ducati has updated its award-winning power cruiser at EICMA 2026 with the global reveal of the 2027 Ducati Diavel V4. Since transitioning from the venerable Testastretta twin to the state-of-the-art V4 Granturismo engine, the Diavel V4 has stood as an uncontested master of design and muscular performance, combining the commanding presence of a power cruiser with the dynamism of a supersport roadster. For the 2027 model year, Borgo Panigale engineers have introduced key refinements: updated extended cylinder deactivation software for cooler urban riding, new carbon-fiber exterior components, recalibrated electronic suspension algorithms, and striking new livery options. The 2027 Diavel V4 reaffirms Ducati's dominance in the ultra-luxury performance cruiser segment.

2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Ducati Diavel V4 was presented at EICMA on November 3, 2026, at the Ducati stand in Hall 15. Ducati confirmed that commercial deliveries in European markets will begin in March 2027, with North American arrivals following in May 2027. For the Indian market, Ducati India has confirmed that the updated 2027 Diavel V4 will arrive in the second half of 2027 as a CBU import from Thailand and Italy, available through authorized Ducati dealerships across major metropolitan centers.

3. Engine and Performance

At the core of the Diavel V4 is the 1,158cc V4 Granturismo engine, an engineering tour de force designed without desmodromic valves to achieve extraordinary 60,000 km valve clearance service intervals. The engine produces a staggering 168 hp (124 kW) at 10,750 rpm and 126 Nm of peak torque at 7,500 rpm. For 2027, Ducati has enhanced its extended cylinder deactivation strategy: the rear cylinder bank now seamlessly disengages not only when stationary at traffic lights, but also during low-speed cruising below 4,000 rpm, substantially reducing heat soak onto the rider and cutting urban fuel consumption. Power transfers via a sixspeed transmission equipped with the Ducati Quick Shift (DQS) up/down system.

4. Design and hardware

The 2027 Diavel V4 retains its unmistakable muscular silhouette, dominated by a massive 240/45-ZR17 rear tire mounted on a five-spoke alloy wheel with diamond-cut finishes. Visual updates for 2027 include sculpted carbon-fiber air intake scoops, a redesigned quadruple-exit exhaust system with ceramic black heat shields, and a new 'Dark Stealth' matte finish alongside classic Ducati Red. The motorcycle utilizes a lightweight aluminum monocoque frame attached directly to the cylinder heads, keeping kerb weight to a nimble 223 kg. Suspension is commanded by 50mm fully adjustable front inverted forks and a cantilever rear monoshock, while braking is anchored by twin 330mm front discs with Brembo Stylema calipers.

5. Features and technology

The technological cockpit is centered on a 5.0-inch TFT colour display supporting the Ducati Multimedia System (DMS) for turn-by-turn navigation and smartphone integration. Electronic rider aids, governed by a six-axis Bosch IMU, include Cornering ABS, Ducati Traction Control (DTC), Ducati Wheelie Control (DWC), and Ducati Power Launch (DPL). The 2027 model introduces revised riding modes: Sport, Touring, Urban, and a newly optimized Wet mode that softens throttle response to 115 hp for maximum stability in poor weather conditions. Cruise control, full-LED lighting with an iconic C-shaped DRL, and dynamic turn indicators complete the package.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Ducati Diavel V4 carries a base retail price of approximately €27,500, with optional carbon packages pushing prices to €31,000. In India, where it is imported under CBU duty structures, the expected ex-showroom price is estimated between Rs 27.50 lakh and Rs 29.50 lakh. Ducati positions the Diavel V4 as the ultimate high-status performance cruiser for affluent riders who demand exotic Italian design, crushing straight-line acceleration, and unexpected cornering agility.

8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati Diavel V4 occupies a distinct luxury niche with few direct rivals, its closest competitors being the Triumph Rocket 3 R / GT and the Harley-Davidson Sportster S. Compared to the massive 2.5-litre Triumph Rocket 3, the Diavel V4 offers a significantly lighter chassis (over 68 kg lighter), sharper sports-bike handling dynamics, and more advanced electronic lean-angle rider aids.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much power does the 2027 Ducati Diavel V4 make?

The 1,158cc V4 Granturismo engine generates 168 hp at 10,750 rpm and 126 Nm of peak torque at 7,500 rpm.

Q: What are the key updates on the 2027 Diavel V4?

The 2027 model gains extended cylinder deactivation during low-speed riding, redesigned carbon fiber air scoops, updated exhaust shielding, and refreshed color options.

Q: What is the valve service interval on the Diavel V4?

Thanks to the non-desmodromic spring valvetrain, the V4 Granturismo requires valve clearance checks only every 60,000 km.

Q: When will the 2027 Diavel V4 launch in India?

Ducati India has confirmed an official launch scheduled for the third quarter of 2027.

10. Conclusion

The 2027 Ducati Diavel V4 unveiled at EICMA 2026 exemplifies Italian design excellence and mechanical artistry at the highest level. With 168 hp of V4 Granturismo ferocity, improved thermal comfort via extended cylinder deactivation, and astonishing sports-bike handling despite a 240mm rear tyre, the Diavel V4 remains the undisputed king of luxury power cruisers.
