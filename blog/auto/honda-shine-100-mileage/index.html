Quick Answer: The Honda Shine 100 (98.98cc) delivers a real-world mileage of 61–65 km/l in city traffic, 64–67 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 60–65 km/l, against a claimed figure of 65.0 km/l. At 63 km/l and an illustrative petrol price of ₹105/litre, its running cost is ₹1.67/km (₹1.62/km in Delhi).

Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Honda Shine 100

Evaluating honda shine 100 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Honda Shine 100 is certified at 65.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Key efficiency hardware includes Class-lightest 99 kg kerb weight, 786mm low seat height, external fuel pump, and offset cylinder eSP engine. City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Honda Shine 100 to 61–65 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Class-lightest 99 kg kerb weight, 786mm low seat height, external fuel pump, and offset cylinder eSP engine) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 50–60 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 64–67 km/l. Related reading: Honda Shine 125 2026 claimed vs real-world fuel economy.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 9.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Honda Shine 100 in 2026?

The Honda Shine 100 delivers 61–65 km/l in city traffic, 64–67 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 60–65 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Honda Shine 100?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Honda Shine 100 is 65.0 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Honda Shine 100?

At 63 km/l, fuel cost is ₹1.62/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹1.77/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹1.67/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Honda Shine 100?

With its 9.0-litre tank and 60–65 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 540–585 km.

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on Honda Shine 100?

Cruising steadily at 50–60 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Honda Shine 100 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
