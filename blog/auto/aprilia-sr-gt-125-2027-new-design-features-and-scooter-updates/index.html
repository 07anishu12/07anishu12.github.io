1. Introduction

Aprilia has sharpened its urban adventure credentials at EICMA 2026 with the global unveiling of the updated 2027 Aprilia SR GT 125. Pioneering the 'urban adventure' scooter concept in the entry-level European category, the SR GT combines Aprilia's racing DNA and sharp aesthetics with the rugged stance and longtravel suspension of a crossover vehicle. For the 2027 model year, Noale engineers have refreshed the SR GT 125 with updated front bodywork, striking new graphics packages, enhanced LCD instrument connectivity, and refinements to the class-leading 15 hp liquid-cooled i-get engine. Built to effortlessly absorb potholes, tram tracks, and cobblestones while delivering sporty handling, the 2027 SR GT 125 stands as the benchmark performance scooter in the European A1 category.

2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Aprilia SR GT 125 was officially revealed on November 3, 2026, at the Piaggio Group stand during EICMA in Milan. Aprilia confirmed that series production is underway at the company's European manufacturing facilities, with commercial deliveries across Italy, Spain, Germany, and the UK scheduled for February 2027. Regarding the Indian domestic market, Piaggio Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. (PVPL) has showcased the SR GT family at domestic expos and is actively assessing local production at its Baramati plant for late 2027.

3. Engine and Performance

Powering the SR GT 125 is Piaggio Group's state-of-the-art 125cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke, single-cylinder i-get engine featuring electronic fuel injection and a four-valve cylinder head. The engine produces the maximum legal power allowed for the European A1 licence class: an impressive 15 hp (11 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 12 Nm of peak torque at 6,500 rpm. The powerplant is equipped with the RISS (Regulator Inverter Start & Stop) system, which replaces the traditional starter motor with a brushless direct-mount electrical device on the crankshaft, delivering whisper-quiet starts and superior fuel economy rated at 40 km/l on the WMTC cycle. A refined CVT automatic transmission ensures instantaneous, responsive acceleration off the line.

4. Design and hardware

The styling of the 2027 SR GT 125 draws heavy inspiration from Aprilia's RSV4 and Tuono superbikes. The front fairing features a full-LED tri-headlight cluster flanked by aerodynamic side vents and an upright darktinted touring screen. High-tensile steel double-cradle frame construction provides outstanding torsional rigidity. Suspension hardware is class-leading, comprising 33mm Showa front forks delivering 122mm of travel (22% more than standard urban scooters) and twin Showa rear shocks with 102mm of travel. Petal wave brake discs (260mm front, 220mm rear) and generous 175mm ground clearance ensure effortless navigation over harsh urban obstacles.

5. Features and technology

Cockpit instrumentation is commanded by an advanced fully digital LCD display that connects to smartphones via the optional Aprilia MIA connectivity platform, enabling turn-by-turn navigation prompts, incoming call notifications, and music management via handlebar buttons. The scooter features wide, motorcycle-style naked aluminum handlebars mounted on a cast clamp. Practical amenities include a 25-litre underseat storage bay capable of swallowing a full-face helmet, a front glove compartment with a USB charging port, and a large 9.0-litre central fuel tank that provides a riding range exceeding 350 km.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Aprilia SR GT 125 carries an introductory retail price of approximately €4,199, with the up-spec SR GT Sport variant retailing at €4,399. In India, if Piaggio localizes manufacturing at its Baramati facility, an estimated ex-showroom price of Rs 1.40 lakh to Rs 1.60 lakh would position the SR GT as a premium performance alternative to the TVS Ntorq and Yamaha Aerox 155, targeting young enthusiasts seeking sporty Italian design.

7. India launch possibility

Note: India Market Status: Strong Possibility, Supported By Evidence

Strong possibility, supported by evidence. Piaggio India

manufactures the Aprilia SR 125/160 range domestically and has actively surveyed Indian consumers regarding introducing the rugged SR GT series. With demand for liquid-cooled performance scooters rapidly climbing in India, reliable industry reporting indicates that Piaggio is developing a localized variant of the SR GT chassis for commercial launch in India in late 2027.

8. Rivals and alternatives

In Europe, the Aprilia SR GT 125 competes against the Honda ADV125 and the Kymco DT X125. In the Indian market, its primary rivals would be the Yamaha Aerox 155 and the Aprilia SXR 125/160. Against the Aerox 155, the SR GT 125 counters with longer suspension travel (122mm Showa forks), higher ground clearance (175mm vs 145mm), and an aggressive motorcycle-style naked handlebar setup.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much power does the 2027 Aprilia SR GT 125 make?

The scooter produces 15 hp (11 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 12 Nm of torque at 6,500 rpm, the maximum legal limit for European A1 licensing.

Q: What makes the SR GT an 'urban adventure' scooter?

It features long-travel 122mm Showa front forks, twin Showa rear shocks, generous 175mm ground clearance, and allterrain dual-purpose tires.

Q: Will the Aprilia SR GT 125 launch in India?

There is a strong possibility of an Indian launch in late 2027, with Piaggio India evaluating localized domestic manufacturing.

Q: What is the fuel economy of the SR GT 125?

The scooter delivers approximately 40 km/l on the WMTC cycle, providing an operating range of over 350 km from its 9-litre fuel tank.

10. Conclusion

The 2027 Aprilia SR GT 125 unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an exhilarating evolution of the urban commuter. By fusing Aprilia's unmistakable racing pedigree with long-travel Showa suspension, 175mm ground clearance, and an energetic 15 hp i-get engine, Noale has created a crossover scooter that turns mundane city streets and rough gravel paths into thrilling riding territory.
