Choose between TVS iQube and Bajaj Chetak by matching battery, required daily range and equipment before comparing price. The iQube family spans several current battery sizes; Chetak's 35-series variants and optional packages are also distinct. A family-level range maximum does not describe the version on your quotation.

Start with a genuinely comparable pair

The current TVS iQube table lists 2.3, 3.1, 3.5, 4.7 and 5.3 kWh families, with a separate edition sharing one capacity. Older 2.2 or 3.4 kWh articles should not be copied into a current variant list without qualification.

The Chetak FAQs identify multiple models, including 3501, 3502, 3503 and 3001. Bajaj's 35-series announcement describes the 3.5 kWh platform. Confirm the latest full specification and local availability for the exact offered Chetak.

A useful starting pair is the quoted iQube 3.5 kWh against a verified Chetak 3501 or 3502, rather than the smallest iQube against a flagship Chetak. Equal installed capacity is only a starting point: usable energy, efficiency, equipment and charger can still differ.

Compare range with its measurement label

TVS lists 145 km IDC for the current 3.5 kWh iQube, while Chetak's FAQ lists 153 km under its stated IDC conditions for 3501/3502. The documented claimed difference is 8 km. This does not establish that Chetak will travel exactly 8 km farther on your commute.

Neither figure is an independent result from this guide. Load, speed, temperature, road conditions and battery age affect real travel. Plan from conservative matching evidence and retain a reserve rather than choosing solely on a small certification difference.

For a hypothetical daily trip of 40 km, a personal planning allowance of 60 km including diversions may be more relevant than the maximum printed number. That allowance is a planning example, not a guarantee that either scooter will achieve a specific real-world range.

Compare the same charging window

TVS's current table gives a manufacturer 10–80% charging duration of 3 hours 45 minutes for its 3.5 kWh family. Bajaj's 35-series release quotes 0–80% in three hours for the described package. These windows differ, so do not subtract them as though they measured the same task under identical conditions.

Check the supplied charger, socket requirements and charging location before purchase. Get appropriate electrical advice for the actual circuit and avoid improvised extension arrangements. A portable charger does not make every outlet or parking location suitable.

Ask whether public charging is supported and useful for the exact scooter, connector and network. Do not assume all electric scooters can use any car fast charger. For a daily commuter, reliable overnight charging can matter more than a headline rapid-charge claim you cannot access.

Price the functions and commercial terms

Chetak's FAQ explains that some functions depend on TecPac activation. Establish the included period, future charges and what works without it. TVS's table also distinguishes display sizes and types across iQube variants; a large touchscreen from the ST should not be assigned to the entry version.

Inspect storage with your helmet and riding kit, then bring the regular pillion for a fit check. Marketing capacity and a photograph do not establish that your items or passengers fit comfortably. Test parking, reverse assistance if fitted and the actual weight you must manage.

Compare battery/vehicle warranty, charger coverage and local service arrangements. No comparative reliability ranking was established. A nearby sales outlet is not proof of immediate high-voltage service capability; ask where diagnosis and repair actually happen.

Choose the variant that covers your week

Start with the Chetak 35-series shortlist when its verified 153 km IDC package and chosen TecPac/equipment arrangement suit the commute at the better complete quote. Start with iQube when its wider documented capacity range lets you select a smaller-budget or larger-reserve configuration that better matches your needs.

A hypothetical ₹15,000 premium spread over five years is ₹250 monthly before financing and resale. Decide whether the extra verified battery/equipment benefit is worth that amount, then include installation, insurance and subscriptions. No current transaction prices or incentives are asserted here; obtain dated city-specific offers.

Quick questions, clear answers

Is the biggest iQube range the range of every iQube?

No. The current table assigns different capacities and IDC figures to different variants. Match the complete model name and battery on the quote. A family-level advertisement highlighting the ST maximum cannot describe an entry configuration automatically.

Can I compare 0–80% with 10–80% charging times?

Only with the differing windows clearly stated. They are not directly identical tests, and charger power or battery conditions can differ too. Ask for matching-window information before declaring a charging-speed winner.

Are optional software packs just cosmetic?

Not necessarily. Chetak's FAQ links some functions to TecPac status. Check exactly what is included, what expires and the ongoing cost. Do the same for any iQube package rather than assuming every demonstrated function remains available indefinitely.
