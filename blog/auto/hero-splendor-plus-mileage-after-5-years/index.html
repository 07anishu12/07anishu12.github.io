Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Hero Splendor Plus

Evaluating hero splendor plus mileage after 5 years requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Hero Splendor Plus is certified at 70.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating.

Key efficiency hardware includes i3S (Idle Stop-Start System) cuts the engine after 5 seconds of neutral idling in traffic and restarts via clutch pull. Across variants (Drum Brake (Self Start Alloy), i3S Drum, Black and Accent, XTEC 2.0 (Digital Console & LED)), core engine mapping remains identical. How Hero Splendor Plus Mileage Evolves Over 5 Years of Ownership

During the initial 500–750 km run-in phase, tight piston rings cause the Hero Splendor Plus to return 3–5 km/l below its peak. Between 2,000 and 25,000 km (Years 1–3), internal friction settles and the 97.2cc engine achieves 60–66 km/l with timely oil services.

By Year 5 (40,000–60,000+ km), neglected units lose 8–14 km/l due to five main wear factors: (1) slipping clutch plates, (2) carbon-fouled injector nozzles or carburettor jets, (3) tight valve clearances reducing compression, (4) dry or stretched drive chains, and (5) dragging brake shoes.
