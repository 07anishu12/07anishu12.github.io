Quick Answer: Between the Hero Xtreme 125R and TVS Raider 125, the TVS Raider 125 delivers higher real-world fuel economy. In mixed Indian conditions, the Hero Xtreme 125R (124.7cc, 136 kg) returns 52–58 km/l (benchmark 56 km/l, or ₹1.88/km at ₹105/litre), while the TVS Raider 125 (124.8cc, 123 kg) returns 54–60 km/l (benchmark 57 km/l, or ₹1.84/km), saving ₹33 per 1,000 km on the TVS Raider 125. Hero Xtreme 125R vs TVS Raider 125: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Hero Xtreme 125R and TVS Raider 125 requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Hero Xtreme 125R pairs a 124.7cc engine (11.4 bhp @ 8,250 rpm, 10.5 Nm @ 6,500 rpm) with a 136 kg chassis and claims 66.0 km/l.

The TVS Raider 125 uses a 124.8cc powertrain (11.2 bhp @ 7,500 rpm, 11.2 Nm @ 6,000 rpm (up to 11.75 Nm with iGO Assist)) weighing 123 kg with a claimed 67.0 km/l. Their 13 kg mass difference and gearing (5-speed constant mesh vs 5-speed) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Hero Xtreme 125R features Segment-first single-channel ABS option, 37mm telescopic forks, 120/80-17 wide radial rear tyre, and Showa rear monoshock, returning 54–58 km/l in urban use. The TVS Raider 125 counters with 3-valve air-and-oil-cooled engine, two ride modes (Eco with IntelliGO stop-start and Power), under-seat storage, and 5-step monoshock, achieving 55–59 km/l. Related reading: best bikes for office commuting in heavy urban traffic.

On open highways, the Hero Xtreme 125R reaches 107 km/h and cruises cleanly at 65–80 km/h (57–61 km/l), whereas the TVS Raider 125 tops out at 99 km/h and cruises comfortably at 65–80 km/h (58–62 km/l).

Conditional Verdict: Which Model Fits Your Commute?

Choose the Hero Xtreme 125R (₹95,000 – ₹99,500) if you value its 52–58 km/l real-world efficiency, 136 kg weight, and Segment-first single-channel ABS option, 37mm telescopic forks, 120/80-17 wide radial rear tyre, and Showa rear monoshock. Choose the TVS Raider 125 (₹84,869 – ₹1,04,330) if your riding prioritizes its 124.8cc performance character, 54–60 km/l fuel economy, and 65–80 km/h highway composure.
