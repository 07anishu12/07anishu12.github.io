Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 3.4 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 3.85 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 90 km per charge equals 3.78 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 4.28 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.

Charging time for the TVS iQube is 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W). Keeping the lithium-ion pack between 20% and 80% State of Charge (SoC) maximizes battery life, meaning a scooter with 90 km real range comfortably supports a 58 km daily round trip with a healthy reserve.
