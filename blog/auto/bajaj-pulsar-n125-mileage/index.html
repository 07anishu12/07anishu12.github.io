Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Bajaj Pulsar N125

Evaluating bajaj pulsar n125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Bajaj Pulsar N125 is certified at 58.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Bajaj Pulsar N125 to 52–56 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (15 kg lighter than classic Pulsar 125 (125 kg kerb weight), 198mm ground clearance, and ISG auto start-stop) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 65–75 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 55–59 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 9.5L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.
