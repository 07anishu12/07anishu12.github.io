1. Introduction

Yamaha has completed its aggressive dual-pronged assault on the adventure-scooter category at EICMA 2026 by unveiling the all-new 2027 Yamaha RW350 alongside its larger twin-cylinder sibling, the RW700. Serving as the middleweight cornerstone of Yamaha's newly minted 'RW' (Redefine the World) crossover family, the RW350 is engineered to dominate the popular 350cc urban adventure segment currently led by the Honda ADV350. Powered by an all-new 327cc liquid-cooled single-cylinder Blue Core engine, the RW350 combines rugged rally-raid bodywork, long-travel suspension, and dual-purpose tires with the seamless everyday twist-and-go simplicity of an advanced V-belt CVT transmission. It represents the perfect crossover tool for urban professionals seeking weekday commuter efficiency and weekend trail escape.

2. Launch and Availability

The Yamaha RW350 made its global debut at EICMA 2026 in Milan on November 3, 2026. Yamaha announced that series commercial production will commence in spring 2027 at Yamaha's European assembly facilities. European market deliveries are slated to begin in June 2027, followed by Australia and select Southeast Asian markets later in the year. Regarding the Indian marketplace, India Yamaha Motor has not made an official launch announcement, though the burgeoning popularity of performance maxi-scooters provides a compelling domestic business case.

3. Engine and Performance

At the core of the RW350 is an all-new 327cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke, single-cylinder SOHC four-valve engine developed under Yamaha's advanced Blue Core philosophy. Featuring an offset cylinder, low-friction roller rocker arms, and optimized intake porting, the engine produces 29.2 hp (21.5 kW) at 7,500 rpm and 31.5 Nm of peak torque at 5,750 rpm. Power is managed through a newly engineered continuously variable transmission (CVT) featuring heavy-duty clutch centrifugal shoes and reinforced belts calibrated to handle technical off-road slip and rapid city acceleration. Fuel economy is rated at an efficient 3.4 litres per 100 km (29.4 km/l) on the WMTC cycle, affording a touring range of over 380 km from its 13.0-litre fuel cell.

4. Design and hardware

The RW350 projects an athletic, Dakar-inspired stance. The front fairing features twin high-output LED projector lamps crowned by a four-stage manually adjustable touring windscreen. Solid knuckle handguards, integrated aluminum lower skid plates, and durable side crash cladding protect the vehicle against loose stones and trail hazards. The chassis utilizes a high-tensile tubular steel backbone frame designed to minimize chassis flex over broken surfaces. Suspension is commanded by 37mm inverted KYB front forks delivering 145mm of travel, paired with twin gas-charged KYB rear shock absorbers featuring remote piggyback reservoirs and 135mm of travel. Rugged alloy wheels (15-inch front and 14-inch rear) are shod in dual-sport tires, yielding an impressive ground clearance of 160mm.

5. Features and technology

The cockpit of the RW350 is outfitted with a high-definition 5.0-inch TFT display offering comprehensive smartphone connectivity via the Yamaha Y-Connect application. The system provides turn-by-turn Garmin navigation, incoming call and message alerts, and fuel consumption tracking. The safety suite features Yamaha's Traction Control System (TCS), which can be switched off for gravel riding, and dual-channel ABS. Urban convenience is elevated by Yamaha's Smart Key keyless ignition system, a spacious 38-litre underseat storage box that accommodates two full-face helmets, and a weather-sealed glovebox containing a USB Type-C fast charger.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Yamaha RW350 is projected to carry an introductory retail price between €6,700 and €7,200, positioning it directly against the Honda ADV350 (€7,100). For the Indian market, if imported via the CKD assembly route, the estimated ex-showroom price would range between Rs 4.50 lakh and Rs 5.00 lakh. Yamaha will position the RW350 as a premium urban adventure crossover for riders who want maxi-scooter luxury and off-road capability without the intimidating weight of a 700cc twin.

7. India launch possibility

8. Rivals and alternatives

The primary rival to the Yamaha RW350 is the benchmark Honda ADV350, alongside the Kymco DT X360 and the BMW C 400 X. Compared to the Honda ADV350, the Yamaha RW350 offers lighter, more agile handling, a distinctive rugged design language, inverted front forks, and superior underseat helmet capacity.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What engine powers the Yamaha RW350?

The scooter is powered by an all-new 327cc, liquid-cooled, single-cylinder SOHC 4-valve Blue Core engine producing 29.2 hp and 31.5 Nm of torque.

Q: Does the RW350 have an automatic or manual transmission?

The RW350 uses an advanced V-belt continuously variable transmission (CVT), providing smooth automatic twist-andgo operation.

Q: How much underseat storage does the RW350 have?

The scooter provides a cavernous 38-litre underseat storage bay capable of accommodating two full-face helmets.

Q: Will the Yamaha RW350 launch in India?

India Yamaha Motor has not officially confirmed an Indian launch, although a CKD introduction remains under evaluation for late 2027.

10. Conclusion

The Yamaha RW350 unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an exceptionally well-rounded middleweight adventure crossover. By pairing a punchy 29.2 hp 327cc engine with robust KYB suspension, authentic dual-sport styling, and generous underseat storage, Yamaha has crafted the ultimate all-terrain urban commuter. It promises to become an immediate favorite among European and international scooter enthusiasts.
