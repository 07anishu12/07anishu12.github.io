Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of TVS NTorq 125

Evaluating tvs ntorq 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the TVS NTorq 125 is certified at 48.5 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating.

Driven by a 124.8cc (Single-cylinder, 4-stroke, SI, air-cooled, 3-valve CVTi-REVV engine) motor producing 9.25 bhp @ 7,000 rpm (10.06 bhp on Race XP) and 10.5 Nm @ 5,500 rpm (10.8 Nm on Race XP) through a CVT Automatic transmission, the TVS NTorq 125 weighs 118 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes 3-valve CVTi-REVV engine, bass-rich exhaust note, 110/80-12 wide rear tyre, and Street/Race modes on Race XP. Across variants (Drum, Disc, Race Edition, Super Squad Edition, Race XP (Ride Modes), XT (TFT Voice Assist)), core engine mapping remains identical.

Speed-Wise Fuel Economy, Top Speed (95 km/h) and Gearing

Aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed, rapidly increasing fuel demand above 65 km/h. At 35–45 km/h in 4th or top gear, the TVS NTorq 125 operates under light load and returns 44–46 km/l unless traffic forces frequent downshifts.
