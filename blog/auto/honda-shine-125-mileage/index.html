Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Honda Shine 125

Evaluating honda shine 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Honda Shine 125 is certified at 60.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: premium sibling Honda SP 125 city vs highway mileage.

Driven by a 123.94cc (4-stroke, SI engine with eSP, ACG Silent Start, and PGM-FI) motor producing 10.59 bhp @ 7,500 rpm and 11.0 Nm @ 6,000 rpm through a 5-speed transmission, the Honda Shine 125 weighs 113 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes 113 kg low kerb weight for a 125cc bike, silent ACG starter motor, and 5-speed transmission. Across variants (Drum CBS, Disc CBS, Limited Edition), core engine mapping remains identical. Related reading: best 100-125cc bikes for daily commuting in India.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Honda Shine 125 to 54–58 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (113 kg low kerb weight for a 125cc bike, silent ACG starter motor, and 5-speed transmission) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 60–75 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 58–62 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 10.5L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Honda Shine 125 in 2026?

The Honda Shine 125 delivers 54–58 km/l in city traffic, 58–62 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 55–60 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Honda Shine 125?

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Honda Shine 125?

At 57 km/l, fuel cost is ₹1.79/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹1.95/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹1.84/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Honda Shine 125?

Does Honda Shine 125 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
