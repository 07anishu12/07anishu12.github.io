Bajaj Chetak vs Ather Rizta: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Bajaj Chetak and Ather Rizta requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Bajaj Chetak offers 2.9 kWh / 3.2 kWh / 3.5 kWh (35 Series) paired with a 4.0–4.2 kW PMSM swingarm-mounted motor (63 km/h (base) / 73 km/h (TecPac & 35 Series) top speed) and claims 123 km (2.9 kWh) / 137 km (3.2 kWh) / 153 km IDC (3.5 kWh). Related reading: Bajaj Chetak real-world distance per charge and battery efficiency.

The Ather Rizta provides 2.9 kWh / 3.7 kWh driving a 4.3 kW PMSM mid-drive motor (22 Nm torque) (80 km/h top speed) with a claimed range of 123 km IDC (2.9 kWh) / 159 km IDC (3.7 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Bajaj Chetak highlights Pressed steel unibody shell, IP67 water resistance, onboard charger option, and 35-litre boot on the 3.5 kWh 35 Series, offering 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery) of under-seat storage and a charging time of 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 3501 with 950W onboard charger) / ~4 hr 30 min (standard trims).

Meanwhile, the Ather Rizta counters with 900mm longest family seat in segment, 34-litre under-seat storage, SkidControl traction control, Magic Twist regen, and Zip/SmartEco ride modes, providing 34 litres under-seat + optional 22-litre Frunk (56L total) of storage and charging in 5 hr 45 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 350W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W Ather Duo charger).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (3.95 kWh wall draw for 3.5 kWh on Bajaj Chetak vs 4.15 kWh wall draw for 3.7 kWh on Ather Rizta), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Conditional Verdict: Which Electric Scooter Should You Choose?

Choose the Bajaj Chetak (₹99,998 – ₹1,35,000) if you prioritize its 90–98 km (2.9 kWh) / 100–110 km (3.2 kWh) / 110–125 km (3.5 kWh) Eco range, 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery) storage, and Pressed steel unibody shell, IP67 water resistance, onboard charger option, and 35-litre boot on the 3.5 kWh 35 Series. Choose the Ather Rizta (₹1,09,999 – ₹1,49,999) if your routine benefits more from its 95–100 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 115–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco) range, 80 km/h top speed, and 900mm longest family seat in segment, 34-litre under-seat storage, SkidControl traction control, Magic Twist regen, and Zip/SmartEco ride modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Bajaj Chetak or Ather Rizta?

In Eco mode, Bajaj Chetak delivers 90–98 km (2.9 kWh) / 100–110 km (3.2 kWh) / 110–125 km (3.5 kWh) (benchmark 115 km) while Ather Rizta delivers 95–100 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 115–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco) (benchmark 120 km), giving Ather Rizta the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Bajaj Chetak vs Ather Rizta?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Bajaj Chetak costs ₹0.24/km and Ather Rizta costs ₹0.24/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Bajaj Chetak charges in 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 3501 with 950W onboard charger) / ~4 hr 30 min (standard trims), whereas Ather Rizta takes 5 hr 45 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 350W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W Ather Duo charger).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Bajaj Chetak provides 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery) compared to 34 litres under-seat + optional 22-litre Frunk (56L total) on Ather Rizta.

Which has a higher top speed?

Bajaj Chetak reaches 63 km/h (base) / 73 km/h (TecPac & 35 Series) compared to 80 km/h for Ather Rizta.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Bajaj Chetak and Ather Rizta?

Bajaj Chetak costs ₹99,998 – ₹1,35,000 ex-showroom, while Ather Rizta costs ₹1,09,999 – ₹1,49,999.
