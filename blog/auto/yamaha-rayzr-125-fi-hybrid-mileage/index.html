Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid

Evaluating yamaha rayzr 125 fi hybrid mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid is certified at 71.33 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating.

Driven by a 125.0cc (Air-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 2-valve Blue Core FI with SMG (Smart Motor Generator) Hybrid Power Assist) motor producing 8.04 bhp @ 6,500 rpm and 10.3 Nm @ 5,000 rpm (with SMG hybrid assist) through a V-Belt Automatic CVT transmission, the Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid weighs 99 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes 99 kg featherweight chassis, Smart Motor Generator providing 3-second electric torque boost from standstill, and Quiet Engine Start + Stop & Start System. Across variants (Drum, Disc, Street Rally (with Brush Guards & Block-Pattern Tyres)), core engine mapping remains identical.
