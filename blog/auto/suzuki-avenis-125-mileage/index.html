Suzuki Avenis 125 vs TVS NTorq 125: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Suzuki Avenis 125 and TVS NTorq 125 requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Suzuki Avenis 125 pairs a 124.3cc engine (8.58 bhp @ 6,750 rpm, 10.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a 106 kg chassis and claims 55.0 km/l.

The TVS NTorq 125 uses a 124.8cc powertrain (9.25 bhp @ 7,000 rpm (10.06 bhp on Race XP), 10.5 Nm @ 5,500 rpm (10.8 Nm on Race XP)) weighing 118 kg with a claimed 48.5 km/l. Their 12 kg mass difference and gearing (CVT Automatic vs CVT Automatic) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Suzuki Avenis 125 features 12 kg lighter than TVS NTorq 125 (106 kg vs 118 kg), external hinge fuel cap, and split LED tail lamps, returning 47–51 km/l in urban use. The TVS NTorq 125 counters with 3-valve CVTi-REVV engine, bass-rich exhaust note, 110/80-12 wide rear tyre, and Street/Race modes on Race XP, achieving 40–44 km/l. Related reading: Suzuki Access 125 city and highway mileage review.

On open highways, the Suzuki Avenis 125 reaches 90 km/h and cruises cleanly at 50–65 km/h (50–54 km/l), whereas the TVS NTorq 125 tops out at 95 km/h and cruises comfortably at 55–70 km/h (44–48 km/l).
