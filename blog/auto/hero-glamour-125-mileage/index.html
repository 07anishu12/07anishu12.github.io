Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Hero Glamour 125

Evaluating hero glamour 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Hero Glamour 125 is certified at 63.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: Hero Passion Plus city and highway mileage breakdown. Related reading: why claimed mileage differs from real-world mileage on Indian roads.

Driven by a 124.7cc (Air-cooled, 4-stroke, single-cylinder with XSens FI and i3S) motor producing 10.72 bhp @ 7,500 rpm and 10.6 Nm @ 6,000 rpm through a 5-speed constant mesh transmission, the Hero Glamour 125 weighs 122 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Real-time mileage indicator (RTMI) on digital display, 5-speed transmission, and 180mm ground clearance. Across variants (Glamour Classic Drum, Glamour Classic Disc, Glamour XTEC / Glamour X 125), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Hero Glamour 125 to 55–59 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Real-time mileage indicator (RTMI) on digital display, 5-speed transmission, and 180mm ground clearance) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 10.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.
