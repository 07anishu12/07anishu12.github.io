At 47 km/l and an illustrative petrol price of ₹105/litre, its running cost is ₹2.23/km (₹2.17/km in Delhi). Related reading: naked sibling Yamaha MT-15 V2 mileage at different speeds.

Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Yamaha R15 V4

Evaluating yamaha r15 v4 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Yamaha R15 V4 is certified at 55.2 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: TVS Apache RTR 200 4V city and highway fuel efficiency. Related reading: city vs highway riding conditions and petrol consumption.

Driven by a 155.0cc (Liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 4-valve with Variable Valve Actuation (VVA)) motor producing 18.1 bhp @ 10,000 rpm and 14.2 Nm @ 7,500 rpm through a 6-speed with Assist & Slipper Clutch and Quickshifter (on select variants) transmission, the Yamaha R15 V4 weighs 141 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Aerodynamic R1-inspired full fairing reduces high-speed drag, VVA dual-cam lobe system, Track & Street modes, Traction Control, and 6th-gear overdrive highway efficiency. Across variants (Metallic Red, Dark Knight, Racing Blue (with Quickshifter), M (Color TFT), MotoGP Edition), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Yamaha R15 V4 to 44–48 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Aerodynamic R1-inspired full fairing reduces high-speed drag, VVA dual-cam lobe system, Track & Street modes, Traction Control, and 6th-gear overdrive highway efficiency) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 90–110 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 49–55 km/l. Related reading: semi-faired Bajaj Pulsar 220F mileage and top speed.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 11.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Yamaha R15 V4 in 2026?

The Yamaha R15 V4 delivers 44–48 km/l in city traffic, 49–55 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 45–51 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Yamaha R15 V4?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Yamaha R15 V4 is 55.2 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Yamaha R15 V4?

At 47 km/l, fuel cost is ₹2.17/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹2.37/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹2.23/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Yamaha R15 V4?

With its 11.0-litre tank and 45–51 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 495–561 km.

Cruising steadily at 90–110 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Yamaha R15 V4 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
