Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Honda Activa 6G

Evaluating honda activa 6g mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Honda Activa 6G is certified at 55.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: Honda Activa 125 claimed vs real-world mileage and Idling Stop benefits.

Driven by a 109.51cc (Fan-cooled, 4-stroke, SI engine with eSP and PGM-FI) motor producing 7.88 bhp @ 8,000 rpm and 8.90 Nm @ 5,500 rpm through a V-Matic CVT Automatic transmission, the Honda Activa 6G weighs 106 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes All-steel body panels, telescopic front fork, 12-inch front wheel, external double-lid fuel filler, and ACG silent start. Across variants (Standard, Deluxe, H-Smart (Keyless Smart Key)), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Honda Activa 6G to 45–49 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (All-steel body panels, telescopic front fork, 12-inch front wheel, external double-lid fuel filler, and ACG silent start) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 45–60 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 49–53 km/l. Related reading: lighter sibling Honda Dio actual fuel economy and top speed.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 5.3L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Honda Activa 6G in 2026?

The Honda Activa 6G delivers 45–49 km/l in city traffic, 49–53 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 46–50 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Honda Activa 6G?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Honda Activa 6G is 55.0 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Honda Activa 6G?

At 48 km/l, fuel cost is ₹2.13/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹2.32/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹2.19/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Honda Activa 6G?

With its 5.3-litre tank and 46–50 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 244–265 km.

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on Honda Activa 6G?

Cruising steadily at 45–60 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Honda Activa 6G mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
