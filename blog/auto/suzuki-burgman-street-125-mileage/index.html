Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Suzuki Burgman Street 125

Evaluating suzuki burgman street 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Suzuki Burgman Street 125 is certified at 55.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: Suzuki Access 125 city and highway mileage guide.

Driven by a 124.0cc (4-stroke, single-cylinder, air-cooled SEP / SEP-alpha (on EX variant)) motor producing 8.58 bhp @ 6,750 rpm (8.48 bhp on EX) and 10.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm through a CVT Automatic transmission, the Suzuki Burgman Street 125 weighs 110 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Maxi-scooter front apron with windscreen, stretched dual footboard positions, and SEP-alpha silent starter + auto stop-start on EX. Across variants (Standard, Ride Connect Edition, Burgman Street EX (12-inch Rear Wheel & EASS Auto Stop-Start)), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Suzuki Burgman Street 125 to 46–50 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Maxi-scooter front apron with windscreen, stretched dual footboard positions, and SEP-alpha silent starter + auto stop-start on EX) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 55–70 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 50–54 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 5.5L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.
