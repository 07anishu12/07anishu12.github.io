1. Introduction

Royal Enfield reaffirmed its passionate commitment to global custom motorcycle culture at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the stunning Royal Enfield Shotgun 650 'Son of a Gun' limited edition. Developed by Royal Enfield's bespoke Custom World division in collaboration with premier European custom builders, this factory custom motorcycle celebrates the modular, neo-retro roadster platform introduced on the Shotgun 650. Featuring a hand-finished, raw industrial livery, a single floating bobber saddle, bar-end mirrors, blackened twin peashooter exhausts, and bespoke machined aluminum accessories, the 'Son of a Gun' edition transforms the production roadster into a menacing piece of rideable street art, paying homage to the rebellious spirit of underground custom garages.

2. Launch and Availability

The Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun was officially unveiled on November 3, 2026, taking center stage at Royal Enfield's Custom World showcase in Hall 15 at EICMA in Milan. Royal Enfield confirmed that the Son of a Gun will be produced as an exclusive factory-custom limited edition, with production strictly capped at 500 individually numbered units worldwide. European and North American deliveries will commence in March 2027. For the Indian market, Royal Enfield has officially confirmed that an exclusive allocation of 150 units will be made available to domestic collectors via an online flash booking event in early 2027.

3. Engine and Performance

Beneath its bespoke custom exterior, the Son of a Gun is powered by Royal Enfield's acclaimed 648cc, airand-oil-cooled, parallel-twin engine featuring a 270-degree crankshaft, single overhead camshaft (SOHC), and four valves per cylinder. The engine produces a smooth, linear 47 bhp (34.6 kW) at 7,250 rpm and 52.3 Nm of peak torque at 5,650 rpm. The 270-degree firing order delivers a distinctive, rumbling exhaust cadence that is amplified through custom matte-black reverse-cone megaphone exhaust mufflers. Power is routed through a slick six-speed transmission equipped with an assist-and-slipper clutch that prevents rear-wheel chatter during aggressive downshifts.

4. Design and hardware

The visual allure of the Son of a Gun edition lies in its exquisite custom craftsmanship. The fuel tank and front nacelle feature a striking hand-applied brushed steel finish protected by a satin clear coat, contrasted by airbrushed faded-smoke gradients and vintage hand-lettered 'Son of a Gun' gold-leaf typography. The motorcycle embraces a pure bobber silhouette, featuring a solo floating leather bucket seat mounted on a cast aluminum cantilever subframe, exposing the muscular rear fender and 150-section tire. The front cockpit is aggressively stripped back, showcasing wide drag handlebars, round bar-end mirrors, and machined billet aluminum footpegs and control levers.

5. Features and technology

While exuding raw custom garage attitude, the motorcycle incorporates contemporary rider amenities. The instrument pod features Royal Enfield's unique digital-analog display paired alongside the secondary Tripper navigation pod, providing turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation prompts via Bluetooth smartphone pairing. Lighting is 100 percent LED, including a neo-retro circular projector headlight housed in a cast aluminum nacelle, mini bullet LED turn indicators, and an integrated rear LED brake lamp. A discreetly integrated USB charging port is located beneath the handlebar clamp.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the limited-edition Royal Enfield Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun is priced at €8,200, representing a modest premium over the standard Shotgun 650 (€7,590) given its extensive hand-finished paintwork and billet accessories. In India, where it will be built at the Vallam Vadagal plant, the ex-showroom price is expected to be approximately Rs 3.95 lakh to Rs 4.15 lakh. Royal Enfield positions the Son of a Gun as an authentic, collectible factory custom for riders who desire bespoke show-bike exclusivity with the peace of mind of a full factory warranty.

7. India launch possibility

8. Rivals and alternatives

The Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun competes against custom roadsters and modern bobbers including the Triumph Bonneville Bobber, the BSA Gold Star 650 Shadow, and the Harley-Davidson Nightster. While the Triumph Bonneville Bobber costs more than triple the price, the Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun delivers comparable custom garage attitude, twin-cylinder character, and authentic bobber proportions at an accessible price point.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many units of the Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun will be produced?

Production is strictly limited to 500 units globally, with 150 units exclusively reserved for the Indian domestic market.

Q: Can a pillion seat be installed on the Son of a Gun edition?

While configured from the factory as a pure solo bobber, the modular Shotgun subframe allows owners to bolt on an optional pillion seat and luggage rack.

Q: What engine powers the Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun?

It is powered by Royal Enfield's 648cc air/oil-cooled parallel-twin engine producing 47 bhp and 52.3 Nm of torque.

Q: What is the expected price in India?

The limited edition is expected to carry an ex-showroom price of approximately Rs 3.95 lakh to Rs 4.15 lakh in India.

10. Conclusion

The Royal Enfield Shotgun 650 Son of a Gun unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an inspiring triumph of factory custom design. By transforming the versatile Shotgun 650 into a hand-finished, raw bobber masterwork with 47 bhp of twin-cylinder soul, Royal Enfield has proved that custom culture and mass production can meet in perfect harmony. It is an instant collector's icon for motorcycle purists.
