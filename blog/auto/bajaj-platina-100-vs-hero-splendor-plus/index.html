Bajaj Platina 100 vs Hero Splendor Plus: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Bajaj Platina 100 and Hero Splendor Plus requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Bajaj Platina 100 pairs a 102.0cc engine (7.79 bhp @ 7,500 rpm, 8.34 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a 117 kg chassis and claims 72.0 km/l.

The Hero Splendor Plus uses a 97.2cc powertrain (7.91 bhp @ 8,000 rpm, 8.05 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) weighing 112 kg with a claimed 70.0 km/l. Their 5 kg mass difference and gearing (4-speed (all-down pattern) vs 4-speed constant mesh (all-up pattern)) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Bajaj Platina 100 features Comfortec Spring-in-Spring rear suspension, long quilted seat, and long-stroke 102cc engine peaking at 5,500 rpm, returning 64–68 km/l in urban use. The Hero Splendor Plus counters with i3S (Idle Stop-Start System) cuts the engine after 5 seconds of neutral idling in traffic and restarts via clutch pull, achieving 62–65 km/l.

On open highways, the Bajaj Platina 100 reaches 90 km/h and cruises cleanly at 50–65 km/h (67–70 km/l), whereas the Hero Splendor Plus tops out at 87 km/h and cruises comfortably at 50–60 km/h (65–68 km/l).

Conditional Verdict: Which Model Fits Your Commute?

Choose the Bajaj Platina 100 (₹68,685 – ₹71,050) if you value its 62–68 km/l real-world efficiency, 117 kg weight, and Comfortec Spring-in-Spring rear suspension, long quilted seat, and long-stroke 102cc engine peaking at 5,500 rpm. Choose the Hero Splendor Plus (₹76,306 – ₹82,911) if your riding prioritizes its 97.2cc performance character, 60–66 km/l fuel economy, and 50–60 km/h highway composure.
