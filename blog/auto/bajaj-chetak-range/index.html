Certified Claimed Range vs Real-World Distance per Charge of Bajaj Chetak

When researching bajaj chetak range, buyers must never use petrol units (km/l) and must separate Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) laboratory range from real-world on-road range across riding modes, nominal battery capacity (kWh) vs wall-meter energy consumed (kWh), and electricity cost per kilometre (₹/km). Under certified laboratory testing, the Bajaj Chetak is rated at 123 km (2.9 kWh) / 137 km (3.2 kWh) / 153 km IDC (3.5 kWh). In real Indian city traffic—with rider weight, flyover gradients, and regenerative braking—riders achieve 90–98 km (2.9 kWh) / 100–110 km (3.2 kWh) / 110–125 km (3.5 kWh) in economy mode and 75–82 km (2.9 kWh) / 85–95 km (3.2 kWh) / 95–105 km (3.5 kWh) in normal/sport modes.

Available across variants (Chetak 2901 / 2903 (2.9 kWh), Chetak 3202 / 3201 (3.2 kWh), Chetak 35 Series (3501 / 3502 / 3503 with 3.5 kWh floorboard battery)), the Bajaj Chetak combines a Fixed IP67 Lithium-ion battery (under-floor on 35 Series) with a 4.0–4.2 kW PMSM swingarm-mounted motor reaching a top speed of 63 km/h (base) / 73 km/h (TecPac & 35 Series). Key highlights include Pressed steel unibody shell, IP67 water resistance, onboard charger option, and 35-litre boot on the 3.5 kWh 35 Series and 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery) of under-seat storage.

Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 3.5 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 3.95 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 115 km per charge equals 3.04 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 3.43 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.

Charging time for the Bajaj Chetak is 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 3501 with 950W onboard charger) / ~4 hr 30 min (standard trims). Keeping the lithium-ion pack between 20% and 80% State of Charge (SoC) maximizes battery life, meaning a scooter with 115 km real range comfortably supports a 75 km daily round trip with a healthy reserve.

Factors That Reduce EV Range and Conditional Buyer Verdict

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world range of Bajaj Chetak in 2026?

In city riding, the Bajaj Chetak delivers 90–98 km (2.9 kWh) / 100–110 km (3.2 kWh) / 110–125 km (3.5 kWh) in Eco mode and 75–82 km (2.9 kWh) / 85–95 km (3.2 kWh) / 95–105 km (3.5 kWh) in Power/Sport modes.

What is the claimed IDC range of Bajaj Chetak?

The official claimed range of the Bajaj Chetak is 123 km (2.9 kWh) / 137 km (3.2 kWh) / 153 km IDC (3.5 kWh) depending on battery variant (2.9 kWh / 3.2 kWh / 3.5 kWh (35 Series)).

How much does it cost to fully charge Bajaj Chetak at home?

Charging the 3.5 kWh pack draws ~3.95 kWh from the wall, costing ₹21.73 in Delhi (₹5.50/kWh), ₹33.58 in Maharashtra (₹8.50/kWh), and ₹27.65 at ₹7.00/kWh.

What is the per-kilometre running cost of Bajaj Chetak?

At 115 km real range and 3.95 kWh wall draw, electricity cost is ₹0.19/km in Delhi and ₹0.24/km at ₹7.00/kWh.

How long does Bajaj Chetak take to charge?

Charging time is 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 3501 with 950W onboard charger) / ~4 hr 30 min (standard trims).

What is the top speed and boot space of Bajaj Chetak?

The Bajaj Chetak has a top speed of 63 km/h (base) / 73 km/h (TecPac & 35 Series) and under-seat storage of 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery).
