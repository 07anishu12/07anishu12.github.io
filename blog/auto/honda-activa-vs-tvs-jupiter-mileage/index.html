Quick Answer: Between the Honda Activa 6G and TVS Jupiter 110, the TVS Jupiter 110 delivers higher real-world fuel economy. In mixed Indian conditions, the Honda Activa 6G (109.51cc, 106 kg) returns 46–50 km/l (benchmark 48 km/l, or ₹2.19/km at ₹105/litre), while the TVS Jupiter 110 (113.3cc, 105 kg) returns 48–52 km/l (benchmark 50 km/l, or ₹2.10/km), saving ₹88 per 1,000 km on the TVS Jupiter 110. Honda Activa 6G vs TVS Jupiter 110: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Honda Activa 6G and TVS Jupiter 110 requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Honda Activa 6G pairs a 109.51cc engine (7.88 bhp @ 8,000 rpm, 8.90 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a 106 kg chassis and claims 55.0 km/l.

The TVS Jupiter 110 uses a 113.3cc powertrain (7.91 bhp @ 6,500 rpm, 9.2 Nm @ 5,000 rpm (9.8 Nm with iGO Assist)) weighing 105 kg with a claimed 53.8 km/l. Their 1 kg mass difference and gearing (V-Matic CVT Automatic vs CVT Automatic) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Honda Activa 6G features All-steel body panels, telescopic front fork, 12-inch front wheel, external double-lid fuel filler, and ACG silent start, returning 45–49 km/l in urban use. The TVS Jupiter 110 counters with New-generation 113.3cc engine with iGO Assist micro-hybrid torque boost, front apron fuel filler, 33-litre under-seat storage (fits two helmets), and 12-inch wheels, achieving 48–52 km/l. Related reading: Honda Activa 6G mileage in heavy traffic and city conditions. Related reading: TVS Jupiter 110 real mileage in city traffic and on open roads.

On open highways, the Honda Activa 6G reaches 85 km/h and cruises cleanly at 45–60 km/h (49–53 km/l), whereas the TVS Jupiter 110 tops out at 82 km/h and cruises comfortably at 45–60 km/h (51–55 km/l).

Conditional Verdict: Which Model Fits Your Commute?

Choose the Honda Activa 6G (₹76,358 – ₹91,482) if you value its 46–50 km/l real-world efficiency, 106 kg weight, and All-steel body panels, telescopic front fork, 12-inch front wheel, external double-lid fuel filler, and ACG silent start.
