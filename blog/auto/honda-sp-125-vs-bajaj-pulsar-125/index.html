Honda SP 125 vs Bajaj Pulsar 125: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Honda SP 125 and Bajaj Pulsar 125 requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Honda SP 125 pairs a 123.94cc engine (10.72 bhp @ 7,500 rpm, 10.9 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) with a 116 kg chassis and claims 63.0 km/l.

The Bajaj Pulsar 125 uses a 124.4cc powertrain (11.64 bhp @ 8,500 rpm, 10.8 Nm @ 6,500 rpm) weighing 140 kg with a claimed 51.46 km/l. Their 24 kg mass difference and gearing (5-speed vs 5-speed) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Honda SP 125 features Full digital LCD with real-time mileage, average fuel economy, distance-to-empty, gear position indicator, and LED headlamp, returning 56–60 km/l in urban use. The Bajaj Pulsar 125 counters with Classic wolf-eyed Pulsar styling with counter-balancer shaft and 140 kg planted highway stability, achieving 47–51 km/l. On open highways, the Honda SP 125 reaches 100 km/h and cruises cleanly at 60–75 km/h (60–64 km/l), whereas the Bajaj Pulsar 125 tops out at 105 km/h and cruises comfortably at 65–80 km/h (51–55 km/l). Related reading: best 100-125cc bikes for daily commuting in India.
