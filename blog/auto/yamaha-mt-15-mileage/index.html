Peak efficiency occurs at a steady 85–100 km/h in top gear, where fuel cost averages ₹2.19/km at an illustrative ₹105/litre petrol price.

Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Yamaha MT-15 V2

Evaluating yamaha mt-15 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Yamaha MT-15 V2 is certified at 56.87 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Driven by a 155.0cc (Liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 4-valve with Variable Valve Actuation (VVA)) motor producing 18.1 bhp @ 10,000 rpm and 14.1 Nm @ 7,500 rpm through a 6-speed with Assist and Slipper Clutch transmission, the Yamaha MT-15 V2 weighs 141 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes VVA switches cam profiles at 7,400 rpm (preserving low-rpm fuel economy while delivering 18.1 bhp top end), Deltabox frame, aluminium swingarm, 37mm USD forks, and 6-speed gearbox. Across variants (Standard, Deluxe (Bluetooth Y-Connect & LED Turn Indicators), MotoGP Edition), core engine mapping remains identical. Speed-Wise Fuel Economy, Top Speed (130 km/h) and Gearing

Aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed, rapidly increasing fuel demand above 65 km/h. At 35–45 km/h in 4th or top gear, the Yamaha MT-15 V2 operates under light load and returns 50–52 km/l unless traffic forces frequent downshifts.

Its peak thermal efficiency window is 85–100 km/h in top gear, delivering 52–54 km/l on open roads. Pushing toward its 130 km/h flat-road top speed triggers rich fuel mapping, dropping economy to 45–47 km/l at 80 km/h and 39–41 km/l near full throttle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Yamaha MT-15 V2 in 2026?

The Yamaha MT-15 V2 delivers 45–49 km/l in city traffic, 49–54 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 46–51 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Yamaha MT-15 V2?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Yamaha MT-15 V2 is 56.87 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Yamaha MT-15 V2?

At 48 km/l, fuel cost is ₹2.13/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹2.32/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹2.19/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Yamaha MT-15 V2?

With its 10.0-litre tank and 46–51 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 460–510 km.

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on Yamaha MT-15 V2?

Cruising steadily at 85–100 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Yamaha MT-15 V2 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
