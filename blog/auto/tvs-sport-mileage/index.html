Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of TVS Sport

Evaluating tvs sport mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the TVS Sport is certified at 70.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Driven by a 109.7cc (Single-cylinder, 4-stroke, fuel injection, air-cooled, spark ignition ET-Fi (Eco Thrust Fuel Injection) engine) motor producing 8.18 bhp @ 7,350 rpm and 8.7 Nm @ 4,500 rpm through a 4-speed constant mesh transmission, the TVS Sport weighs 112 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Duralife 109.7cc engine with ET-Fi technology, Econometer (Eco & Power LED indicators), and 8.7 Nm torque at just 4,500 rpm. Across variants (ES (Electric Start), ELS (Electric Start Long Seat)), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the TVS Sport to 63–67 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Duralife 109.7cc engine with ET-Fi technology, Econometer (Eco & Power LED indicators), and 8.7 Nm torque at just 4,500 rpm) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 50–65 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 67–70 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 10.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of TVS Sport in 2026?

The TVS Sport delivers 63–67 km/l in city traffic, 67–70 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 62–68 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of TVS Sport?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the TVS Sport is 70.0 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for TVS Sport?

At 65 km/l, fuel cost is ₹1.57/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹1.71/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹1.62/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of TVS Sport?

With its 10.0-litre tank and 62–68 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 620–680 km.

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on TVS Sport?

Cruising steadily at 50–65 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does TVS Sport mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
