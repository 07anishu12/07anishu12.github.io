Hero Vida VX2 vs TVS iQube: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Hero Vida VX2 and TVS iQube requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Hero Vida VX2 offers 2.2 kWh (VX2 Go single pack) / 3.44–4.4 kWh (VX2 Plus dual removable packs) paired with a 6.0 kW peak PMSM swingarm-mounted motor (25 Nm torque) (70 km/h (Go) / 80–85 km/h (Plus) top speed) and claims 94 km IDC (2.2 kWh) / 142–187 km IDC (3.44–4.4 kWh dual pack).

The TVS iQube provides 2.2 kWh (Base) / 3.4 kWh (Standard & S) driving a 4.4 kW BLDC hub-mounted motor (140 Nm wheel torque) (75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) top speed) with a claimed range of 75 km (2.2 kWh) / 100–114 km IDC (3.4 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Hero Vida VX2 highlights Lift-out removable battery packs allowing apartment dwellers without basement sockets to charge indoors at any 5A plug, plus Ather-compatible fast-charging grid access, offering 26 litres of under-seat storage and a charging time of 3 hr 30 min (2.2 kWh) / 5 hr 15 min – 5 hr 55 min (dual-pack 0–80% with 700W portable charger).

Meanwhile, the TVS iQube counters with Silent hub motor with zero belt maintenance, 32-litre under-seat storage on 3.4 kWh, and broad pan-India TVS dealership service support, providing 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) of storage and charging in 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (3.90 kWh wall draw for 3.44 kWh on Hero Vida VX2 vs 3.85 kWh wall draw for 3.4 kWh on TVS iQube), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Conditional Verdict: Which Electric Scooter Should You Choose?

Choose the Hero Vida VX2 (₹99,490 – ₹1,39,990) if you prioritize its 65–72 km (2.2 kWh) / 105–135 km (dual-pack in Eco mode) Eco range, 26 litres storage, and Lift-out removable battery packs allowing apartment dwellers without basement sockets to charge indoors at any 5A plug, plus Ather-compatible fast-charging grid access. Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Hero Vida VX2 or TVS iQube?

In Eco mode, Hero Vida VX2 delivers 65–72 km (2.2 kWh) / 105–135 km (dual-pack in Eco mode) (benchmark 105 km) while TVS iQube delivers 65 km (2.2 kWh) / 90–100 km (3.4 kWh) (benchmark 90 km), giving Hero Vida VX2 the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Hero Vida VX2 vs TVS iQube?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Hero Vida VX2 costs ₹0.26/km and TVS iQube costs ₹0.30/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Hero Vida VX2 charges in 3 hr 30 min (2.2 kWh) / 5 hr 15 min – 5 hr 55 min (dual-pack 0–80% with 700W portable charger), whereas TVS iQube takes 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Hero Vida VX2 provides 26 litres compared to 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) on TVS iQube.

Which has a higher top speed?

Hero Vida VX2 reaches 70 km/h (Go) / 80–85 km/h (Plus) compared to 75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) for TVS iQube.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Hero Vida VX2 and TVS iQube?

Hero Vida VX2 costs ₹99,490 – ₹1,39,990 ex-showroom, while TVS iQube costs ₹94,999 – ₹1,29,444.
