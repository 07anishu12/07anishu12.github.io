Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of TVS Radeon

Evaluating tvs radeon mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the TVS Radeon is certified at 68.6 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating.

Driven by a 109.7cc (4-stroke Duralife engine with ET-Fi and optional IntelliGO stop-start) motor producing 8.08 bhp @ 7,350 rpm and 8.7 Nm @ 4,500 rpm through a 4-speed constant mesh transmission, the TVS Radeon weighs 113 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes 180mm high ground clearance, heavy-duty metal grab rail with luggage carrier, reverse LCD display, and Synchronized Braking Technology (SBT). Across variants (Base Edition, Digi Cluster Edition Dual Tone (Drum & Disc) with IntelliGO), core engine mapping remains identical.
