Royal Enfield Classic 350 vs Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350: Engine Specs, Weight & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Evaluating Royal Enfield Classic 350 against Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350 requires separating official ARAI claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Royal Enfield Classic 350 uses a 349.34cc engine (20.2 bhp @ 6,100 rpm, 27.0 Nm @ 4,000 rpm) in a 195 kg chassis with a claimed 41.55 km/l.

The Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350 features a 348.36cc powertrain (20.78 bhp @ 5,500 rpm, 30.0 Nm @ 3,000 rpm) weighing 181 kg and claiming 42.17 km/l. Their 14 kg weight difference and gearing (5-speed vs 5-speed with Assist and Slipper Clutch) drive their real-world efficiency gap.

City Traffic Tractability, Highway Touring Range and Performance

In urban traffic, the Royal Enfield Classic 350 leverages Counter-balanced J-series long-stroke engine, 19-inch front / 18-inch rear wheel stability, twin downtube spine frame, and relaxed 4,000 rpm torque peak to return 31–35 km/l, while the Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350 uses 30 Nm peak torque at just 3,000 rpm, tall gearing for low-rpm highway cruising, slip-and-assist clutch, and traction control (HSTC) to achieve 34–38 km/l. On open highways, the Royal Enfield Classic 350 reaches 114 km/h and cruises best at 80–95 km/h (36–39 km/l, ~442 km tank range), whereas the Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350 reaches 125 km/h and cruises cleanly at 85–100 km/h (38–42 km/l, ~555 km tank range).

Conditional Verdict: Which Motorcycle Suits You Best?

Choose the Royal Enfield Classic 350 (₹1,87,434 – ₹2,30,000) if you prefer its 349.34cc torque delivery, 13.0L tank range, and Counter-balanced J-series long-stroke engine, 19-inch front / 18-inch rear wheel stability, twin downtube spine frame, and relaxed 4,000 rpm torque peak. Choose the Honda CB350 / H'ness CB350 (₹1,93,000 – ₹2,16,000) if your riding benefits more from its 181 kg weight, 35–40 km/l real-world efficiency, and 30 Nm peak torque at just 3,000 rpm, tall gearing for low-rpm highway cruising, slip-and-assist clutch, and traction control (HSTC).
