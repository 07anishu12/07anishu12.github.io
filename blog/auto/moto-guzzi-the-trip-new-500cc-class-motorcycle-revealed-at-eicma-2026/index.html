1. Introduction

Historic Italian manufacturer Moto Guzzi has stunned the retro-modern motorcycling community at EICMA 2026 by unveiling the all-new Moto Guzzi The Trip. Representing Mandello del Lario's first venture into the accessible 500cc middleweight category in decades, The Trip introduces an authentic, newly developed transverse 90-degree V-twin engine engineered specifically for global A2 licensing and urban lifestyle riding. Proudly retaining Moto Guzzi's signature engineering hallmark—a longitudinally mounted transverse V-twin with low-maintenance shaft drive—The Trip combines 105 years of Italian heritage with contemporary electronic amenities, offering riders an accessible, characterful modern-classic roadster that stands completely apart from the generic parallel-twin competition.

2. Launch and Availability

Moto Guzzi The Trip celebrated its global unveiling on November 3, 2026, at the Piaggio Group pavilion during EICMA in Milan. Moto Guzzi announced that commercial production will take place at the historic Mandello del Lario factory on the shores of Lake Como, following a major multi-million euro factory modernization. European showroom deliveries will commence in May 2027. Regarding the Indian market, Piaggio Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. (PVPL) is actively evaluating introducing The Trip through its premium Motoplex dealership chain in late 2027 to capture the rapidly expanding luxury modern-classic segment.

3. Engine and Performance

At the mechanical heart of The Trip is an all-new 498cc, air-and-oil-cooled, transverse 90-degree V-twin engine with two valves per cylinder operated by pushrods and rockers, preserving traditional Mandello mechanical charm. Despite its heritage valvetrain, the engine features modern electronic fuel injection, twin spark plugs, and ride-by-wire throttle control to easily meet Euro 5+ emissions. The powerplant develops an exact 47.6 hp (35 kW) at 7,200 rpm and 46 Nm of peak torque at 5,100 rpm, matching the A2 licensing threshold without needing electronic restrictors. Power is transferred to the rear wheel via a six-speed gearbox and Moto Guzzi's signature enclosed cardan shaft drive, completely eliminating messy chain maintenance.

4. Design and hardware

The design of The Trip is an evocative tribute to classic 1970s Italian roadsters. It features a sculpted steel fuel tank with deep knee scallops and iconic polished Moto Guzzi eagle badges, flowing into a flat, ribbed leather bench seat. The signature cylinder heads protrude proudly on either side below the tank, giving the bike unmistakable presence. The chassis is a newly designed tubular steel double-cradle frame riding on 18inch front and 17-inch rear wire-spoked wheels shod in classic-tread Pirelli Phantom Sportscomp tires. An accessible 780mm seat height and wide, swept-back handlebars ensure a relaxed, upright riding posture that inspires confidence in city traffic.

5. Features and technology

While honoring heritage styling, The Trip incorporates modern rider conveniences. A clean circular 3.5-inch colour TFT display is housed within a vintage chrome bezel, providing digital speed, gear position, fuel level, and smartphone connectivity via the Moto Guzzi MIA multimedia platform. Electronic safety features include dual-channel ABS developed by Continental and 2-level Moto Guzzi Traction Control (MGCT) that can be switched off for gravel paths. Lighting is 100 percent LED, featuring an exquisite Mandello eagle silhouette integrated into the circular daytime running light.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, Moto Guzzi The Trip is projected to retail at an attractive introductory price of approximately €6,900 to €7,400, directly challenging the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 and Triumph Speed 400. In India, if introduced via the CKD assembly route through Piaggio's Baramati facility, the estimated exshowroom price would sit between Rs 4.50 lakh and Rs 5.20 lakh. Moto Guzzi will position The Trip as an authentic, boutique Italian alternative to mass-market retro motorcycles, highlighting its unique shaft drive and transverse V-twin character.

7. India launch possibility

8. Rivals and alternatives

Moto Guzzi The Trip competes directly against the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650, the Triumph Speed 400, the BSA Gold Star 650, and the Kawasaki W800. The Trip stands completely apart from all rivals by being the only motorcycle in the sub-500cc category to offer an authentic transverse 90-degree V-twin engine and a premium, maintenance-free shaft final drive system.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What engine is fitted to Moto Guzzi The Trip?

It is powered by an all-new 498cc, air-and-oil-cooled, transverse 90-degree V-twin producing 47.6 hp and 46 Nm of torque.

Q: Does Moto Guzzi The Trip use a chain or shaft drive?

True to Moto Guzzi heritage, The Trip utilizes a clean, maintenance-free enclosed cardan shaft final drive.

Q: Can The Trip be ridden on an A2 motorcycle licence?

Yes, producing an exact 35 kW (47.6 hp), it perfectly complies with European A2 licensing limits without modification.

Q: Will Moto Guzzi The Trip launch in India?

Piaggio India is evaluating the model for Motoplex dealerships, with an unconfirmed launch possibility in late 2027.

10. Conclusion

The Moto Guzzi The Trip unveiled at EICMA 2026 is a breathtaking celebration of authentic Italian motorcycling heritage. By packaging Mandello's legendary transverse V-twin architecture and shaft drive into an accessible, 47.6 hp modern-classic roadster, Moto Guzzi has created a machine overflowing with soul, character, and visual charm. It is destined to become an instant modern classic.
