Finding a dependable motorcycle under ₹1,00,000 ex-showroom in India requires looking past headline brochure figures to evaluate real-world city fuel economy, pillion comfort on broken roads, and actual on-road dealer quotes. In 2026, the sub-₹1 lakh segment has evolved beyond bare-bones 100cc commuters into feature-packed 125cc machines equipped with digital consoles, silent ACG starters, and even single-channel ABS. This comparative evaluation examines the top five contenders in Delhi-NCR and major metro markets to help you pick the right balance of running costs, safety, and everyday reliability.

Quick Answer & At-a-Glance Buying Verdict

For Indian buyers evaluating motorcycles under ₹1 Lakh (verified October 2026), the best all-round pick is the Honda SP 125 (TFT RoadSync Edition) (STD Drum: ₹90,498 – ₹91,771 ex-showroom Delhi; ₹1,05,400 – ₹1,15,800 on-road), returning 56–60 km/l in daily traffic. Riders prioritising sporty commuter aesthetics should choose the TVS Raider 125, while the Hero Xtreme 125R uniquely brings Single-Channel ABS safety into this price bracket. Always compare final on-road invoices (including state RTO and mandatory 5-year insurance) rather than base ex-showroom quotes.

Detailed Model-by-Model Evaluation & Official Links

1. Honda SP 125 (TFT RoadSync Edition)

Built by Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (125cc Premium Commuter Bike), the Honda SP 125 (TFT RoadSync Edition) pairs a refined 123.94 cc eSP engine (10.87 PS @ 7,500 rpm / 10.9 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) with verified Delhi pricing of STD Drum: ₹90,498 – ₹91,771 and Disc: ₹95,000. It features an integrated TFT screen with smartphone connectivity and silent ACG starting. Official Link: Honda SP 125 (TFT RoadSync Edition) Official Page

2. TVS Raider 125

Built by TVS Motor Company (125cc Sporty Commuter Bike), the TVS Raider 125 pairs a 124.8 cc engine (11.38 PS @ 7,500 rpm / 11.2 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) with verified Delhi pricing starting at Drum: ₹84,869 to Single Seat Disc: ₹95,219. Its monoshock rear suspension and energetic throttle response make it the top choice for young commuters. Official Link: TVS Raider 125 Official Page

3. Hero Xtreme 125R

Built by Hero MotoCorp, the Hero Xtreme 125R brings sharp streetfighter aesthetics and safety to the budget category, pairing a 124.7 cc Sprint-EBT engine (11.55 PS @ 8,250 rpm / 10.5 Nm @ 6,500 rpm) with verified Delhi pricing of IBS Variant: ₹95,000 and Single-Channel ABS Variant: ₹99,500 ex-showroom (₹1,10,500 – ₹1,15,900 on-road). It is the only motorcycle in this price band to feature front ABS. Official Link: Hero Xtreme 125R Official Page

4. Bajaj Pulsar N125

Built by Bajaj Auto, the Bajaj Pulsar N125 pairs a lightweight 124.38 cc engine (12.0 PS @ 8,500 rpm / 11.0 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) with verified Delhi pricing of LED Disc: ₹92,270 – ₹94,707 and Bluetooth Disc: ₹96,707 – ₹98,707. At just 125 kg, its power-to-weight ratio provides swift urban acceleration. Official Link: Bajaj Pulsar N125 Official Page

5. Honda Shine 125 (OBD2B Digital Edition)

Built by Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India, the Honda Shine 125 pairs a proven 123.94 cc engine (10.78 PS @ 7,500 rpm / 11 Nm @ 6,000 rpm) with verified Delhi pricing of Drum: ₹84,493 and Disc: ₹88,081. Known for long-term engine durability and high resale value, it is the benchmark for sensible family commuting. Official Link: Honda Shine 125 (OBD2B Digital Edition) Official Page

Buyer-Specific Recommendations & Use-Case Verdict

Match your riding profile to the right motorcycle:

  1. Daily Office Commuters (25–50 km/day): Pick the Honda SP 125 or TVS Raider 125 for 56–60 km/l real-world efficiency, light clutch action, and low maintenance.
  2. Family & Pillion Riders: Choose the Honda Shine 125 or TVS Raider 125 (Single-seat variant) for flat two-up seating comfort and plush rear suspension over broken roads.
  3. Safety-Focused Riders: Choose the Hero Xtreme 125R (Single-Channel ABS) for superior wet-weather stopping distance without front-wheel lockup.
  4. College & Young Riders: Choose the Bajaj Pulsar N125 or TVS Raider 125 for bold naked-street styling and smartphone connectivity.

Ownership Cost, City On-Road Variations & Financing

On-road prices vary across Indian states due to road taxes ranging from 4%–8% in Delhi to 12%–20% in Karnataka and Maharashtra. Over a typical 1,000 km monthly commute with petrol at ₹95/L, a 58 km/l 125cc motorcycle costs approximately ₹1,630/month in fuel. Annual scheduled servicing at authorized centers averages ₹1,200 to ₹1,800.

Pre-Purchase Showroom Checklist & Frequently Asked Questions

Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Checklist:

  1. Check the 17-digit VIN on the steering headstock in daylight to confirm a fresh 2026 manufacturing month.
  2. Demand an itemized proforma invoice separating ex-showroom price, state road tax, and mandatory insurance. Reject unauthorized dealer handling or logistics charges.
  3. Verify that the fuel tank and documentation confirm OBD2B and E20 ethanol compliance.

Frequently Asked Questions:

  • Q1: Which bike under ₹1 lakh has the best mileage?
    The Honda SP 125 and Honda Shine 125 consistently return 56–60 km/l in mixed city conditions when ridden moderately between 40–55 km/h.
  • Q2: Is ABS available under ₹1 lakh ex-showroom?
    Yes, the Hero Xtreme 125R Single-Channel ABS variant is priced at ₹99,500 ex-showroom Delhi, making it the most affordable ABS motorcycle in India.

Summary

Choosing the best bike under ₹1 Lakh comes down to balancing fuel economy, safety hardware, and dealer service density. For maximum reliability, low upkeep, and modern features, the Honda SP 125 remains the benchmark commuter. If you want sportier dynamics, pick the TVS Raider 125, and if front ABS braking safety is your priority, select the Hero Xtreme 125R.
