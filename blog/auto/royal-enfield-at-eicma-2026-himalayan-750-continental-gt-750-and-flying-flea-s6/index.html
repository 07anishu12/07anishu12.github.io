1. Introduction

Royal Enfield commanded the international spotlight at EICMA 2026 in Milan, orchestrating the most monumental and technologically diverse exhibition in its storied 125-year history. Celebrating a century and a quarter of uninterrupted pure motorcycling, the iconic Indian-owned British marque unveiled a breathtaking double-pronged vision of its future: expanding its revered internal combustion heritage into the heavy middleweight multi-cylinder arena with the all-new Himalayan 750 and Continental GT 750, while simultaneously charging into the electric frontier with the production-ready Flying Flea S6 electric scrambler. Together with the custom-crafted Shotgun 650 'Son of a Gun' factory special, Royal Enfield proved that it can seamlessly honor its timeless heritage while pioneering cutting-edge middleweight performance and sustainable urban mobility.

2. Launch and Availability

Royal Enfield's massive anniversary showcase took center stage in Hall 15 at Fiera Milano on November 3, 2026, attended by global dealers, international media, and brand purists. Managing Director Siddhartha Lal and CEO B. Govindarajan detailed the company's global rollout schedule. The liquid-cooled 750cc twincylinder platform will enter commercial production at the Vallam Vadagal facility in Tamil Nadu in mid-2027, with European and Indian deliveries scheduled for the third quarter of 2027. The electric Flying Flea S6 will follow in summer 2027, fulfilling European and domestic urban zero-emission mandates.

3. Engine and Performance

The internal combustion highlight of Royal Enfield's pavilion was the all-new 750cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke parallel-twin engine featuring a 270-degree crankshaft and four valves per cylinder driven by dual overhead camshafts. Tuned across two distinct states, the engine produces 58 bhp and 65 Nm of torque in the expedition-ready Himalayan 750, and a higher-revving 62 bhp and 68 Nm in the sporty Continental GT 750. On the electric side, the Flying Flea S6 features an in-house developed permanent magnet electric motor powered by a magnesium-encased lithium-ion battery pack, delivering 90 km/h top speed and 110 km of realworld urban range.

4. Design and hardware

Royal Enfield's design renaissance was evident across every model on display. The Himalayan 750 projects an authentic overland rally-raid aesthetic with a twin-spar steel frame developed alongside Harris Performance, Showa suspension delivering 200mm of travel, and tubeless cross-spoked wheels. The Continental GT 750 adopts an athletic café racer crouch with a sculpted fuel tank, rearset pegs, clip-on bars, and twin front brake discs clamped by radial calipers. Meanwhile, the electric Flying Flea S6 captured design awards with its retrofuturistic forged aluminum girder front suspension, ribbed cooling fins on the magnesium battery casing, and minimalist scrambler bench seat.

5. Features and technology

Technology across the Royal Enfield lineup reached unprecedented sophistication. The 750cc twins introduced a new circular 5.0-inch TFT TripperDash instrument console powered by Google Maps engine, offering full-screen turn-by-turn navigation, ride analytics, and music telemetry. A six-axis IMU governs Cornering ABS, Lean-Sensitive Traction Control, and selectable ride modes. On the Flying Flea S6, Royal Enfield showcased its custom-developed Vehicle Control Unit (VCU) with over-the-air (OTA) updates, predictive maintenance diagnostics, and smartphone-enabled digital keyless ignition.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, Royal Enfield will position the Himalayan 750 between €8,500 and €9,200, and the Continental GT 750 between €7,800 and €8,400, substantially undercutting established Japanese and European middleweights. In India, deep domestic localization ensures aggressive pricing: the Himalayan 750 is estimated between Rs 4.20 lakh and Rs 4.60 lakh, while the Continental GT 750 is projected between Rs 3.80 lakh and Rs 4.20 lakh ex-showroom. The Flying Flea S6 electric scrambler is estimated around Rs 1.80 lakh to Rs 2.10 lakh, positioning it as an aspirational, design-led urban EV.

7. India launch possibility

Confirmed for India. India is Royal Enfield's home market and the engine of its global

growth. Company leadership confirmed that the Himalayan 750 and Continental GT 750 will spearhead the brand's domestic premium expansion during the 2027 festive season, followed by the domestic rollout of the Flying Flea electric brand through specialized boutique lifestyle experience stores.

8. Rivals and alternatives

Royal Enfield's new portfolio takes direct aim at global market leaders. The Himalayan 750 rivals the Yamaha Ténéré 700 and Aprilia Tuareg 660. The Continental GT 750 challenges the Kawasaki Z650RS and Triumph Speed 400. The Flying Flea S6 competes against urban electric scramblers such as the Maeving RM1S and Super Soco TC Max.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What was the biggest motorcycle reveal for Royal Enfield at EICMA 2026?

The headline reveals were the Himalayan 750 adventure tourer and the Continental GT 750 café racer, both powered by a new 750cc liquid-cooled twin.

Q: What is the Flying Flea brand?

Flying Flea is Royal Enfield's dedicated all-electric urban motorcycle brand, inspired by the lightweight wartime motorcycle of the 1940s.

Q: When will the Himalayan 750 launch in India?

Royal Enfield has officially scheduled the domestic launch of the Himalayan 750 for mid-to-late 2027.

Q: Does the Continental GT 750 feature twin front disc brakes?

Yes, the Continental GT 750 comes equipped with dual 300mm front disc brakes and radial calipers to handle its 62 bhp output.

10. Conclusion

Royal Enfield's triumphant showcase at EICMA 2026 was a masterclass in modern motorcycle brand stewardship. By celebrating 125 years of heritage with the mighty 750cc liquid-cooled twin platform while boldly embracing the zero-emission future with the Flying Flea S6, Royal Enfield demonstrated that it possesses the vision, engineering excellence, and cultural authenticity to lead the global middleweight motorcycling revolution.
