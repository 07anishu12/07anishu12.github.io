Tata Nexon vs Hyundai Venue: Choosing the Right Variant

Compare Nexon and Venue variants, safety evidence and ownership costs before buying.

Shortlist Nexon iCNG when factory CNG and a manual gearbox meet your needs. For petrol or diesel, compare the exact transmissions and complete quotes: Nexon and Venue have equal documented overall length. Neither brand establishes a universal mileage, safety or ownership-cost winner, and current prices need local verification.

Match the engine and transmission first

The Tata Nexon specifications list turbo-petrol, diesel and iCNG families. The checked Hyundai catalogue lists Venue petrol and diesel. A factory-CNG shopper should therefore begin with the Nexon iCNG configuration and verify the current Venue offering, rather than comparing two petrol demonstrators.

Tata documents petrol manual, AMT and seven-speed dual-clutch choices, diesel manual/AMT, and a six-speed manual for iCNG. These are different mechanisms, not one generic “automatic”. Match the Venue's actual quoted gearbox and test traffic creep, hill starts and parking before selecting an equivalent trim.

Write fuel, engine, gearbox, trim and production year on both quotations. A Nexon CNG manual and a Venue petrol automatic solve different needs even if their prices overlap. Do not infer a real-world consumption or maintenance advantage from the transmission label alone.

Make the family test specific

The Venue page and Tata's specification page supply useful physical differences, checked on 22 October 2026:

Manufacturer dimension Nexon Venue
Length 3,995 mm 3,995 mm
Width 1,804 mm 1,800 mm
Height 1,620 mm 1,665 mm

The equal length means the Venue does not need a longer parking bay on this measure; the small width difference is unlikely to decide a purchase by itself. Height is not headroom: seat position and roof trim still require a passenger check.

Tata quotes 382 litres of boot space for petrol/diesel and 321 litres for iCNG. Do not transfer the larger figure to the CNG car. Compare loading openings and your actual bags, and obtain the Venue's corresponding capacity and measurement convention before declaring a luggage winner.

Read safety evidence correctly

Use the Bharat NCAP website to locate the relevant assessment, if available. Match the tested model, report date and applicable variants. Do not transfer a score from an older generation or compare stars across different protocols as though they were identical measurements.

Alongside crash evidence, compare stability control, airbags, rear-seat restraints, parking visibility and tyre condition on the exact cars. A camera can make parking easier, but it does not establish crash protection. Driver-assistance features need their own operating-limit explanation and must not justify distracted driving.

Calculate what the premium buys

Consider a wholly hypothetical comparison: one suitable SUV costs ₹80,000 more and saves ₹0.80 per kilometre in fuel. Simple fuel-only recovery is ₹80,000 ÷ ₹0.80 = 100,000 km. At 10,000 km annually, that is ten years before interest, servicing, insurance or resale differences.

These figures are illustrative, not Nexon or Venue results. Replace them with your city-specific quotes and a conservative fuel budget. Fuel cost per kilometre equals local fuel price divided by kilometres per litre; do not use a manufacturer's claimed number as guaranteed urban mileage.

Request the same insurance cover and deductible when comparing quotes. Keep accessories and extended warranty separate so that one dealer's bundled package does not appear to be a mechanical advantage. Ask both workshops for scheduled-service estimates and common wear-item prices.

Make a conditional choice

Shortlist Nexon iCNG when factory CNG is essential and its manual gearbox suits you. For petrol or diesel, the equal overall length makes drivetrain behaviour, passenger fit and the complete quote more useful tie-breakers than size labels. Consider Venue when its specific gearbox and demonstrated seating fit your routine better; no measured reliability winner is established here.

Before paying, record the promised variant, manufacturing month, delivery timeline and refund terms. Inspect the allotted vehicle and confirm both keys, documents and equipment. A well-matched middle variant can be a better household purchase than a more expensive trim whose features you rarely use.

Quick questions, clear answers

Which has better real-world mileage?

No controlled Nexon-versus-Venue mileage test was performed for this guide. Engine, gearbox, traffic, trip length and driving style all matter. Use comparable independent tests as context and budget conservatively; a short dealer demonstration is not an accurate consumption measurement.

Should I pay more for diesel?

Calculate the extra purchase and financing cost against your expected fuel savings and ownership period. Also consider your journey pattern and the manufacturer's operating guidance. A lower pump cost per kilometre alone does not prove that diesel is the cheaper overall choice.

Can I compare entry-level advertised prices?

They help locate the broad budget segment, but they do not establish equivalent value. Compare versions with your required gearbox and safety equipment, then request itemised on-road quotations in the same city with consistent insurance and optional extras.
