Ather Rizta vs TVS iQube: Battery Capacity, Motor Specs and Certified vs Real Range

Comparing the Ather Rizta and TVS iQube requires separating IDC laboratory claims from verified on-road distance per charge across Eco and Power/Sport modes. The Ather Rizta offers 2.9 kWh / 3.7 kWh paired with a 4.3 kW PMSM mid-drive motor (22 Nm torque) (80 km/h top speed) and claims 123 km IDC (2.9 kWh) / 159 km IDC (3.7 kWh).

The TVS iQube provides 2.2 kWh (Base) / 3.4 kWh (Standard & S) driving a 4.4 kW BLDC hub-mounted motor (140 Nm wheel torque) (75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) top speed) with a claimed range of 75 km (2.2 kWh) / 100–114 km IDC (3.4 kWh). Usable capacity and motor architecture shape real urban range.

Charging Convenience, Boot Storage and Daily Family Usability

Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Ather Rizta highlights 900mm longest family seat in segment, 34-litre under-seat storage, SkidControl traction control, Magic Twist regen, and Zip/SmartEco ride modes, offering 34 litres under-seat + optional 22-litre Frunk (56L total) of under-seat storage and a charging time of 5 hr 45 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 350W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W Ather Duo charger). Related reading: Bajaj Chetak vs Ather Rizta family EV comparison.

Meanwhile, the TVS iQube counters with Silent hub motor with zero belt maintenance, 32-litre under-seat storage on 3.4 kWh, and broad pan-India TVS dealership service support, providing 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) of storage and charging in 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Electricity Consumption (kWh/100 km) and Charging Cost Comparison

Accounting for ~13% AC-to-DC wall charging losses (4.15 kWh wall draw for 3.7 kWh on Ather Rizta vs 3.85 kWh wall draw for 3.4 kWh on TVS iQube), both scooters cost ₹0.24–₹0.35 per kilometre—roughly 85% cheaper to run than a petrol scooter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which gives better real-world range: Ather Rizta or TVS iQube?

In Eco mode, Ather Rizta delivers 95–100 km TrueRange (2.9 kWh) / 115–125 km TrueRange (3.7 kWh in SmartEco) (benchmark 120 km) while TVS iQube delivers 65 km (2.2 kWh) / 90–100 km (3.4 kWh) (benchmark 90 km), giving Ather Rizta the range edge.

What is the charging cost per kilometre of Ather Rizta vs TVS iQube?

At ₹7.00/kWh including charging losses, Ather Rizta costs ₹0.24/km and TVS iQube costs ₹0.30/km.

Which electric scooter charges faster?

Ather Rizta charges in 5 hr 45 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 350W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W Ather Duo charger), whereas TVS iQube takes 2 hr 00 min (0–80% on 2.2 kWh with 950W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.4 kWh with 650W charger) or 2 hr 40 min (950W).

Which has more under-seat boot space?

Ather Rizta provides 34 litres under-seat + optional 22-litre Frunk (56L total) compared to 30 litres (2.2 kWh) / 32 litres (3.4 kWh) on TVS iQube.

Which has a higher top speed?

Ather Rizta reaches 80 km/h compared to 75 km/h (2.2 kWh) / 78 km/h (3.4 kWh) for TVS iQube.

What are the ex-showroom prices of Ather Rizta and TVS iQube?

Ather Rizta costs ₹1,09,999 – ₹1,49,999 ex-showroom, while TVS iQube costs ₹94,999 – ₹1,29,444.
