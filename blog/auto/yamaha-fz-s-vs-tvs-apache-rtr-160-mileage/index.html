Yamaha FZ-S Fi V4 / Hybrid vs TVS Apache RTR 160 4V: Specifications & Claimed vs Real Mileage

Comparing Yamaha FZ-S Fi V4 / Hybrid and TVS Apache RTR 160 4V requires separating ARAI lab claims from independent road tests and owner averages. The Yamaha FZ-S Fi V4 / Hybrid pairs a 149.0cc engine (12.2 bhp @ 7,250 rpm, 13.3 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a 136 kg chassis and claims 60.0 km/l.

The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V uses a 159.7cc powertrain (17.31 bhp @ 9,250 rpm (Sport) / 15.42 bhp (Urban/Rain), 14.73 Nm @ 7,250 rpm) weighing 146 kg with a claimed 45.0 km/l. Their 10 kg mass difference and gearing (5-speed vs 5-speed with GTT) shape their real-world fuel gap.

City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Yamaha FZ-S Fi V4 / Hybrid features 136 kg light kerb weight, Traction Control System (TCS), single-channel ABS, 140/60-R17 radial rear tyre, and efficiency-focused Blue Core tuning, returning 45–49 km/l in urban use. The TVS Apache RTR 160 4V counters with 4-valve oil-cooled engine with O3C cooling, double-cradle Split Synchro Stiff frame, Showa monoshock, and 3 ride modes, achieving 40–44 km/l.

On open highways, the Yamaha FZ-S Fi V4 / Hybrid reaches 115 km/h and cruises cleanly at 70–85 km/h (48–52 km/l), whereas the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V tops out at 114 km/h and cruises comfortably at 75–90 km/h (44–48 km/l).
