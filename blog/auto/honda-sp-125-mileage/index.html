Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Honda SP 125

Evaluating honda sp 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Honda SP 125 is certified at 63.0 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating.

Driven by a 123.94cc (4-stroke, SI engine with eSP, piston cooling jet, and PGM-FI) motor producing 10.72 bhp @ 7,500 rpm and 10.9 Nm @ 6,000 rpm through a 5-speed transmission, the Honda SP 125 weighs 116 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Full digital LCD with real-time mileage, average fuel economy, distance-to-empty, gear position indicator, and LED headlamp. Across variants (Drum, Disc, Sports Edition), core engine mapping remains identical. Related reading: Honda Shine 125 claimed vs real-world fuel economy.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Honda SP 125 to 56–60 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Full digital LCD with real-time mileage, average fuel economy, distance-to-empty, gear position indicator, and LED headlamp) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 60–75 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 60–64 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 11.2L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Honda SP 125 in 2026?

The Honda SP 125 delivers 56–60 km/l in city traffic, 60–64 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 56–62 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Honda SP 125?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Honda SP 125 is 63.0 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Honda SP 125?

At 58 km/l, fuel cost is ₹1.76/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹1.92/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹1.81/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Honda SP 125?

With its 11.2-litre tank and 56–62 km/l real efficiency, full-tank range is 627–694 km.

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on Honda SP 125?

Cruising steadily at 60–75 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Honda SP 125 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
