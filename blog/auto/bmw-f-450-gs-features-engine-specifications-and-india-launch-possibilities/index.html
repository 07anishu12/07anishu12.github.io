1. Introduction

BMW Motorrad has officially unveiled the production-ready BMW F 450 GS at EICMA 2026 in Milan, converting the widespread acclaim of its earlier prototype into a definitive, showroom-ready adventure machine. Slotting directly between the entry-level single-cylinder G 310 GS and the larger middleweight F 800 GS / F 900 GS range, the F 450 GS is engineered to deliver genuine dual-sport versatility without the intimidating mass of heavy multi-cylinder adventure tourers. Built around an all-new 420cc parallel-twin engine delivering 48 hp, the F 450 GS achieves the targeted A2 minimum weight requirement of 175 kg, creating an exceptional power-to-weight ratio that promises effortless trail maneuverability and relaxed cross-country highway touring.

2. Launch and Availability

The production F 450 GS took center stage at EICMA 2026 on November 3, 2026, marking BMW Motorrad's primary global product launch of the exhibition. BMW confirmed that commercial market introductions will begin across Europe in spring 2027. Developed in strategic collaboration with TVS Motor Company, the F 450 GS will be manufactured at TVS's manufacturing facility in Hosur, India, for both global export and domestic sales. For the Indian domestic market, BMW Motorrad India has scheduled an official commercial launch in mid-2027, with customer bookings opening in the second quarter of 2027.

3. Engine and Performance

At the mechanical heart of the F 450 GS is an all-new 420cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke parallel-twin engine with dual overhead camshafts and four valves per cylinder. Engineered with a compact cylinder layout and a 270-degree firing order, the powerplant delivers 48 hp (35 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 43 Nm of peak torque at 6,750 rpm. The torque curve has been specifically flattened across the lower and middle rpm ranges to provide smooth, tractive power out of technical off-road ruts and steep uphill climbs. Power is directed through a six-speed gearbox featuring an anti-hopping clutch, with an optional electronic e-clutch system and bi-directional quickshifter allowing clutchless gear management across both city traffic and difficult terrain.

4. Design and hardware

The F 450 GS carries unmistakable GS DNA, featuring the iconic front beak, an aggressive asymmetrical LED headlight assembly, an upright rally-style touring windscreen, and rugged tank cladding designed for knee grip during standing riding. The chassis utilizes a newly designed tubular steel trellis frame paired with a lightweight aluminum subframe and a die-cast aluminum swingarm. Suspension hardware comprises 43mm inverted front forks delivering 180mm of travel, while the rear utilizes an adjustable monoshock with loaddependent damping providing an identical 180mm of wheel travel. Cross-spoked tubeless wheels (19-inch front and 17-inch rear) are fitted with dual-purpose adventure tires, achieving a generous ground clearance of 215mm with an accessible 830mm seat height.

5. Features and technology

True to BMW's technological leadership, the F 450 GS is outfitted with an intuitive 6.5-inch full-colour TFT instrument cluster featuring full smartphone connectivity, turn-by-turn navigation, and ride data logging. An advanced six-axis IMU governs lean-angle sensitive Cornering ABS (BMW Motorrad ABS Pro) and Dynamic Traction Control (DTC). The motorcycle provides four riding modes: Rain, Road, Enduro, and Enduro Pro (which disengages rear-wheel ABS for dirt sliding). Standard amenities include full-LED lighting, heated grips on higher trims, a USB-C fast charging port, and integrated mounting points for factory aluminum luggage.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the BMW F 450 GS is projected to carry an introductory base retail price of approximately €7,200 to €7,600. In India, local production by TVS Motor Company provides BMW with a commanding cost structure. Industry estimates place the expected ex-showroom price in India between Rs 3.80 lakh and Rs 4.20 lakh. This price positioning places the F 450 GS directly into the premium sweet spot between single-cylinder adventure offerings and high-end imported multi-cylinder tourers.

8. Rivals and alternatives

The BMW F 450 GS will directly compete against the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450, the KTM 390 Adventure, the CFMoto 450MT, and the Honda NX500. Against the Himalayan 450, the F 450 GS offers multi-cylinder smoothness, an optional electronic e-clutch, and superior highway cruising reserves. Compared to the Honda NX500, the BMW provides superior off-road credentials, a lighter kerb weight (175 kg vs 196 kg), and dedicated Enduro electronic modes.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What engine is fitted to the BMW F 450 GS?

It is powered by an all-new 420cc, liquid-cooled, parallel-twin engine generating 48 hp and 43 Nm of torque.

Q: What is the kerb weight of the BMW F 450 GS?

The motorcycle has a kerb weight of 175 kg, perfectly meeting the European A2 class power-to-weight limit.

Q: Will the BMW F 450 GS feature tubeless spoked wheels?

Yes, the motorcycle features 19-inch front and 17-inch rear cross-spoked wheels compatible with tubeless tires.

Q: When will the BMW F 450 GS launch in India?

BMW Motorrad has confirmed an Indian market launch for mid-2027.

10. Conclusion

The BMW F 450 GS unveiled at EICMA 2026 represents the holy grail for middleweight adventure motorcycling: a true multi-cylinder GS weighing just 175 kg with genuine off-road capability, sophisticated IMU electronics, and accessible pricing. By combining Bavarian adventure touring pedigree with localized manufacturing efficiency, the F 450 GS is destined to redefine global adventure touring benchmarks in 2027.
