Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 3.0 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 3.40 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 80 km per charge equals 3.75 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 4.25 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.

Charging time for the Honda Activa e: is Under 2 minutes via battery swap at Honda e:Swap station (Note: Activa e: relies on commercial battery-swap kiosks rather than home plug-in charging; Honda QC1 is the fixed-battery home-charged sibling). Keeping the lithium-ion pack between 20% and 80% State of Charge (SoC) maximizes battery life, meaning a scooter with 80 km real range comfortably supports a 52 km daily round trip with a healthy reserve.
