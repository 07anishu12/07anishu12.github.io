Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Suzuki Access 125

Evaluating suzuki access 125 mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Suzuki Access 125 is certified at 52.5 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. Related reading: TVS Jupiter 125 vs Suzuki Access 125 fuel efficiency comparison.

Driven by a 124.0cc (4-stroke, single-cylinder, air-cooled, SOHC 2-valve with SEP (Suzuki Eco Performance) FI) motor producing 8.58 bhp @ 6,750 rpm and 10.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm through a CVT Automatic transmission, the Suzuki Access 125 weighs 103 kg. Key efficiency hardware includes Light 103 kg kerb weight paired with brisk 8.58 bhp SEP engine, Eco Drive Illumination ring, and external tail-mounted fuel filler. Across variants (Drum, Disc, Special Edition, Ride Connect Edition (Bluetooth Turn-by-Turn Navigation)), core engine mapping remains identical.

City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

In dense metro commutes, frequent signal stops and 1st/2nd gear crawling hold the Suzuki Access 125 to 48–52 km/l. Using its efficiency tech (Light 103 kg kerb weight paired with brisk 8.58 bhp SEP engine, Eco Drive Illumination ring, and external tail-mounted fuel filler) trims idle fuel waste by 4%–7%. On highways at 50–65 km/h, top-gear cruising raises economy to 51–55 km/l.

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 5.0L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Suzuki Access 125 in 2026?

The Suzuki Access 125 delivers 48–52 km/l in city traffic, 51–55 km/l on highways, and an owner-reported mixed average of 47–52 km/l.

What is the ARAI claimed mileage of Suzuki Access 125?

The official ARAI-certified mileage of the Suzuki Access 125 is 52.5 km/l under laboratory test conditions.

What is the petrol cost per kilometre for Suzuki Access 125?

At 50 km/l, fuel cost is ₹2.04/km in Delhi (₹102.12/L), ₹2.22/km in Mumbai (₹111.21/L), and ₹2.10/km at ₹105/L.

What is the full-tank range of Suzuki Access 125?

What is the best cruising speed for mileage on Suzuki Access 125?

Cruising steadily at 50–65 km/h in top gear yields maximum fuel efficiency.

Does Suzuki Access 125 mileage increase after the first service?

Yes, fuel efficiency typically improves by 3–5 km/l after the 500–750 km first service once break-in oil is changed and valves are adjusted.
