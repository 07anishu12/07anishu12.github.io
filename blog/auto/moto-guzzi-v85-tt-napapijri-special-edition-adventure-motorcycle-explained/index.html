1. Introduction

Moto Guzzi has partnered with iconic Italian outdoor apparel and expedition brand Napapijri at EICMA 2026 to reveal the stunning Moto Guzzi V85 TT Napapijri special edition. Celebrating the shared ethos of exploration, Italian design heritage, and arctic expedition durability, this exclusive collaboration transforms the beloved 'Classic Travel Enduro' into an adventurous collector's piece. Dressed in bespoke livery inspired by the extreme landscapes of the Far North, the V85 TT Napapijri combines high-altitude touring hardware, factory aluminum luggage, and premium weather-resistant finishes with Moto Guzzi's characterful 853cc aircooled transverse V-twin engine, creating an evocative machine ready for grand transcontinental journeys.

2. Launch and Availability

The Moto Guzzi V85 TT Napapijri was officially unveiled on November 3, 2026, at the Moto Guzzi pavilion during EICMA in Milan. Moto Guzzi announced that this special edition will be produced in a limited, individually numbered run of 1,000 units globally, with the production number laser-etched onto the handlebar clamp. European deliveries will commence in March 2027. For the Indian market, Piaggio Vehicles Pvt. Ltd. (PVPL) confirmed that a small allocation of units will be imported via CBU channels for Indian collectors starting in the third quarter of 2027.

3. Engine and Performance

Powering the V85 TT Napapijri is Moto Guzzi's thoroughly refined 853cc, air-and-oil-cooled, transverse 90degree V-twin engine with variable valve timing (VVT). The engine produces 80 hp (58.8 kW) at 7,750 rpm and 83 Nm of peak torque at 5,100 rpm, with an impressive 90 percent of torque available from as low as 3,500 rpm. The variable valve timing system broadens the torque delivery, ensuring effortless roll-on acceleration when carrying a passenger and fully loaded hard luggage across steep mountain passes. Power is directed through a six-speed transmission and an enclosed cardan shaft drive housed inside the robust aluminum swingarm, completely eliminating chain adjustments on remote overland journeys.

4. Design and hardware

The aesthetic signature of the V85 TT Napapijri is a masterclass in expedition styling. The bodywork is painted in an exclusive matte 'Arctic White' and 'Nordic Blue' two-tone palette, accented by subtle topographic map graphics and Napapijri's iconic Norwegian flag emblem on the tank flanks. The motorcycle comes factory-equipped with rugged aluminum side panniers and a matching top case offering a combined 112 litres of lockable storage. Additional expedition hardware includes a high touring windscreen, tubular steel engine crash guards, an aluminum oil sump guard, heated rider grips, and tubeless cross-spoked wheels shod in Michelin Anakee Adventure tires.

5. Features and technology

The cockpit features a 5.0-inch full-colour TFT instrument cluster equipped with Moto Guzzi MIA multimedia connectivity as standard, providing full-map turn-by-turn navigation and smartphone telemetry. Electronic rider aids, governed by a six-axis IMU, include Cornering ABS and Moto Guzzi Traction Control (MGCT). Riders can toggle between five distinct riding modes: Road, Sport, Rain, Off-Road (which disengages rearwheel ABS for gravel exploration), and a customizable Custom mode. Cruise control is standard, operated via an intuitive single-button handlebar switch.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the limited-edition Moto Guzzi V85 TT Napapijri is priced at €15,999, representing exceptional value given the comprehensive inclusion of factory aluminum luggage, heated grips, and exclusive apparel accessories. In India, where CBU taxation applies, the estimated ex-showroom price is projected between Rs 16.50 lakh and Rs 18.00 lakh. Moto Guzzi positions the model as an exclusive, character-rich adventure tourer for connoisseurs who value classic Italian styling over aggressive modern rally shapes.

7. India launch possibility

8. Rivals and alternatives

The Moto Guzzi V85 TT Napapijri competes against middleweight adventure tourers such as the BMW F 900 GS Adventure, the Triumph Tiger 900 GT Pro, and the Suzuki V-Strom 800DE. Compared to its competitors, the V85 TT is the only motorcycle in the class to offer the timeless character of an air-cooled transverse Vtwin, the hassle-free reliability of a shaft drive, and limited-edition designer cachet.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What makes the Napapijri edition special on the V85 TT?

It features an exclusive Arctic expedition color scheme, Napapijri branding, full factory aluminum luggage, heated grips, touring screen, and is limited to 1,000 numbered units globally.

Q: How much power does the Moto Guzzi V85 TT make?

The 853cc transverse V-twin engine produces 80 hp at 7,750 rpm and 83 Nm of peak torque at 5,100 rpm, featuring variable valve timing.

Q: Does the V85 TT require chain lubrication?

No, the V85 TT uses an enclosed cardan shaft final drive, eliminating chain cleaning, lubrication, and tension adjustments entirely.

Q: Will the V85 TT Napapijri be available in India?

Yes, a limited allocation of CBU units will be available through Motoplex dealerships in India starting in the third quarter of 2027.

10. Conclusion

The Moto Guzzi V85 TT Napapijri unveiled at EICMA 2026 is an exquisite fusion of Italian engineering soul and outdoor adventure lifestyle. By pairing the versatile, shaft-driven 80 hp V85 TT platform with bespoke Arctic styling, full expedition luggage, and unmatched exclusivity, Moto Guzzi and Napapijri have created a collector-grade adventure machine that inspires riders to point their compass toward the horizon.
