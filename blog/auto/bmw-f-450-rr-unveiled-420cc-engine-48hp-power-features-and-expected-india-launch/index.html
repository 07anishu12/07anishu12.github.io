1. Introduction

BMW Motorrad has made a stunning statement in the lightweight supersport arena at EICMA 2026 with the global unveiling of the all-new BMW F 450 RR. Drawing aggressive aerodynamic and aesthetic inspiration from the class-dominating S 1000 RR superbike, the F 450 RR represents BMW's first dedicated parallel-twin supersport motorcycle engineered specifically for the global A2 licence tier. While the previous single-cylinder G 310 RR provided an accessible entry point, track-day riders and sportbike purists long demanded multicylinder revving characteristics, sophisticated lean-angle electronics, and serious racetrack potential. The F 450 RR fulfills these aspirations by combining an all-new 420cc twin-cylinder powerplant with a lightweight chassis, integrated downforce winglets, and comprehensive electronic rider aids, setting a blistering new standard for sub-500cc sportbikes.

2. Launch and Availability

The BMW F 450 RR was officially presented during BMW Motorrad's keynote address at EICMA 2026 on November 3, 2026. Production is scheduled to commence in early 2027 under BMW's strategic manufacturing partnership with TVS Motor Company in India. European and North American market sales are confirmed to begin in May 2027, catering directly to sportbike riders progressing through tiered licensing systems. For the Indian market, BMW Motorrad India has confirmed an official launch slated for mid-2027, with retail distribution managed through BMW Motorrad dealerships across the country.

3. Engine and Performance

At the core of the F 450 RR is BMW's newly developed 420cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke parallel-twin engine with dual overhead camshafts and four valves per cylinder. Engineered with a short-stroke bore and stroke architecture (72.0mm x 51.6mm) and a high 13.0:1 compression ratio, the motor revs eagerly to an 11,500 rpm redline. It produces an exact 48 hp (35 kW) at 8,750 rpm and 43 Nm of peak torque at 6,750 rpm, perfectly maximizing the European A2 power-to-weight ceiling. Power delivery is managed via a six-speed gearbox featuring an anti-hopping assist clutch and BMW's Shift Assistant Pro (bi-directional quickshifter). BMW claims a 0-100 km/h acceleration time of 5.1 seconds and a top track speed exceeding 180 km/h.

4. Design and hardware

Styled with unmistakable Bavarian motorsport DNA, the F 450 RR features sharp, wind-tunnel-developed bodywork complete with functional aerodynamic winglets that generate genuine downforce at high track speeds. The front fairing incorporates twin sunken LED projector headlights flanking a central ram-air induction duct, mirroring the aggressive face of the M 1000 RR. Beneath the fairings lies a lightweight tubular steel bridge frame paired with an asymmetrical cast aluminum swingarm. Suspension is commanded by 43mm inverted front forks offering full compression and rebound damping adjustability, while the rear utilizes an adjustable monoshock. Lightweight 17-inch cast aluminum wheels are shod in sticky sport-compound rubber (110/70-17 front and 150/60-17 rear), keeping total kerb weight down to an agile 165 kg.

5. Features and technology

The technological loadout on the F 450 RR is class-leading. A 6.5-inch full-colour TFT instrument console features a dedicated racetrack display mode showcasing lap timers, lean-angle indicators, and shift lights alongside Bluetooth smartphone integration. A sophisticated electronic suite powered by a six-axis IMU includes BMW Dynamic Traction Control (DTC), Engine Drag Torque Control (MSR), and BMW Motorrad Race ABS with Supermoto brake slide functionality. Riders can toggle between four riding modes: Rain, Road, Dynamic, and Race, with the Race mode allowing tailored customization of throttle response and traction intervention.

6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the BMW F 450 RR is projected to retail between €6,800 and €7,200. In India, local manufacturing synergy at TVS's Hosur facility will allow BMW Motorrad India to offer highly competitive pricing compared to imported supersport machinery. The estimated ex-showroom price in India is expected to sit between Rs 3.60 lakh and Rs 4.00 lakh. This positions the F 450 RR directly above single-cylinder offerings, giving track-day enthusiasts a premium German twin at an accessible price.

8. Rivals and alternatives

The BMW F 450 RR enters into fierce competition with the Yamaha YZF-R3, the Kawasaki Ninja 500, the Aprilia RS 457, and the KTM RC 390. Compared to the Aprilia RS 457, the BMW F 450 RR matches its 48 hp output while providing a more comprehensive IMU-backed electronics package and aerodynamic winglet technology. Against the Ninja 500, the BMW offers superior inverted suspension hardware and an authentic track-focused geometry.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the top speed of the BMW F 450 RR?

BMW claims a track-tested top speed exceeding 180 km/h, with a 0-100 km/h sprint completed in 5.1 seconds.

Q: How much power does the BMW F 450 RR make?

The 420cc parallel-twin engine produces 48 hp at 8,750 rpm and 43 Nm of torque at 6,750 rpm.

Q: When will the BMW F 450 RR launch in India?

BMW Motorrad has confirmed that the F 450 RR will launch in India in mid-2027.

Q: Where is the BMW F 450 RR manufactured?

The motorcycle is manufactured in India through BMW Motorrad's long-standing strategic manufacturing alliance with TVS Motor Company.

10. Conclusion

The BMW F 450 RR unveiled at EICMA 2026 is a masterclass in modern lightweight supersport design. By bringing S 1000 RR aesthetic dna, aerodynamic winglet technology, an eager 48 hp parallel-twin engine, and track-grade electronics into the accessible A2 bracket, BMW has engineered the definitive track-day weapon for rising sportbike riders. It promises to become an instant sensation both on international circuits and Indian canyon roads.
