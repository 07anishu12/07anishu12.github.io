Certified Claimed Range vs Real-World Distance per Charge of Ola S1 Pro

When researching ola s1 pro range, buyers must never use petrol units (km/l) and must separate Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) laboratory range from real-world on-road range across riding modes, nominal battery capacity (kWh) vs wall-meter energy consumed (kWh), and electricity cost per kilometre (₹/km). Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 4.0 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 4.50 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 130 km per charge equals 3.08 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 3.46 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.

Charging time for the Ola S1 Pro is 4 hr 50 min (0–80%) / 6 hr 30 min (0–100% with 750W portable home charger). Keeping the lithium-ion pack between 20% and 80% State of Charge (SoC) maximizes battery life, meaning a scooter with 130 km real range comfortably supports a 84 km daily round trip with a healthy reserve.

Factors That Reduce EV Range and Conditional Buyer Verdict

Six real-world variables reduce electric scooter range by 15%–30%: riding continuously in Sport/Power mode at 120 km/h (4 kWh), carrying a pillion, under-inflated tyres, steep gradients, summer heat above 40°C, and gradual cell aging after 3–5 years. Conditional Verdict: Priced at ₹1,29,999 – ₹1,59,999 ex-showroom, the Ola S1 Pro suits commuters covering up to 91 km/day who want ₹0.24/km energy costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world range of Ola S1 Pro in 2026?

In city riding, the Ola S1 Pro delivers 130–145 km (4 kWh in Eco) / 160–185 km (5.3 kWh Pro+ in Eco) in Eco mode and 95–110 km (4 kWh in Normal/Sport) / 80–85 km (Hyper mode) in Power/Sport modes.

What is the claimed IDC range of Ola S1 Pro?

The official claimed range of the Ola S1 Pro is 195 km IDC (4 kWh) / 242–320 km IDC (Pro+) depending on battery variant (4.0 kWh (Standard S1 Pro) / 5.2–5.3 kWh (S1 Pro+)).

How much does it cost to fully charge Ola S1 Pro at home?

Charging the 4.0 kWh pack draws ~4.50 kWh from the wall, costing ₹24.75 in Delhi (₹5.50/kWh), ₹38.25 in Maharashtra (₹8.50/kWh), and ₹31.50 at ₹7.00/kWh.

What is the per-kilometre running cost of Ola S1 Pro?

At 130 km real range and 4.50 kWh wall draw, electricity cost is ₹0.19/km in Delhi and ₹0.24/km at ₹7.00/kWh.

How long does Ola S1 Pro take to charge?

Charging time is 4 hr 50 min (0–80%) / 6 hr 30 min (0–100% with 750W portable home charger).

What is the top speed and boot space of Ola S1 Pro?

The Ola S1 Pro has a top speed of 120 km/h (4 kWh) and under-seat storage of 34 litres.
