Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Yamaha Fascino 125 Fi Hybrid

Evaluating yamaha fascino 125 fi hybrid mileage requires separating five distinct figures: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, dashboard readouts, and calculated petrol costs. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) dynamometer testing, the Yamaha Fascino 125 Fi Hybrid is certified at 68.75 km/l. Because lab rollers eliminate wind drag, gradients, pillion weight, and signal idling, on-road efficiency is typically 10% to 18% lower than the brochure rating. City vs Highway Performance and Full-Tank Verification

Because digital console economy readouts typically overestimate by 3%–6%, verify your bike's true mileage via the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 5.2L tank to auto-cut, ride 100+ km, refill to auto-cut at the same nozzle, and divide distance by litres added.
