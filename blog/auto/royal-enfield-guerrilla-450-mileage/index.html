Claimed Mileage vs Real-World Efficiency of Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450

Assessing royal enfield guerrilla 450 mileage requires distinguishing five metrics: ARAI-certified laboratory claims, independent city/highway road tests, owner-reported mixed averages, instrument-console readouts, and calculated fuel expenses. Under standardized Indian Driving Cycle (IDC) laboratory testing, the Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450 carries a certified rating of 29.5 km/l. On actual Indian roads—accounting for kerb weight (184 kg), wind resistance, gradients, and traffic signals—real-world efficiency sits between 26–30 km/l. City Traffic Behaviour, Highway Cruising and Full-Tank Measurement

In urban traffic, accelerating 184 kg of motorcycle plus rider from repeated stops holds fuel economy to 25–28 km/l. Short-shifting into 3rd and 4th gear using the engine's 40.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm torque curve prevents unnecessary fuel burn. On open highways at 95–115 km/h in top gear, the engine runs unstressed and achieves 29–32 km/l, whereas pushing toward its 150 km/h top speed increases aerodynamic drag and drops mileage by 4–6 km/l.

To measure true fuel consumption on your Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450, use the full-tank auto-cut method: fill the 11.0-litre tank on the centre/level stand until the pump nozzle auto-cuts, reset Trip A, ride 150+ km, refill at the same station to auto-cut, and divide distance by litres dispensed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world mileage of Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450 in 2026?

The Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450 returns 25–28 km/l in city traffic, 29–32 km/l on highways, and a mixed owner average of 26–30 km/l.

What is the full-tank range of Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450?

Cruising steadily at 95–115 km/h in top gear delivers optimal fuel economy and low vibration.

Does Royal Enfield Guerrilla 450 mileage improve after the first service?
