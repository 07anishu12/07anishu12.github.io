Limitations, Analytical Caveats & Common Mistakes

A primary analytical error when utilizing fully diluted ownership calculations: options, warrants, and notes is treating static point estimates as definitive forecasts without dynamic sensitivity modeling. Practitioners frequently commit 'false precision' bias by projecting cash flows to decimal places while relying on unverified qualitative assumptions. Additionally, failing to normalize for aggressive accounting accruals, deferred maintenance capital expenditures, or structural customer concentration creates severe downside vulnerability. Finally, ignoring secondary market liquidity constraints and execution friction often leads to overestimating net realized.
