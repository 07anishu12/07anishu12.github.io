Introduction

How to Prepare and Analyse Consolidated Financial Statements covers line-by-line consolidation of 100% of subsidiary assets/liabilities/revenues/expenses even when owning < 100%, eliminating intercompany receivables/sales, NCI income allocation, and VIE consolidation in Level II Financial Statement Analysis (LM 1: Intercorporate Investments — Line-by-line consolidation mechanics, intercompany elimination, Non-Controlling Interest (NCI) on Income Statement and Balance Sheet, Special Purpose Entities (SPEs) and Variable Interest Entities (VIEs)), with verified formulas, two worked examples, and three practice MCQs.

Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

Consolidated Balance Sheet & Intercompany Elimination Rules (Consolidated Assets = P_Assets (excl Inv in S) + 100% S_Assets (at Fair Value) + Goodwill − Intercompany AR/Inventory Profit) and Consolidated Net Income & Non-Controlling Interest (NCI) Share (NCI Share of Net Income = NCI % × (Subsidiary Net Income − Fair Value Excess Depreciation − Upstream Unrealized Profit)) govern line-by-line consolidation of 100% of subsidiary assets/liabilities/revenues/expenses even when owning < 100%, eliminating intercompany receivables/sales, NCI income allocation, and VIE consolidation.

Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: Consolidated Balance Sheet & Intercompany Elimination Rules

Formula Expression
Consolidated Assets = P_Assets (excl Inv in S) + 100% S_Assets (at Fair Value) + Goodwill − Intercompany AR/Inventory Profit

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Always combine 100% of the subsidiary's assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses (never pro-rate by 80%!), then carve out the 20% Non-Controlling Interest in Equity and Net Income.

Formula 2: Consolidated Net Income & Non-Controlling Interest (NCI) Share

Formula Expression
NCI Share of Net Income = NCI % × (Subsidiary Net Income − Fair Value Excess Depreciation − Upstream Unrealized Profit)

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Deducted at the bottom of the consolidated income statement to arrive at Net Income Attributable to Controlling Parent Shareholders.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Constructing a Consolidated Balance Sheet and Income Statement for an 80%-Owned Subsidiary

Problem & Given Scenario: Parent P buys 80% of Sub S on Jan 1 for $400M (100% FV = $500M). S's Book Net Assets = $350M, and S's PP&E has a +$50M fair value step-up (10-year life -> $5M/yr extra depreciation), so FV of Identifiable Net Assets = $400M and Full Goodwill = $100M (NCI_0 = $100M). During Year 1: P has standalone Revenue = $900M and Operating Expenses = $700M; S has Revenue = $300M and Operating Expenses = $225M (S standalone NI = $75M). Furthermore, P sold $40M of goods to S during the year (all resold to outside customers by year-end, so $0 unrealized profit left in ending inventory). Compute Consolidated Revenue, Consolidated Operating Profit, and Net Income Attributable to Parent P.

Formula Expression
Consolidated Assets = P_Assets (excl Inv in S) + 100% S_Assets (at Fair Value) + Goodwill − Intercompany AR/Inventory Profit

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Step 1 (Consolidated Revenue — Eliminate $40M intercompany sales!): $900M + $300M − $40M = $1,160M. Step 2 (Consolidated Operating Expenses — Eliminate $40M intercompany COGS and add $5M FV PP&E depreciation!): $700M + $225M − $40M + $5M = $890M. Step 3 (Total Consolidated Net Income, zero tax for simplicity): $1,160M − $890M = $270M. Step 4 (NCI Share of S's Adjusted NI): 20% × ($75M − $5M FV Depr) = 20% × $70M = $14M. Step 5 (Net Income Attributable to Parent P): $270M − $14M = $256M.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Intercompany sales ($40M) must be eliminated from both Consolidated Revenue and Consolidated COGS so total external revenue is not double-counted.

Worked Example 2: Consolidating a Variable Interest Entity (VIE) Under US GAAP / IFRS 10

Problem & Given Scenario: Corporation M creates a Special Purpose Entity (SPE) to finance $200M of equipment leases. Third-party investors hold 97% of the nominal voting equity of the SPE ($6M), while Corporation M guarantees the SPE's debt losses and receives 85% of residual variable cash flows.

Formula Expression
NCI Share of Net Income = NCI % × (Subsidiary Net Income − Fair Value Excess Depreciation − Upstream Unrealized Profit)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Under US GAAP (VIE rules) and IFRS 10 (control model), voting percentages are ignored when an entity is thinly capitalized or controlled via contractual risk/reward agreements. Because Corporation M has (1) the power to direct the activities of the VIE and (2) the obligation to absorb losses / right to receive residual benefits, Corporation M is the Primary Beneficiary and MUST consolidate 100% of the VIE onto its balance sheet!

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Post-Enron accounting standards (ASC 810 / IFRS 10) require the Primary Beneficiary of a Variable Interest Entity (VIE) to consolidate 100% of the VIE's assets and debt.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: Parent Corp owns 70% of Subsidiary Inc. During the year, Parent Corp had standalone revenues of $800 million, Subsidiary Inc. had standalone revenues of $250 million, and Parent Corp sold $30 million of components to Subsidiary Inc. (all of which Subsidiary Inc. assembled and sold to third-party customers before year-end). Consolidated revenue for the year is:

  • [A] $945 million

  • [B] $1,020 million

  • [C] $1,050 million

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Consolidation combines 100% of Parent revenue ($800M) and 100% of Subsidiary revenue ($250M), and eliminates 100% of intercompany sales ($30M): $800M + $250M − $30M = $1,020 million.

Practice Question 2

Question: Where is Non-Controlling Interest (minority interest) reported on a consolidated balance sheet under both IFRS and US GAAP?

  • [A] As a mezzanine liability between Long-Term Debt and Shareholders' Equity

  • [B] Within Consolidated Shareholders' Equity, presented separately from the parent shareholders' equity

  • [C] As a contra-asset deducted from Goodwill

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Both IFRS and US GAAP require Non-Controlling Interest (NCI) to be classified inside Consolidated Total Shareholders' Equity (separate from controlling parent equity).

Practice Question 3

Question: Under US GAAP, an enterprise must consolidate a Variable Interest Entity (VIE) as its 'Primary Beneficiary' when the enterprise:

  • [A] Owns more than 50% of the VIE's common voting stock

  • [B] Has both the power to direct the activities that most significantly impact the VIE's economic performance AND the obligation to absorb potentially significant losses (or right to receive significant benefits) of the VIE

  • [C] Provides an arm's-length bank loan to the VIE

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: The Primary Beneficiary of a VIE is the party holding both decision-making power over key economic activities and exposure to expected losses or residual returns, regardless of voting share ownership.

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Consolidated Balance Sheet & Intercompany Elimination Rules.

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Consolidated Net Income & Non-Controlling Interest (NCI) Share.

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
