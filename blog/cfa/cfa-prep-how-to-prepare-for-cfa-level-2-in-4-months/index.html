Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Week-by-Week 16-Week (320-Hour at 20 Hours/Week) CFA Level II Execution Schedule

Problem & Given Scenario: Map out a concrete 16-week calendar for a candidate investing 20 hours per week (2 hours/day Mon–Fri + 5 hours Sat + 5 hours Sun = 320 total hours).

Formula Expression
Weeks 1–7 (7 Weeks): Master the 'Big Five' (Equity, FSA, Fixed Income, PM, Ethics = 50%–75% of Exam)

Weeks 8–12 (5 Weeks): Tactical Five (Derivatives, Quant, CI, Alt, Econ)

Weeks 13–16 (4 Weeks): 5 Full Item-Set Mocks + Blue-Box Drills

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

• Weeks 1–2 (40 hrs): Equity Valuation (DDM, H-Model, FCFF/FCFE, Residual Income, Multiples, Private Co). • Weeks 3–4 (40 hrs): Financial Statement Analysis (Intercorporate Inv, Pensions, Stock Comp, Multinational FX, Banks, Earnings Quality). • Weeks 5–6 (40 hrs): Fixed Income (Spot/Forward curves, Binomial Trees, Callable/Putable/Convertible, CVA Credit, CDS). • Week 7 (20 hrs): Portfolio Management (Active Mgmt Fundamental Law, Multifactor Models, VaR, ETFs). • Weeks 8–9 (40 hrs): Derivatives (Forwards/FRA/Swaps, Binomial Options, Black-Scholes, Greeks) & Quantitative Methods (Multiple Regression, Diagnostics, Time Series). • Weeks 10–11 (40 hrs): Corporate Issuers (M&A, Divestitures, WACC/Grinold-Kroner, Dividends, ESG) & Alternative Investments (REIT NAVPS, Real Estate DCF, PE/VC Method, Hedge Funds) & Economics (FX Parity, Mundell-Fleming, Solow Growth). • Week 12 (20 hrs): Ethics & GIPS Vignettes + Complete Practical Skills Module. • Weeks 13–16 (80 hrs): 5 Full-Length Vignette Mock Exams + EOC Re-Solves + Formula/Blue-Box Mastery.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: By completing the Big Five by Week 7 (and Ethics in Week 12 + daily in Weeks 13–16), you lock down 50%–75% of the exam with two full months left on the clock!

Worked Example 2: Transitioning from Level I One-Step Recall to Level II Multi-Step Vignette Chains

Problem & Given Scenario: Contrast how FCFF is tested at Level I vs. Level II to show why 4-month Level II candidates must drill multi-step item sets immediately.

Formula Expression
Every Level II Exam Vignette Is Modeled Directly on Official Curriculum In-Text 'Blue-Box' Worked Examples and End-of-Chapter (EOC) Item Sets

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

At Level I: You are given CFO = $200M, Interest = $20M, t = 25%, and FCInv = $80M, and asked for FCFF in 1 step ($135M). At Level II: Exhibit 1 gives an Income Statement and Balance Sheet; Question 1 asks you to adjust EBIT for an operating lease or R&D; Question 2 asks you to compute WCInv (remembering to exclude Cash and Short-Term Debt) and FCFF; Question 3 asks you to plug that FCFF into a two-stage DCF model; and Question 4 asks how a share buyback affects the WACC used in Question 3!

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Practicing end-to-end 4-question item sets from Day 1 trains you to pull exact lines out of 3 financial statement exhibits without getting overwhelmed.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: When preparing for CFA Level II on a compressed 4-month (16-week) timeline, why should a candidate study Equity Investments, Financial Statement Analysis, and Fixed Income in the first 6 weeks rather than saving them for the end?

  • [A] Because they are the easiest subjects on the exam

  • [B] Because together they represent 30% to 45% of the entire Level II exam score and contain the heaviest multi-step accounting and valuation models, requiring maximum time for spaced repetition and practice

  • [C] Because they are only tested in Session 1

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Equity, FSA, and Fixed Income are the deepest calculation-and-analysis pillars of Level II; conquering them in Weeks 1–6 prevents running out of time on high-weight topics in Month 4.

Practice Question 2

Question: Which official CFA Institute study resource is most closely aligned with the structure, difficulty, and exhibit style of actual CFA Level II exam vignettes?

  • [A] Glossary definitions at the back of the book

  • [B] The in-text worked 'Blue-Box' examples and the End-of-Chapter (EOC) / Learning Ecosystem vignette item sets

  • [C] Level I flashcards

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Level II exam item sets are patterned directly after the curriculum's multi-part in-text 'Blue-Box' examples and End-of-Chapter vignette problems.

Practice Question 3

Question: To maintain a 320-hour study target over a 16-week (4-month) preparation window, how many hours per week must a candidate consistently log, and when should full-length vignette practice begin?

  • [A] 10 hours/week; wait until Week 15 to solve any vignette questions

  • [B] 20 hours/week (e.g., 2 hours per weekday + 10 hours across the weekend), solving End-of-Chapter vignette item sets immediately after finishing each learning module starting in Week 1

  • [C] 40 hours/week in the final month only

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: A 4-month Level II timeline requires ~20 disciplined hours per week and immediate vignette practice after every single learning module.
