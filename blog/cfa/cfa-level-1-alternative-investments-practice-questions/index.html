Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Comparing Hedge Fund Net Return Under Independent vs. Net-of-Management-Fee Rules

Problem & Given Scenario: A hedge fund starts with $100.0M and grows to $120.0M (+20.0% gross) at year-end. It charges a 2.0% management fee on year-end AUM ($2.40M) and a 20% incentive fee with no hurdle. Compare total fees under (A) Incentive calculated Independently vs. (B) Incentive calculated Net of Management Fee.

Formula Expression
TVPI = (Distributed + NAV) / Paid-In

Property V_0 = NOI_1 / Cap Rate

Roll Yield = (Spot − Futures) / Futures

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Case A (Independent): Mgmt Fee = $2.40M; Incentive Fee = 20% × ($120.0M − $100.0M) = $4.00M; Total Fees = $6.40M (Investor Net Return = +13.60%). Case B (Net of Management Fee): Mgmt Fee = $2.40M; Incentive Fee = 20% × ($120.0M − $100.0M − $2.40M) = 20% × $17.60M = $3.52M; Total Fees = $5.92M (Investor Net Return = +14.08%).

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Calculating the incentive fee net of management fees saves the investor $480,000 (+48 bps higher net return).

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: A hedge fund has a Beginning AUM of $80 million and an Ending Gross AUM of $100 million (+25% gross return). It charges a 1.5% management fee on Ending Gross AUM and a 15% incentive fee calculated INDEPENDENTLY of the management fee over a 5% ($4 million) HARD hurdle rate. Total fees paid to the manager are:

  • [A] $3.675 million

  • [B] $3.900 million

  • [C] $4.500 million

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Management fee = 1.5% × $100M = $1.50M. Gross profit = $100M − $80M = $20M. Hard hurdle = 5% × $80M = $4.0M. Because the incentive fee is calculated independently of the management fee, Incentive Fee = 15% × ($20M − $4M) = 15% × $16M = $2.40M. Total fees = $1.50M + $2.40M = $3.900 million.

Practice Question 2

Question: In distributed ledger technology (DLT) and digital assets, what is the primary economic difference between 'Proof of Work' (PoW) and 'Proof of Stake' (PoS) consensus mechanisms?

  • [A] Proof of Work relies on miners solving energy-intensive cryptographic puzzles, whereas Proof of Stake relies on validators locking up (staking) native network tokens as economic collateral to validate transactions

  • [B] Proof of Stake requires a central bank clearinghouse

  • [C] Proof of Work cannot record smart contracts

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: PoW secures the blockchain via computational hash power and energy expenditure, while PoS secures the ledger by requiring validators to stake financial capital that can be slashed for dishonest behavior.

Practice Question 3

Question: An institutional investor adds a low-correlation alternative asset class to a traditional 60/40 stock-bond portfolio. If the alternative asset has the same expected return as equities but a correlation of +0.15 with the 60/40 portfolio, adding a 15% allocation will:

  • [A] Shift the Markowitz efficient frontier upward and to the left, increasing the portfolio's Sharpe ratio

  • [B] Leave the portfolio's standard deviation unchanged

  • [C] Increase the portfolio's beta to +1.50

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Adding a low-correlation asset with competitive expected return reduces portfolio variance for any given target return, shifting the efficient frontier upward/leftward and improving the Sharpe ratio.
