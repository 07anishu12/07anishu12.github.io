Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: End-to-End FSA Case: Interest Capitalization and Interest Coverage Adjustment

Problem & Given Scenario: A utility constructs a power plant spending $100M (weighted average outlay) financed by a 6.0% construction loan ($6.0M capitalized interest). It also pays $18.0M of expensed interest on general corporate bonds and reports EBIT = $72.0M.

Formula Expression
LIFO Reserve Adj: Equity_FIFO = Equity_LIFO + Reserve×(1−t)

FCFF = CFO + Int×(1−t) − FCInv

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Reported Interest Coverage = EBIT / Expensed Interest = $72.0M / $18.0M = 4.00x. Analyst Adjusted Interest Coverage (including capitalized interest in denominator and deducting capitalized interest depreciation if known): Total Interest = $18.0M + $6.0M = $24.0M; Adjusted Coverage = $72.0M / $24.0M = 3.00x.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: CFA Institute recommends including capitalized interest in total interest expense when computing the Interest Coverage Ratio, reducing coverage from 4.00x to 3.00x.

Worked Example 2: End-to-End FSA Case: Revaluation Model Accounting under IFRS (IAS 16)

Problem & Given Scenario: An IFRS firm buys land for $50M at t = 0. At t = 1, fair value drops to $42M. At t = 2, fair value rebounds to $56M. Track the Income Statement and OCI impacts.

Formula Expression
Capitalized Interest = Borrowing Rate × Weighted Avg Construction Outlays

Sale Gain = Cash Proceeds − Net Book Value

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

At t = 1: Revaluation decrease below cost ($50M -> $42M) is recognized as an $8M loss on the Income Statement (Profit & Loss). At t = 2: Revaluation increase from $42M to $56M (+$14M total) first reverses the prior $8M loss on the Income Statement (+$8M gain in P&L), and the excess $6M above original cost ($56M − $50M) is credited to Revaluation Surplus in OCI (+$6M in Equity).

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Revaluation movements below historical cost hit the Income Statement, whereas gains above historical cost go to Revaluation Surplus in OCI.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: Under IFRS (IAS 16), a company using the revaluation model bought a building for $20 million (ignoring depreciation). In Year 1, its fair value increased to $23 million. In Year 2, its fair value declined to $18 million. How is the Year 2 decline of $5 million recorded?

  • [A] $5 million loss on the income statement

  • [B] $3 million reduction in Revaluation Surplus (OCI) and $2 million loss on the income statement

  • [C] $5 million reduction in Revaluation Surplus (OCI)

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: The Year 1 gain of $3M created a $3M Revaluation Surplus in OCI. In Year 2, the $5M drop first eliminates the $3M Revaluation Surplus in OCI, and the remaining $2M drop below original cost ($20M -> $18M) is expensed on the income statement.

Practice Question 2

Question: An analyst computes a company's cash conversion cycle (CCC) and discovers that Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH) is 55 days, Days of Sales Outstanding (DSO) is 35 days, and Days of Payables Outstanding (DPO) is 62 days. If the company stretches supplier payment terms so DPO increases to 70 days, its cash conversion cycle:

  • [A] Decreases from 28 days to 20 days, freeing up operating cash flow

  • [B] Increases from 28 days to 36 days

  • [C] Decreases from 152 days to 144 days

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Initial CCC = 55 + 35 − 62 = 28 days. New CCC = 55 + 35 − 70 = 20 days, reducing working capital financing needs by 8 days.

Practice Question 3

Question: A firm reports Net Income of $60 million, Preferred Dividends of $4 million, Weighted Average Common Shares of 14 million, and $20 million of 7% convertible bonds (tax rate = 25%) convertible into 400,000 common shares. What is the firm's Diluted EPS?

  • [A] $3.96

  • [B] $4.00

  • [C] $4.09

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Basic EPS = ($60M − $4M) / 14M = $4.00. Incremental EPS of the convertible bonds = [$20M × 7% × (1 − 0.25)] / 0.4M = $1.05M / 0.4M = $2.625 < $4.00 (dilutive). Diluted EPS = ($56M + $1.05M) / (14.0M + 0.4M) = $57.05M / 14.4M = $3.96.
