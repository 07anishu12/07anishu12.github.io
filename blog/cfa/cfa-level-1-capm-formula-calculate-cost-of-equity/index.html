Introduction

CAPM Formula: How to Calculate Cost of Equity is a core Level I Corporate Issuers topic (LM 6 & Portfolio Management LM 5: CAPM Cost of Equity, Pure-Play Beta Unlevering and Relevering, Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium approach, Country Risk Premium) focusing on calculating Cost of Equity via CAPM, unlevering and relevering peer beta (Pure-Play Method) for private firms or new divisions, and the Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium build-up method. This guide covers the governing equations (Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) Cost of Equity and Pure-Play Beta Unlevering (Asset Beta) & Relevering), two verified numerical examples, a comparison table, and three original CFA-style practice questions.

Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

From first principles, calculating Cost of Equity via CAPM, unlevering and relevering peer beta (Pure-Play Method) for private firms or new divisions, and the Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium build-up method links theoretical valuation and risk mechanics to practical decisions in Corporate Issuers. Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) Cost of Equity (r_e = R_F + β_e × [E(R_M) − R_F] = R_F + (β_e × ERP)) establishes the primary relationship (R_F = long-term government risk-free yield; β_e = levered equity beta; ERP = E(R_M) − R_F is the Equity Risk Premium.), while Pure-Play Beta Unlevering (Asset Beta) & Relevering (β_Asset = β_Equity_Peer / [1 + (1 − t_Peer) × (D_Peer / E_Peer)] ; β_Project = β_Asset × [1 + (1 − t_Subject) × (D_Subject / E_Subject)]) governs Strips out the comparable public peer's financial leverage to isolate unlevered business risk (β_Asset), then re-levers to the target firm's D/E and tax rate.

Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) Cost of Equity

Formula Expression
r_e = R_F + β_e × [E(R_M) − R_F] = R_F + (β_e × ERP)

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:

  • R_F = long-term government risk-free yield.

  • β_e = levered equity beta.

  • ERP = E(R_M) − R_F is the Equity Risk Premium.

Formula 2: Pure-Play Beta Unlevering (Asset Beta) & Relevering

Formula Expression
β_Asset = β_Equity_Peer / [1 + (1 − t_Peer) × (D_Peer / E_Peer)]

β_Project = β_Asset × [1 + (1 − t_Subject) × (D_Subject / E_Subject)]

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Strips out the comparable public peer's financial leverage to isolate unlevered business risk (β_Asset), then re-levers to the target firm's D/E and tax rate.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Pure-Play Beta Unlevering and Relevering to Estimate Division Cost of Equity

Problem & Given Scenario: Conglomerate X wants to estimate the Cost of Equity for its new Cloud Software division. Publicly traded Pure-Play Peer Z has an equity beta of β_Peer = 1.56, a Debt-to-Equity ratio of D/E = 0.40, and a tax rate of t_Peer = 25%. Conglomerate X will finance the division with a target Debt-to-Equity ratio of D/E = 0.80 at a tax rate of t_X = 25%. The risk-free rate is R_F = 4.20% and the Equity Risk Premium (ERP) is 5.50%.

Formula Expression
r_e = R_F + β_e × [E(R_M) − R_F] = R_F + (β_e × ERP)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Step 1 (Unlever Peer Beta to get Asset Beta): β_Asset = 1.56 / [1 + (1 − 0.25) × 0.40] = 1.56 / (1 + 0.30) = 1.56 / 1.30 = 1.20. Step 2 (Relever Asset Beta to Subject Target D/E = 0.80): β_Project = 1.20 × [1 + (1 − 0.25) × 0.80] = 1.20 × (1 + 0.60) = 1.92. Step 3 (CAPM Cost of Equity): r_e = 4.20% + 1.92 × 5.50% = 4.20% + 10.56% = 14.76%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Higher financial leverage (D/E = 0.80 vs 0.40) magnifies equity beta from 1.56 to 1.92, raising required equity return to 14.76%.

Worked Example 2: Comparing CAPM, Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium, and Emerging Market Country Risk Build-Up

Problem & Given Scenario: Firm M's senior 10-year bonds trade at a YTM of 6.80%, and the historical equity-over-debt risk premium for similar issuers is 4.20%. Alternatively, for its emerging-market subsidiary (β = 1.10, US R_F = 4.20%, US ERP = 5.50%), the sovereign bond spread over US Treasuries is 220 bps and the EM equity volatility ratio to EM bonds is 1.50x.

Formula Expression
β_Asset = β_Equity_Peer / [1 + (1 − t_Peer) × (D_Peer / E_Peer)]

β_Project = β_Asset × [1 + (1 − t_Subject) × (D_Subject / E_Subject)]

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Method 1 (Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium): r_e = 6.80% + 4.20% = 11.00%. Method 2 ( Sovereign Spread CAPM): Country Risk Premium (CRP) = 2.20% × 1.50 = 3.30%; EM r_e = 4.20% + 1.10 × (5.50% + 3.30%) = 4.20% + 9.68% = 13.88%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: When beta is noisy or a firm is private, the Bond-Yield-Plus-Risk-Premium method provides a fast, market-grounded check on cost of equity.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: A publicly traded comparable company has an equity beta of 1.38, a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.50, and a marginal tax rate of 30%. Its unlevered asset beta (β_Asset) is closest to:

  • [A] 0.92

  • [B] 1.02

  • [C] 1.15

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: β_Asset = β_Equity / [1 + (1 − t)(D / E)] = 1.38 / [1 + (1 − 0.30)(0.50)] = 1.38 / (1 + 0.35) = 1.38 / 1.35 = 1.0222 ≈ 1.02.

Practice Question 2

Question: In the CAPM formula r_e = R_F + β × [E(R_M) − R_F], if the risk-free rate is 4.0%, the expected return on the market portfolio E(R_M) is 9.5%, and the stock's beta is 1.40, the cost of equity is:

  • [A] 11.70%

  • [B] 13.30%

  • [C] 17.30%

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Note that E(R_M) = 9.5% is the expected market return, so the Equity Risk Premium is E(R_M) − R_F = 9.5% − 4.0% = 5.5%. Thus r_e = 4.0% + 1.40(5.5%) = 4.0% + 7.7% = 11.70%. (Option C mistakenly uses 9.5% as the ERP).

Practice Question 3

Question: When a company's debt-to-equity ratio increases (assuming debt remains default-free or carries a constant tax shield), its unlevered Asset Beta (β_Asset) and its levered Equity Beta (β_Equity) will:

  • [A] Both increase

  • [B] β_Asset remains unchanged (reflecting pure operating business risk) while β_Equity increases

  • [C] β_Asset increases while β_Equity remains unchanged

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Asset beta measures pure operating business risk and is invariant to financial leverage, whereas equity beta increases linearly with (1 − t)(D/E).

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) Cost of Equity.

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Pure-Play Beta Unlevering (Asset Beta) & Relevering.

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
