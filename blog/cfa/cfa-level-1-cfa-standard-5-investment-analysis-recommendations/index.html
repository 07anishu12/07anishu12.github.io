Introduction

CFA Standard V Investment Analysis and Recommendations covers Standard V(A) reasonable basis for independent, secondary, and quantitative model research (including dissenting from committee consensus), V(B) separating fact from opinion and disclosing model changes, and V(C) 7-year record retention in Level I Ethics and Professional Standards (LM 2 & LM 3: Standard V(A) Diligence and Reasonable Basis (secondary/third-party research, quant models, group consensus), V(B) Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients (fact vs opinion, risk/limitations), V(C) Record Retention (7-year default)), with verified formulas, two worked examples, and three practice MCQs.

Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

Standard V(A) Committee Consensus Dissent Rule (If Investment Committee Reaches a Consensus with a Reasonable and Adequate Basis => Member MAY Leave Name on Report Even If Member Personally Disagrees) and Standard V(C) Record Retention Default Period & Ownership (Retention Period = Local Regulatory Requirement OR 7 Years Default (if no regulation) ; All Records Belong to Employer) govern Standard V(A) reasonable basis for independent, secondary, and quantitative model research (including dissenting from committee consensus), V(B) separating fact from opinion and disclosing model changes, and V(C) 7-year record retention.

Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: Standard V(A) Committee Consensus Dissent Rule

Formula Expression
If Investment Committee Reaches a Consensus with a Reasonable and Adequate Basis => Member MAY Leave Name on Report Even If Member Personally Disagrees

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: As long as the group's conclusion is backed by sound diligence and a reasonable basis, an analyst does NOT have to remove their name when outvoted by an investment committee.

Formula 2: Standard V(C) Record Retention Default Period & Ownership

Formula Expression
Retention Period = Local Regulatory Requirement OR 7 Years Default (if no regulation)

All Records Belong to Employer

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Departing analysts cannot take research workpapers to reconstruct a past track record—they must recreate supporting records from public sources.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Case Study: Group Research Consensus and Third-Party Quantitative Models under Standard V(A)

Problem & Given Scenario: Case 1: Analyst Ravi sits on a 5-member credit committee. Ravi believes Issuer K should be rated 'Sell', while the other 4 members vote for 'Buy' based on a rigorous cash-flow stress test. Case 2: Portfolio Manager Elena subscribes to a third-party AI factor model and immediately trades client accounts on its signals without testing the model's assumptions, data inputs, or downside tail behavior.

Formula Expression
If Investment Committee Reaches a Consensus with a Reasonable and Adequate Basis => Member MAY Leave Name on Report Even If Member Personally Disagrees

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Case 1: Because the committee's 'Buy' rating has a reasonable and adequate basis and followed an objective process, Ravi is NOT required to remove his name from the committee report (though he may document his dissent internally). Case 2: Elena violates Standard V(A)! When using third-party research or quantitative models, members must review the soundness of assumptions, rigor of analysis, and range of scenario outcomes before relying on them.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: This committee consensus rule in Standard V(A) is one of the most frequently tested nuances on CFA Level I and Level II Ethics.

Worked Example 2: Case Study: Fact vs. Opinion and Record Retention After Changing Firms (Standard V(B) & V(C))

Problem & Given Scenario: Analyst Chen writes in a client newsletter: 'Company X's revenue grew 14% last year, and revenue will grow 20% per year for the next three years, guaranteeing a $100 share price.' Later, Chen joins a new firm and wants to reissue his 'Buy' recommendation on Company X.

Formula Expression
Retention Period = Local Regulatory Requirement OR 7 Years Default (if no regulation)

All Records Belong to Employer

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. Standard V(B) Violation: Stating a future forecast ('revenue will grow 20%... guaranteeing $100') as an absolute certainty violates V(B), which requires clearly distinguishing facts (14% past growth) from opinions/predictions (expected 20% growth).

  • Step 2. Standard V(C) Application: At his new firm, Chen cannot take his old employer's research files, nor can he re-issue the 'Buy' rating from memory alone; he must first rebuild a complete supporting research file from public sources (10-K filings, press releases) before issuing the recommendation.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Past facts are verifiable history; all future projections, price targets, and model outputs are opinions that must be framed as expectations alongside their key risks.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: An analyst is a member of an eight-person equity research committee. After a thorough, well-documented valuation debate, seven members vote to issue a 'Buy' recommendation on a stock, while the analyst believes 'Hold' is more appropriate. Under Standard V(A) Diligence and Reasonable Basis, the analyst:

  • [A] Must remove her name from the research report

  • [B] May allow her name to remain on the report without violating the Standards, because the committee's opinion has a reasonable and adequate basis

  • [C] Must resign from the committee

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: When a recommendation is made by a group/committee, a dissenting member does not need to remove their name as long as the committee's consensus opinion has a reasonable and adequate basis.

Practice Question 2

Question: In the absence of local regulatory or legal requirements specifying a different timeframe, how long does CFA Institute recommend retaining research records and supporting documentation under Standard V(C) Record Retention?

  • [A] 3 years

  • [B] 5 years

  • [C] At least 7 years

Tip: Correct Answer: Option C.

Explanation: When no regulatory mandate applies, CFA Institute recommends retaining records for at least 7 years (in physical or electronic form).

Practice Question 3

Question: Which of the following actions by a portfolio manager violates Standard V(B) Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients?

  • [A] Providing clients with a brief summary email recommendation and offering to furnish the full supporting research report upon request

  • [B] Shifting a quantitative equity fund's stock-selection model from a multi-factor value-and-momentum approach to a pure momentum model without notifying clients of the process change

  • [C] Stating that a company's historical 3-year dividend growth rate was 8.2%

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Standard V(B) requires informing clients of the basic format and general principles of the investment process and promptly disclosing any significant changes to that process.

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Standard V(A) Committee Consensus Dissent Rule.

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Standard V(C) Record Retention Default Period & Ownership.

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
