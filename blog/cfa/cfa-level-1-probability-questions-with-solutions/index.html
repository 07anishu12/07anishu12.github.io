Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

From first principles, unconditional vs conditional probabilities, addition and multiplication rules, independence testing, and probability tree expected values links theoretical valuation and risk mechanics to practical decisions in Quantitative Methods. Addition, Multiplication & Conditional Probability Rules (P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(AB) ; P(AB) = P(A|B) × P(B) ; P(A|B) = P(AB) / P(B)) establishes the primary relationship (Governs union, joint intersection, and conditional probability across dependent or independent events.), while Total Probability Rule & Expected Value (P(A) = ∑ P(A | S_i) × P(S_i) ; E(X) = ∑ P(X_i) × X_i) governs Computes unconditional probability and expected asset payoff across mutually exclusive and exhaustive economic states S_i.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Probability Tree for Macro Regime and Corporate Earnings Growth

Problem & Given Scenario: Economy has 60% probability of Expansion and 40% probability of Recession. In Expansion, Probability(EPS Growth) = 80% (with +25% stock return) and Probability(No EPS Growth) = 20% (+5% return). In Recession, Probability(EPS Growth) = 15% (+10% return) and Probability(No EPS Growth) = 85% (−18% return).

Formula Expression
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(AB)

P(AB) = P(A|B) × P(B)

P(A|B) = P(AB) / P(B)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Unconditional P(EPS Growth) = (0.60 × 0.80) + (0.40 × 0.15) = 0.48 + 0.06 = 0.54 (54.0%). Expected Return E(R|Expansion) = 0.80(25%) + 0.20(5%) = 21.0%. Expected Return E(R|Recession) = 0.15(10%) + 0.85(−18%) = −13.8%. Unconditional Expected Stock Return E(R) = 0.60(21.0%) + 0.40(−13.8%) = 12.60% − 5.52% = +7.08%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Using the total probability rule decomposes complex macro-dependent asset payoffs into clean conditional expectations.

Worked Example 2: Testing Statistical Independence and Mutually Exclusive Events

Problem & Given Scenario: In a credit universe, P(Downgrade) = 0.20, P(Dividend Cut) = 0.15, and joint probability P(Downgrade and Dividend Cut) = 0.09.

Formula Expression
P(A) = ∑ P(A | S_i) × P(S_i)

E(X) = ∑ P(X_i) × X_i

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

If independent, P(A and B) would equal 0.20 × 0.15 = 0.03. Since actual P(AB) = 0.09 ≠ 0.03, events are dependent. Conditional probability P(Downgrade | Dividend Cut) = 0.09 / 0.15 = 0.60 (60.0%). Probability of at least one event P(A or B) = 0.20 + 0.15 − 0.09 = 0.26 (26.0%).

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Knowing a firm cut its dividend triples its downgrade probability from 20% (unconditional) to 60% (conditional).

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: If two events A and B have P(A) = 0.40, P(B) = 0.50, and P(A|B) = 0.30, the probability that at least one of the events occurs, P(A or B), is closest to:

  • [A] 0.70

  • [B] 0.75

  • [C] 0.90

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: First find joint probability: P(AB) = P(A|B) × P(B) = 0.30 × 0.50 = 0.15. Then apply the addition rule: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(AB) = 0.40 + 0.50 − 0.15 = 0.75.

Practice Question 2

Question: Two non-zero probability events A and B are mutually exclusive. Which statement is most accurate?

  • [A] Events A and B are statistically independent

  • [B] Events A and B are statistically dependent with P(AB) = 0

  • [C] P(A|B) equals P(A)

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Mutually exclusive events cannot occur simultaneously, so P(AB) = 0 and P(A|B) = 0 ≠ P(A). Thus, non-zero mutually exclusive events are always extreme dependent events, never independent.

Practice Question 3

Question: A bond has a 5% probability of default over Year 1. If it survives Year 1, it has an 8% conditional probability of default in Year 2. What is the probability that the bond survives both years without defaulting?

  • [A] 87.00%

  • [B] 87.40%

  • [C] 92.00%

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: P(Survive Year 1) = 1 − 0.05 = 0.95. P(Survive Year 2 | Survive Year 1) = 1 − 0.08 = 0.92. Joint survival probability = 0.95 × 0.92 = 0.8740 = 87.40%.
