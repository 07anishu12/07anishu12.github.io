Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: High-Speed Formula Verification Drill: Session 1 (Quant, FSA, Corporate Issuers)

Problem & Given Scenario: Solve three linked Session-1 calculations in under 3 minutes: (1) Convert 8.0% semiannual APR to EAR; (2) Compute FIFO COGS given LIFO COGS = $600M, Beg LIFO Reserve = $40M, End LIFO Reserve = $55M; (3) Compute Unlevered Asset Beta given Levered Equity Beta = 1.50, D/E = 0.50, and Tax Rate = 20%.

Formula Expression
EAR = (1+r_s/m)^m − 1

Bayes P(E|I) = P(I|E)P(E)/P(I)

ROE = NI/EBT × EBT/EBIT × EBIT/Rev × Rev/A × A/E

Basic/Diluted EPS

FCFF = CFO + Int(1−t) − FCInv

WACC = w_e r_e + w_p r_p + w_d r_d(1−t)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. EAR = (1 + 0.08/2)^2 − 1 = (1.04)^2 − 1 = 8.16%.

  • Step 2. COGS_FIFO = COGS_LIFO − ΔLIFO_Reserve = $600M − ($55M − $40M) = $585M.

  • Step 3. β_Asset = 1.50 / [1 + (1 − 0.20) × 0.50] = 1.50 / 1.40 = 1.0714 ≈ 1.07.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Practicing rapid 30-second execution on core Session-1 formulas frees up time for longer cash-flow and Diluted EPS problems.

Worked Example 2: High-Speed Formula Verification Drill: Session 2 (Equity, Fixed Income, Derivatives, PM)

Problem & Given Scenario: Solve four linked Session-2 calculations: (1) Gordon Growth Price given D_0 = $2.00, ROE = 15%, Payout = 60%, r = 11%; (2) Bond %ΔPrice given ModDur = 6.0, Convexity = 50.0, Δy = −100 bps; (3) Forward Price given S_0 = $100, PV(D) = $3, R_F = 5%, T = 1; (4) Portfolio Volatility given w_1 = 0.5, σ_1 = 20%, w_2 = 0.5, σ_2 = 12%, ρ = 0.0.

Formula Expression
GGM V_0 = D_1/(r−g)

Justified P/E = (1−b)/(r−g)

%ΔP_Bond ≈ −ModDur(Δy) + 0.5×Conv×(Δy)^2

Put-Call Parity S_0 + p_0 = c_0 + PV(X)

CAPM E(R_i) = R_F + β_i[E(R_M)−R_F]

Sharpe / Treynor / M^2 / Jensen's α

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. g = 0.40 × 15% = 6.0%; D_1 = $2.00 × 1.06 = $2.12; V_0 = $2.12 / (0.11 − 0.06) = $42.40.

  • Step 2. %ΔP ≈ (−6.0 × −0.01) + 0.5(50)(−0.01)^2 = +6.00% + 0.25% = +6.25%.

  • Step 3. F_0(1) = ($100 − $3) × 1.05 = $101.85.

  • Step 4. σ_P = √[(0.25 × 400) + (0.25 × 144)] = √(100 + 36) = √136 = 11.66%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Writing out these exact equations from memory onto a blank sheet once a week during the final month guarantees instant recall on exam day.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: Which of the following formulas correctly links a company's Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF) and its Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE)?

  • [A] FCFE = FCFF + Interest × (1 − Tax Rate) − Net Borrowing

  • [B] FCFE = FCFF − Interest × (1 − Tax Rate) + Net Borrowing

  • [C] FCFE = FCFF − Capital Expenditures

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Because FCFF is pre-debt and adds back after-tax interest Int(1 − t), converting FCFF to FCFE requires subtracting after-tax interest Int(1 − t) and adding Net Borrowing.

Practice Question 2

Question: In the approximate convexity formula [ApproxConv = (PV_− + PV_+ − 2×PV_0) / ((ΔYield)^2 × PV_0)], once Approximate Convexity is computed, how is it used alongside Modified Duration to estimate the percentage price change of a bond for a yield shift of ΔYield?

  • [A] %ΔPrice ≈ (−ModDur × ΔYield) + [Convexity × (ΔYield)^2]

  • [B] %ΔPrice ≈ (−ModDur × ΔYield) + [0.5 × Convexity × (ΔYield)^2]

  • [C] %ΔPrice ≈ (ModDur × ΔYield) − [0.5 × Convexity × ΔYield]

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Always include the 0.5 (1/2) multiplier from the second-order Taylor series expansion when multiplying Convexity by (ΔYield)^2.

Practice Question 3

Question: What is the exact algebraic relationship between the Justified Price-to-Book ratio (P_0 / B_0), Return on Equity (ROE), required return on equity (r), and sustainable growth rate (g)?

  • [A] P_0 / B_0 = (r − g) / (ROE − g)

  • [B] P_0 / B_0 = (ROE − g) / (r − g)

  • [C] P_0 / B_0 = ROE / r

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Derived from V_0 = D_1/(r−g) = (B_0 × ROE × [1 − b]) / (r − g) and g = b × ROE (so ROE × [1 − b] = ROE − g), dividing by B_0 gives P_0 / B_0 = (ROE − g) / (r − g).

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Session 1 Master Equations (Quant, Econ, FSA, Corporate Issuers).

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Session 2 Master Equations (Equity, Fixed Income, Derivatives, Alts, Portfolio Mgmt).

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
