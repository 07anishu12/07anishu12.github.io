Introduction

CFA Standard III Duties to Clients: Case Studies and Questions covers Standard III(A) fiduciary duty and soft dollars, III(B) fair (not equal) dealing and IPO allocation, III(C) total-portfolio IPS suitability, III(D) performance presentation, and III(E) client confidentiality in Level I Ethics and Professional Standards (LM 2 & LM 3: Standard III(A) Loyalty, Prudence, and Care; III(B) Fair Dealing; III(C) Suitability; III(D) Performance Presentation; III(E) Preservation of Confidentiality), with verified formulas, two worked examples, and three practice MCQs.

Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

Standard III(A) Soft-Dollar Brokerage Commission Rule (Client Brokerage Commissions = Client Property -> May ONLY be used to purchase Goods/Services (Research) that Directly Assist in the Investment Decision-Making Process for Clients) and Standard III(B) Fair Dealing & Hot IPO Pro-Rata Allocation (Allocate Oversubscribed Trades Pro-Rata by Order Size Across All Eligible Accounts for Which the Security Is Suitable (Exclude Family/Personal Accounts from Hot IPOs!)) govern Standard III(A) fiduciary duty and soft dollars, III(B) fair (not equal) dealing and IPO allocation, III(C) total-portfolio IPS suitability, III(D) performance presentation, and III(E) client confidentiality.

Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: Standard III(A) Soft-Dollar Brokerage Commission Rule

Formula Expression
Client Brokerage Commissions = Client Property -> May ONLY be used to purchase Goods/Services (Research) that Directly Assist in the Investment Decision-Making Process for Clients

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Using soft dollars to pay for office rent, furniture, computer hardware, or marketing expenses is a strict violation of Standard III(A) (unless directed by the client for their own benefit).

Formula 2: Standard III(B) Fair Dealing & Hot IPO Pro-Rata Allocation

Formula Expression
Allocate Oversubscribed Trades Pro-Rata by Order Size Across All Eligible Accounts for Which the Security Is Suitable (Exclude Family/Personal Accounts from Hot IPOs!)

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Fair dealing means objective, non-discriminatory treatment—not necessarily identical treatment—and prohibits favoring high-fee or personal accounts.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Case Study: Soft Dollars, Directed Brokerage, and Pension Plan Beneficiaries under Standard III(A)

Problem & Given Scenario: Case 1: The CFO of Corporation Z instructs the pension fund manager of Corporation Z's employee retirement plan to buy 500,000 shares of Corporation Z stock to fend off a hostile takeover. Case 2: A client instructs the manager to route all trades for her account through a high-commission broker who is the client's brother ('directed brokerage').

Formula Expression
Client Brokerage Commissions = Client Property -> May ONLY be used to purchase Goods/Services (Research) that Directly Assist in the Investment Decision-Making Process for Clients

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Case 1: Under Standard III(A), the 'client' of a pension fund is the plan beneficiaries (retirees/employees), NOT the corporate executive who hired the manager! Buying stock to protect management's jobs violates III(A). Case 2: Because brokerage commissions belong to the client, if the client explicitly directs brokerage for her own account (after being informed that she may not get best price/execution), following the client's written directed brokerage instruction is PERMITTED (provided it only uses her own account's commissions).

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Always identify the true underlying beneficiary of a trust or pension plan when applying Standard III(A).

Worked Example 2: Case Study: Evaluating Derivatives Suitability on a Total-Portfolio Basis under Standard III(C)

Problem & Given Scenario: A conservative widow's IPS specifies low risk tolerance and capital preservation. Her portfolio manager buys out-of-the-money put options on the S&P 500 to hedge her existing blue-chip equity holdings against a market crash.

Formula Expression
Allocate Oversubscribed Trades Pro-Rata by Order Size Across All Eligible Accounts for Which the Security Is Suitable (Exclude Family/Personal Accounts from Hot IPOs!)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Viewed in isolation, an out-of-the-money put option is a high-risk derivative that can lose 100% of its premium. However, Standard III(C) Suitability mandates evaluating every investment in the context of the client's TOTAL PORTFOLIO! Because the put option reduces total portfolio downside risk in alignment with her conservative IPS, the trade is completely suitable.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Standard III(C) also requires updating a client's IPS at least annually AND whenever a major life or financial change occurs (marriage, inheritance, retirement).

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: An analyst changes his recommendation on a widely held technology stock from 'Hold' to 'Strong Buy.' Under Standard III(B) Fair Dealing, which action is permissible?

  • [A] Calling his three largest performance-fee hedge fund clients first before publishing the change on the firm's client portal

  • [B] Disseminating the recommendation change simultaneously to all clients via email/portal, and then following up with phone calls to clients who pay for a disclosed premium advisory tier

  • [C] Trading for the firm's proprietary desk 30 minutes prior to notifying clients

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Standard III(B) requires simultaneous dissemination of a material recommendation change to all clients; once disseminated fairly, offering different levels of follow-up service is allowed if disclosed to all clients and does not disadvantage any group.

Practice Question 2

Question: An investment manager receives an unsolicited order from a wealth management client to purchase a speculative micro-cap stock that violates the risk parameters of the client's Investment Policy Statement (IPS). If the order will have a material impact on the portfolio's risk profile, the manager should:

  • [A] Execute the trade immediately without discussion

  • [B] Discuss the unsuitability with the client and ask to update the IPS; if the client refuses to update the IPS but insists on the material unsuitable trade, the manager may need to refuse the trade or terminate the relationship per firm policy

  • [C] Secretly buy a Treasury bond instead

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: For an unsolicited trade that materially alters the portfolio's risk outside the IPS, the manager must inform the client of the unsuitability and seek a formal IPS update before executing (if immaterial, a written acknowledgment suffices).

Practice Question 3

Question: Under Standard III(E) Preservation of Confidentiality, a member may disclose confidential information about a current or former client without the client's consent ONLY when:

  • [A] A fellow CFA charterholder at another firm asks for the information

  • [B] The information concerns illegal activities by the client (where disclosure is permitted/required by law) OR is requested by the CFA Institute Professional Conduct Program during an investigation

  • [C] A charitable hospital board asks for the net worth of prospective donors

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Client confidentiality applies to current, former, and prospective clients and may be breached without client consent only when required by law/illegal activity or for a CFA Institute Professional Conduct investigation.

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Standard III(A) Soft-Dollar Brokerage Commission Rule.

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Standard III(B) Fair Dealing & Hot IPO Pro-Rata Allocation.

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
