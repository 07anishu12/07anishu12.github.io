Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Calculating Score Sensitivity and Topic Weight Allocation on CFA Level II

Problem & Given Scenario: With 80 scored questions (20 scored item sets × 4 questions) on the CFA Level II exam, each individual multiple-choice question is worth 1 / 80 = 1.25% of your total exam score (2.25x the weight of a single Level I question!), and a single 4-question vignette is worth 5.0% of the entire exam!

Formula Expression
Total Item Sets = 22 Vignettes (11 per Session × 4 MCQs = 88 Total Questions)

Session Time = 132 Minutes

Time per Vignette = 132 / 11 = 12.0 Minutes (3.0 Minutes per Question)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. Big Five Allocation (Ethics, FSA, Equity, Fixed Income, Portfolio Management at 10%–15% each): 2 to 3 item sets (8 to 12 questions) per topic -> Together, mastering the Big Five secures ~52 to 60 of the 88 questions.

  • Step 2. Tactical Five Allocation (QM, Econ, CI, Derivatives, Alt at 5%–10% each): 1 to 2 item sets (4 to 8 questions) per topic -> Because a 5% topic may be tested by just ONE 4-question vignette on exam day, skipping a sub-module (like Currency Swaps or Time Series) risks losing 5% of your entire exam score if that module is the one selected!

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: At Level II, low-weight topics (5%–10%) have high 'sampling variance risk': if Derivatives is tested by a single 4-question item set on Black-Scholes and Swaps, all 4 questions come from that one case study.

Worked Example 2: The 12-Minute Vignette Extraction Workflow (Question-First vs. Vignette-First)

Problem & Given Scenario: Demonstrate how to solve a 4-question Level II Equity Valuation or FSA vignette in 10 minutes with 2 minutes to spare.

Formula Expression
'Big Five' (10%–15% each: Ethics, FSA, Equity, Fixed Income, Portfolio Mgmt) ≈ 2 to 3 Vignettes each

'Tactical Five' (5%–10% each: Quant, Econ, Corp Issuers, Derivatives, Alternatives) ≈ 1 to 2 Vignettes each

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step Minute 0–1: Read Question Stem 1 BEFORE reading the vignette prose. Note the exact exhibit or paragraph referenced (e.g., 'Based on Exhibit 2 and the H-model...'). Minute 1–3: Scan directly to Exhibit 2 and the surrounding paragraph, extract D_0, g_S, g_L, H, and r, and compute Answer

  • Step 1. Minute 3–9: Repeat sequentially for Questions 2, 3, and 4 (which follow the vignette's paragraphs and exhibits in chronological order!). Minute 9–12: Verify that each question's standalone assumptions (e.g., 'Suppose instead that the tax rate changes to 20% in Question 3...') were NOT accidentally carried over into Question 4!

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Crucial Level II rule: Every question within a 4-question vignette is independent unless explicitly stated otherwise—never carry a hypothetical 'suppose instead' assumption from Question 2 into Question 3!

Practice Question 1

Question: On the computer-based CFA Level II examination, how many item sets (vignettes) and total multiple-choice questions are presented across the two 132-minute sessions?

  • [A] 20 item sets with 6 questions each (120 questions)

  • [B] 22 item sets (11 per session) with 4 multiple-choice questions each, totaling 88 questions (of which 20 item sets / 80 questions are scored and 2 item sets / 8 questions are unscored trial items)

  • [C] 30 item sets with 3 questions each (90 questions)

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Every CFA Level II item set contains exactly 4 multiple-choice questions, with 11 item sets (44 questions) in Session 1 and 11 item sets (44 questions) in Session 2.

Practice Question 3

Question: When Question 2 of a Level II vignette states, 'Assume for this question only that Company X's marginal tax rate increases from 25% to 30%,' which tax rate should the candidate use when solving Question 3 of the same vignette?

  • [A] The 30% tax rate from Question 2

  • [B] The original 25% tax rate stated in the main vignette text, because conditional assumptions introduced inside a single question stem apply only to that specific question

  • [C] The average of 25% and 30%

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Hypothetical changes introduced inside a question stem ('Suppose instead...') apply strictly to that question alone; always revert to the main vignette facts for subsequent questions.
