Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: High-Speed Level II Valuation Drill: H-Model, Residual Income Continuing Value, and Private DLOC/DLOM

Problem & Given Scenario: Solve three Level II valuation problems rapidly: (1) H-Model value given D_0 = $1.00, g_S = 20%, g_L = 4%, 12-year linear transition (so H = 6), r = 9%; (2) PV at t = 0 of Continuing Residual Income starting in Year 4 given RI_4 = $3.00, persistence factor ω = 0.60, r = 10%; (3) Combined discount given Control Premium = 20% and DLOM = 15%.

Formula Expression
H-Model: V_0 = [D_0(1+g_L) + D_0 H(g_S−g_L)]/(r−g_L)

RI: V_0 = B_0 + ∑ [(ROE_t−r)B_{t−1}/(1+r)^t]

Beneish M-Score > −1.78

Grinold-Kroner E(R) = (D/P − %ΔS) + (i + g_real) + %ΔP/E

REIT NAVPS = (NOI_1/CapRate + Cash − Debt)/Shares

VC f = I / V_Post

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. H = 12 / 2 = 6; V_0 = [$1.00(1.04) + $1.00(6)(0.20 − 0.04)] / (0.09 − 0.04) = ($1.04 + $0.96) / 0.05 = $2.00 / 0.05 = $40.00.

  • Step 2. Continuing Value at t = 3 (one period before RI_4!) = RI_4 / (1 + r − ω) = $3.00 / (1 + 0.10 − 0.60) = $3.00 / 0.50 = $6.00; Discount to t = 0: $6.00 / (1.10)^3 = $4.51.

  • Step 3. DLOC = 1 − (1 / 1.20) = 16.67%; Combined Discount = 1 − [(1 − 0.1667)(1 − 0.15)] = 1 − (0.8333 × 0.85) = 1 − 0.7083 = 29.17%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Mastering the exact time-index anchors (like CV_{T−1} = RI_T / (1 + r − ω) anchored at T − 1) eliminates the most common errors in Level II valuation vignettes.

Worked Example 2: High-Speed Level II Quantitative, Fixed Income & Derivatives Drill

Problem & Given Scenario: Solve four Level II quantitative pricing problems: (1) AR(1) mean-reverting level for x_t = 0.02 + 0.75 x_{t−1}; (2) Annualized quarterly Par Swap Rate given P_1 = 0.99, P_2 = 0.98, P_3 = 0.97, P_4 = 0.96; (3) CDS Upfront % on $10M High-Yield protection (500 bps coupon) when CDS spread = 350 bps and Dur = 4.0; (4) Combined Sharpe Ratio given Benchmark SR_B = 0.30 and Active IR = 0.40 at optimal active risk.

Formula Expression
Binomial Rate: r_u = r_d e^(2σ)

CVA = ∑ PV(EAD×LGD×PD)

CDS Upfront ≈ (Spread − Coupon)×Dur

Par Swap s = (1−P_N)/∑P_i

BSM c_0 = S_0 e^(−δT)N(d_1) − X e^(−rT)N(d_2)

Fundamental Law IR = TC × IC × √BR

SR_P^2 = SR_B^2 + IR^2

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

  • Step 1. Mean-Reverting Level = 0.02 / (1 − 0.75) = 0.08 (8.0%).

  • Step 2. ∑ P_i = 3.90; Periodic rate = (1 − 0.96) / 3.90 = 0.04 / 3.90 = 1.0256%; Annualized Swap Rate = 4 × 1.0256% = 4.10%.

  • Step 3. Upfront % ≈ (3.50% − 5.00%) × 4.0 = −6.00% (Protection Seller pays 6.0% = $600,000 to Protection Buyer; CDS Price = 106.00).

  • Step 4. SR_P = √(0.30^2 + 0.40^2) = √(0.09 + 0.16) = √0.25 = 0.50.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: That 3-4-5 Pythagorean triangle (SR_B = 0.30, IR = 0.40 => SR_P = 0.50) is a classic CFA Level II Portfolio Management exam favorite!

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: In the CFA Level II Residual Income model, if an analyst assumes that starting in Year T, a company's residual income will decay gradually over time toward zero with a persistence factor ω (where 0 < ω < 1), the present value as of time (T − 1) of all residual income from Year T onward is:

  • [A] RI_T / (r − ω)

  • [B] RI_T / (1 + r − ω)

  • [C] [RI_T × (1 + ω)] / (r − ω)

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: The continuing value of residual income at time T − 1 with persistence factor ω ∈ [0, 1] is RI_T / (1 + r − ω).

Practice Question 2

Question: In the Fundamental Law of Active Management, if a benchmark portfolio has a Sharpe ratio of SR_B = 0.50 and an active portfolio has an Information Ratio of IR = 0.50, what is the maximum achievable Sharpe ratio of the combined portfolio when active risk is set to its optimal level [σ_A* = (IR / SR_B) × σ_B]?

  • [A] 0.500

  • [B] 0.707

  • [C] 1.000

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: SR_P = √(SR_B^2 + IR^2) = √(0.50^2 + 0.50^2) = √(0.25 + 0.25) = √0.50 = 0.7071 ≈ 0.707.

Practice Question 3

Question: When calculating the value of an existing (seasoned) Receive-Fixed, Pay-Floating interest rate swap on a reset date using current zero-coupon discount factors (P_1, P_2, ..., P_N), if the original fixed swap rate (s_0) is higher than the current market par swap rate (s_t), the value of the swap to the Receive-Fixed party is:

  • [A] Positive, equal to [(s_0 − s_t) / m] × (∑ P_i) × Notional

  • [B] Negative, equal to [(s_t − s_0) / m] × (∑ P_i) × Notional

  • [C] Zero

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Receiving a locked-in fixed coupon s_0 that is higher than the current market fixed swap rate s_t produces a positive present value annuity equal to the periodic coupon difference [(s_0 − s_t)/m] multiplied by the sum of discount factors ∑ P_i and Notional.

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Level II Equity, FSA, Corporate & Alts Valuation Equations.

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
