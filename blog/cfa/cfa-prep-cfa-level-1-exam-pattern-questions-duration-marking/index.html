Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: CFA Level I Time Allocation & Pacing Equation

Formula Expression
Total Questions = 180 MCQs (90 in Session 1 + 90 in Session 2)

Total Exam Time = 270 Minutes (135 Min/Session)

Average Time per Question = 135 × 60 / 90 = 90 Seconds (1.5 Minutes)

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Of the 180 questions, 160 are scored operational items and 20 are unscored trial (pretest) items distributed indistinguishably across both sessions.

Formula 2: Expected Score Under Zero Negative Marking (3-Option A, B, C Format)

Formula Expression
Penalty for Wrong Answer = 0 Points

Expected Value of Pure Random Guess = (1 / 3) × (+1) + (2 / 3) × (0) = +0.333 Points

Expected Value after Eliminating 1 Option = +0.500 Points

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Because CFA Institute deducts zero marks for incorrect answers, leaving any question blank on exam day is a mathematical error.

Worked Example 1: Session-by-Session Pacing Budget and Topic Weight Allocation (2026 & 2027 Syllabus)

Problem & Given Scenario: Map out a candidate's 135-minute Session 1 and 135-minute Session 2 time budget and expected question counts across the 10 CFA Level I subjects.

Formula Expression
Total Questions = 180 MCQs (90 in Session 1 + 90 in Session 2)

Total Exam Time = 270 Minutes (135 Min/Session)

Average Time per Question = 135 × 60 / 90 = 90 Seconds (1.5 Minutes)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Session 1 (135 minutes, 90 questions): Ethical & Professional Standards (15%–20%, ~27–36 questions; 10%–15% in 2027), Quantitative Methods (6%–9%, ~11–16 questions; 11%–14% in 2027), Economics (6%–9%, ~11–16 questions), Financial Statement Analysis (11%–14%, ~20–25 questions), Corporate Issuers (6%–9%, ~11–16 questions). Session 2 (135 minutes, 90 questions): Equity Investments (11%–14%, ~20–25 questions), Fixed Income (11%–14%, ~20–25 questions), Derivatives (5%–8%, ~9–14 questions), Alternative Investments (7%–10%, ~13–18 questions), Portfolio Management (8%–12%, ~14–22 questions). Target completing all 90 questions by Minute 115 (75 seconds/question) to leave a 20-minute review buffer for flagged calculation items.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Completing your first pass in 115 minutes leaves 20 minutes per session to double-check flagged TVM, FSA, and Fixed Income calculations.

Worked Example 2: Expected Score Gain from Strategic Elimination vs. Blank Responses

Problem & Given Scenario: A candidate is 100% confident on 140 of the 180 questions (getting 85% = 119 right). On the remaining 40 difficult questions, the candidate eliminates 1 clearly wrong option out of {A, B, C} on each question and guesses between the remaining 2 options.

Formula Expression
Penalty for Wrong Answer = 0 Points

Expected Value of Pure Random Guess = (1 / 3) × (+1) + (2 / 3) × (0) = +0.333 Points

Expected Value after Eliminating 1 Option = +0.500 Points

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Expected additional correct answers from 40 two-option educated guesses = 40 × (1 / 2) = +20.0 questions (vs +13.3 for pure 3-option guesses and +0.0 if left blank). Total Expected Raw Score = (119 + 20) / 180 = 139 / 180 = 77.22% (comfortably above historical Minimum Passing Scores of ~65%–70%).

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Eliminating just one distractor raises your probability of a correct answer from 33.3% to 50.0%, adding +6.7 raw score points across 40 tough questions.

Practice Question 2

Question: What happens if a CFA Level I candidate passes the computer-based exam above the Minimum Passing Score (MPS) but fails to complete the required Practical Skills Module (PSM) before the result release deadline?

  • [A] The candidate receives a 5% score deduction

  • [B] The candidate's exam result is withheld/voided and is not released until the Practical Skills Module requirement is satisfied

  • [C] The candidate is exempted from the PSM

Explanation: Completing one Practical Skills Module (Financial Modeling or Python Programming Fundamentals at Level I) is a mandatory condition for receiving your official exam result.

Practice Question 3

Question: How are the Minimum Passing Score (MPS) and negative marking determined on the CFA Level I examination?

  • [A] There is a −0.25 point penalty for wrong answers and a fixed 70% cutoff

  • [C] Only the top 40% of candidates pass regardless of exam difficulty

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: CFA Institute applies zero negative marking and uses the modified Angoff method (coupled with psychometric equating across exam forms) to set the competency-based Minimum Passing Score.
