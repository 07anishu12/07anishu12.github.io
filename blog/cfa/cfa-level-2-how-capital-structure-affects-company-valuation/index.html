Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

Note: The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

Formula 1: Grinold-Kroner Forward-Looking Equity Return & ERP Model

Formula Expression
E(R_Equity) = [D_1 / P_0 − %ΔShares] + [E(Inflation) + E(Real_EPS_Growth)] + E(%ΔP/E)

ERP = E(R_Equity) − R_F

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: Combines Total Cash Yield (Dividend Yield + Net Buyback Yield = D/P − %ΔS), Nominal Earnings Growth (Inflation + Real Growth), and Repricing (%ΔP/E).

Formula 2: Static Trade-Off Firm Valuation & Extended Hamada Beta (with Debt Beta β_d)

Formula Expression
V_L = V_U + (t × D) − PV(Costs of Financial Distress)

β_e = β_a + (β_a − β_d) × (1 − t) × (D / E)

Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown: When corporate debt is risky (β_d > 0), debt holders bear part of the firm's systematic operating risk, reducing the levered equity beta β_e slightly below the zero-debt-beta Hamada formula.

Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Calculating the Equity Risk Premium Using the Grinold-Kroner Model (Watch the Sign on %ΔShares!)

Problem & Given Scenario: An equity strategist estimates for an index: Expected Forward Dividend Yield (D_1/P_0) = 2.10%, Expected Annual Net Share Change (%ΔS) = −1.40% (net share repurchases of 1.40%), Expected Inflation = 2.30%, Expected Real Earnings Growth = 2.80%, Expected Annual P/E Change (%ΔP/E) = −0.60%, and 10-year Government Bond Yield = 4.10%.

Formula Expression
E(R_Equity) = [D_1 / P_0 − %ΔShares] + [E(Inflation) + E(Real_EPS_Growth)] + E(%ΔP/E)

ERP = E(R_Equity) − R_F

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Step 1 (Total Income / Cash Yield = D/P − %ΔS): Because %ΔS = −1.40% (buybacks shrink share count!), Income Return = 2.10% − (−1.40%) = +3.50%. Step 2 (Nominal Earnings Growth): 2.30% + 2.80% = +5.10%. Step 3 (Repricing Return): −0.60%. Step 4 (Expected Equity Return): E(R_M) = 3.50% + 5.10% − 0.60% = 8.00%. Step 5 (Grinold-Kroner ERP): ERP = 8.00% − 4.10% = 3.90%.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Watch the minus sign on %ΔS in Grinold-Kroner: net share repurchases mean %ΔS is NEGATIVE (−1.40%), which INCREASES investor return (−(−1.40%) = +1.40%); net share issuance (+%ΔS) dilutes and reduces investor return!

Worked Example 2: Optimal Capital Structure Under Static Trade-Off Theory

Problem & Given Scenario: An unlevered firm has constant EBIT = $120M, tax rate t = 25%, and unlevered cost of capital r_0 = 9.0%. It considers issuing $300M of permanent debt, which incurs an estimated $35M present value of expected financial distress costs. Compute V_U, V_L under pure MM with taxes, and V_L under Static Trade-Off Theory.

Formula Expression
V_L = V_U + (t × D) − PV(Costs of Financial Distress)

β_e = β_a + (β_a − β_d) × (1 − t) × (D / E)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

Step 1 (Unlevered Value V_U): V_U = [EBIT × (1 − t)] / r_0 = [$120M × 0.75] / 0.09 = $90M / 0.09 = $1,000.0M. Step 2 (MM Value with Taxes): V_L(MM) = V_U + (t × D) = $1,000.0M + (0.25 × $300M) = $1,075.0M. Step 3 (Static Trade-Off Value): V_L(Trade-Off) = $1,075.0M − $35.0M (PV Distress) = $1,040.0M.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Because the $75M tax shield exceeds the $35M distress cost, the recapitalization adds +$40M to total firm value.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: Using the Grinold-Kroner model, an analyst estimates for an equity market: dividend yield = 1.8%, net share issuances (%ΔS) = +0.5% per year, expected inflation = 2.5%, expected real earnings growth = 3.0%, expected P/E expansion = +0.4% per year, and the 10-year Treasury yield = 4.2%. The implied Equity Risk Premium (ERP) is:

  • [A] 3.0%

  • [B] 4.0%

  • [C] 7.2%

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: Expected Equity Return = (1.8% − 0.5%) + (2.5% + 3.0%) + 0.4% = 1.3% + 5.5% + 0.4% = 7.2%. Subtract the 4.2% risk-free yield: ERP = 7.2% − 4.2% = 3.0%. (Notice that positive net share issuance +0.5% is subtracted because it dilutes existing shareholders!).

Practice Question 2

Question: An unlevered company has a market value of $600 million. If the company issues $200 million of permanent debt under a 25% corporate tax rate and incurs $18 million in present value of financial distress costs, its levered firm value under the Static Trade-Off Theory is:

  • [A] $632 million

  • [B] $650 million

  • [C] $668 million

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: V_L = V_U + (t × D) − PV(Financial Distress) = $600M + (0.25 × $200M) − $18M = $600M + $50M − $18M = $632 million.

Practice Question 3

Question: Using the extended Hamada equation that accounts for risky debt beta [β_e = β_a + (β_a − β_d)(1 − t)(D/E)], if a company's asset beta is β_a = 1.00, its debt beta is β_d = 0.20, its tax rate is 25%, and its D/E ratio is 1.00, its levered equity beta is:

  • [A] 1.60

  • [B] 1.75

  • [C] 1.80

Tip: Correct Answer: Option A.

Explanation: β_e = 1.00 + (1.00 − 0.20) × (1 − 0.25) × 1.00 = 1.00 + (0.80 × 0.75) = 1.00 + 0.60 = 1.60. (Ignoring debt beta gives 1.75).

Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Grinold-Kroner Forward-Looking Equity Return & ERP Model.

  • Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Static Trade-Off Firm Valuation & Extended Hamada Beta (with Debt Beta β_d).

  • Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.
