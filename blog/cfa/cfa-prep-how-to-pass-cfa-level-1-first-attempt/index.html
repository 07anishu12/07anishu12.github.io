Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

Worked Example 1: Comparing Two Candidates with Identical 310 Study Hours (Why Candidate A Passed in the 90th Percentile and Candidate B Failed)

Problem & Given Scenario: Candidate A and Candidate B both log 310 study hours over 5 months. Candidate A reads each module once (120 hrs), immediately solves all CFA Institute End-of-Chapter (EOC) and LES Q-Bank questions (130 hrs, logging every missed formula in an Error Notebook), runs weekly cumulative 30-question quizzes, and takes 5 full timed mocks in the final month (60 hrs). Candidate B watches 160 hours of video lectures, highlights notes in 3 colors for 110 hours, skips EOC questions until 10 days before the exam, and takes 1 untimed mock.

Formula Expression
Phase 1: Quant -> FSA -> Fixed Income -> Equity -> Corporate Issuers

Phase 2: Derivatives -> Alternatives -> Economics -> Portfolio Management -> Ethics (Read Twice: Early & Final 2 Weeks!)

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

On exam day, Candidate A has solved 2,800+ CFA-style questions under timed pressure and recognizes every calculation trap in <60 seconds (passing above the 90th percentile). Candidate B understands concepts vaguely when reading them, but freezes when asked to compute Diluted EPS or Macaulay Duration from scratch in 90 seconds.

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Passing CFA Level I on the first attempt is driven by question volume and error-log remediation—not passive reading or highlighting.

Worked Example 2: Leveraging the Heavy Session-1 Ethics Weight (15%–20%)

Problem & Given Scenario: Demonstrate how improving your Ethics score from 55% to 80% impacts your overall 180-question score margin.

Formula Expression
Every 3rd Saturday = Cumulative 50-Question Mixed Q-Bank Quiz on All Previously Completed Topics

Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:

At an 18% weight (32 questions), scoring 80% (25.6 right) vs. 55% (17.6 right) adds +8.0 raw points (+4.44% to your entire overall exam score!). That +8-question swing is equivalent to going from 50% to 100% across the entire Derivatives or Economics section!

Tip: Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway: Because Ethics is 15%–20% of the exam and tests the exact same 7 Standards every window, reading the official CFA Institute Standards of Practice Handbook examples in the final 2 weeks offers the highest return per study hour of any topic.

CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

Practice Question 1

Question: Why is Quantitative Methods universally recommended as the FIRST subject to study when beginning CFA Level I preparation?

  • [A] Because Quantitative Methods has the highest exam weight

  • [B] Because Time Value of Money (TVM), probability, and statistics are prerequisite building blocks used directly inside Fixed Income bond pricing, Equity DCF valuation, Corporate Issuers capital budgeting, Derivatives, and Portfolio Management

  • [C] Because Quantitative Methods has no formulas

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Mastering TVM, EAR, discounting, and standard deviation/covariance in Week 1 makes Fixed Income, Equity, Corporate Issuers, and Portfolio Management much faster to learn.

Practice Question 2

Question: What is the single most effective cure for the 'forgetting curve' (forgetting early subjects like Quant and FSA while studying later subjects 3 months later)?

  • [A] Waiting until the night before the exam to review Quant and FSA

  • [B] Scheduling a weekly or bi-weekly cumulative mixed Q-Bank quiz (30–50 questions) covering all previously completed subjects throughout the study period

  • [C] Re-reading the entire textbook three times

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Interleaved spaced repetition—testing yourself on earlier topics every 1–2 weeks—forces active memory retrieval and locks formulas into long-term memory.

Practice Question 3

Question: When solving practice questions in the CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem (LES), what should a candidate do immediately after getting a question wrong or guessing a question right?

  • [A] Move on immediately to maximize question count

  • [B] Read the full solution explanation for all three options (A, B, and C), write the missed concept or formula in a dedicated Error Log, and re-solve a similar problem within 48 hours

  • [C] Delete the question from the Q-Bank

Tip: Correct Answer: Option B.

Explanation: Notice that lucky guesses must be treated just like wrong answers during review: logging every missed or guessed concept in an Error Notebook turns weaknesses into guaranteed points.
