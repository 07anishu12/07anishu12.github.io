Professional Trajectory

Research rigor meets production product execution.

Aniket's technical foundation began in statistical computing and data analytics at the Delhi Skill and Entrepreneurship University (DSEU), where he graduated with a 3.82 / 4.0 GPA, ranked 2nd across the cohort. In 2025, he joined the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) in Japan as an AI Research Intern, developing Graph Neural Network (GraphSAGE, GAT) architectures to model non-Euclidean ecological trophic dynamics and orchestrating multi-LLM data synthesis pipelines.

As an Associate Product Manager at Drivio Technologies, Aniket owns loan origination journeys, LMS workflows, and banking API integrations for KYC, disbursement, and repayment across a two-wheeler vehicle marketplace. He is also currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University, studying continuous-time finance, stochastic calculus, derivative pricing, and algorithmic credit risk modeling.
