I work at the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, product ownership, and financial engineering. My focus is turning theoretical machine learning capabilities into robust, scalable systems that organizations and customers rely upon daily.

Current Focus

End-to-end fintech systems and structured LLM workflows.

At Drivio Technologies, I serve as an Associate Product Manager owning the loan journey ecosystem for a high-volume two-wheeler marketplace. This includes integrating core banking partner APIs for KYC verification, automated loan disbursement, and multi-mode repayment inside the Loan Management System (LMS).

Simultaneously, I design and fine-tune LLM content-generation agents with deterministic output validation schemas, ensuring that AI-driven automation delivers high throughput without sacrificing brand compliance or analytical accuracy.

The Trajectory

From statistical analytics to graph machine learning in Japan.

My analytical foundation took root during my B.Sc. in Data Analytics at DSEU Shakarpur-2 Campus in New Delhi, where I graduated with a 3.82 / 4.0 GPA, ranked 2nd across the program.

In 2025, I joined the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) in Japan as an AI Research Intern. Working in computational ecology, I developed Graph Neural Network (GNN) architectures to model topological trophic interactions and energy-flow mechanics within intricate food webs. To overcome unstructured literature bottlenecks, I engineered semantic synthesis pipelines leveraging multi-LLM ensembles across Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic, and OpenAI.

Now pursuing an M.Sc. in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University, I am deepening my quantitative foundation across derivative pricing, stochastic processes, algorithmic risk systems, and fintech mechanics.
