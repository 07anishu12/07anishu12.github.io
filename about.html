ANIKET THAKURAI × FINANCE × PRODUCT

The arc

From Rank 2 in New Delhi to AI research in Japan.

My path started with data analytics at DSEU Shakarpur-2, where I graduated with a 3.82/4.0 GPA and Rank 2. It moved to Okinawa, Japan, as an AI Research Intern at OIST, where I developed GNN algorithms to analyze ecological food webs and energy-flow dynamics.

At OIST, I also engineered data pipelines using multi-LLM ensembles across Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic, and OpenAI to synthesize semantic representations. That work made the research question concrete: how do you turn complicated data into a system people can reason with?

The return

Now building AI for Indian finance.

Back in India, I have worked across the used-car and two-wheeler finance space at Drivio Technologies. As a Business Analyst, I led AI implementation for a used-car finance system, using LLM automation to accelerate loan processing and credit decisions. As an Associate Product Manager, I own end-to-end loan journeys for a two-wheeler marketplace and integrate core banking APIs for KYC, disbursement, and repayment in the LMS.

Alongside the product work, I build LLM content-generation agents with structured output validation — improving latency and consistency without losing sight of the people who use the workflow.

Applied AI engineer who ships product — and a PM who can actually build the model.
