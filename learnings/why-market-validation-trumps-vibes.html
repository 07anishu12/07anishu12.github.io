Why Market Validation Trumps Vibes: A Three-Step Query Framework

Stop writing production software before knowing whether active search demand exists. A practical framework to validate buyer intent before committing capital.

Aniket Thakur · Sep 12, 2026 · 3 min read
Product Management Idea Validation Market Research Startups
Originally shared on LinkedIn View original post on LinkedIn ↗

The Expensive Illusion of Building in the Dark

The most expensive engineering mistake a product builder or founder can make is building a complete software system before confirming that a clear, urgent demand exists. Shipping to complete silence is not bad luck; it is a predictable consequence of prioritizing execution velocity over customer demand verification.

Market research does not require expensive multi-week consulting reports. It requires answering three foundational questions:

  1. Demand: Are prospective buyers actively formulating queries for this exact outcome?
  2. Supply: What solutions currently occupy the top organic search rankings?
  3. Saturation: Is the category dominated by entrenched incumbents, or fragmented by underserved edge cases?

The Three-Step Autocomplete Validation Query

Search marketplaces and Google Autocomplete reveal unfiltered consumer behavior. By analyzing search string completion and trend direction, you extract real demand signal from vanity noise:

Act as a rigorous market research analyst. Analyze this product opportunity: [Product / Problem Space].


  1. Identify 5 high-intent autocomplete search queries customers use when seeking this outcome.
  2. Segment 3 underserved niche variations facing minimal established competition.
  3. Evaluate whether search interest across these variations is expanding or stagnating over the trailing 12 months.

Filtering for Buying Intent vs. Browsing Curiosity

Informational Queries: "What is automated invoicing?"* (Zero commercial urgency).
Commercial Consideration: "Best automated invoicing for freelancers in India"* (High qualification).
Transactional Urgency: "Invoicing software integrating ICICI bank payout API"* (Immediate buyer intent).

Focusing on tight, niched problem spaces dramatically reduces customer acquisition costs. Evidence consistently outperforms intuition.
