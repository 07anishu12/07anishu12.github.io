Academic Research · OIST Japan

Graph Neural Networks for ecological food-web modelling.

At the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST), my research focused on computational modeling of complex ecological networks. Food webs describe intricate trophic dependencies and energy-flow dynamics across species communities.

By developing inductive Graph Neural Network algorithms (incorporating GraphSAGE and Graph Attention Networks), we modeled non-linear trophic interaction probabilities, capturing topological centrality and community structures that traditional pairwise models overlooked.

Alongside structural GNN modeling, I engineered semantic synthesis pipelines leveraging multi-LLM ensembles (Gemini, DeepSeek, Anthropic, and OpenAI) to standardize, cross-verify, and ingest ecological literature into structured topological datasets.

Methodologies & Stack

GraphSAGE Graph Attention (GAT) NetworkX Centrality Analysis PyTorch Geometric Multi-LLM Pipeline Energy Dynamics

Research conducted at OIST Computational Ecology Unit, Okinawa, Japan (June 2025 – Feb 2026).
