Privacy notice

A short, plain-language explanation.

This notice covers the optional contact and bike-enquiry forms on this portfolio. It does not replace the terms of any external service you choose to open, including WhatsApp.

What you can submit

A lead form may ask for your name, Indian PIN code, mobile number, bike brand preference, and consent to be contacted. The general contact form may ask for your name, email address, and message. Do not submit identity documents, financial account numbers, passwords, or other sensitive material.

Why it is used

The information is used only to respond to the enquiry and understand which page or campaign prompted it. The submission records the source page, approved campaign attribution, consent policy version, and a server-generated timestamp. A PIN code is a delivery-area identifier, not proof of your current location.

Where it goes

When the lead service is configured, it saves your enquiry in a private PostgreSQL database. It queues a copy for a private Google Sheets register and an owner notification through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Integration failures do not delete saved enquiries. These providers process the details needed for follow-up under their own service terms. General contact messages use the configured email service.

Retention and deletion

Enquiries are reviewed for deletion after 90 days unless an active enquiry or applicable obligation requires longer retention. You may request access, correction, withdrawal of consent, or deletion by email below. Deletion includes the database and spreadsheet; provider messages and backups may have separate retention periods.

Your choices

Submission is optional. You can use the public email, phone, or WhatsApp links instead. WhatsApp opens an external service and does not send a message until you review and press Send there.

Questions

For a privacy question about an enquiry, contact kumaraniketn@gmail.com. Consent policy version: 2026-10-25. This notice was last reviewed on October 25, 2026.
