## Battery Capacity (kWh), Charging Time and Wall-to-Battery Efficiency

A crucial distinction in EV ownership is energy stored in the battery versus energy drawn from your home electricity meter. Due to charger inverter heat and BMS cell balancing, charging a 3.7 kWh battery from 0% to 100% draws approximately 4.15 kWh from the wall socket (~13% overhead). Covering 110 km per charge equals 3.36 kWh/100 km from the battery, or 3.77 kWh/100 km at the wall plug.
