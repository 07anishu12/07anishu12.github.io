## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Does a valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate allow me to drive a 10-year-old diesel car in Delhi NCR?

No. A valid PUC certificate is mandatory for all active vehicles, but it does NOT override the NGT/Supreme Court 10-year diesel and 15-year petrol End-of-Life Vehicle (ELV) ban, nor does it exempt a BS4 diesel car during GRAP Stage-III restrictions.

### Q2. What is a 'Certificate of Deposit' (CoD) if an old car is scrapped at a Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility (RVSF)?

When an End-of-Life Vehicle is scrapped at a government-authorised RVSF, the owner receives scrap metal value (~₹25,000–₹45,000) plus a tradable digital Certificate of Deposit (CoD) that grants motor-vehicle tax rebates (up to 15%–25% depending on state rules) and registration fee waivers when buying a new car.

### Q3. Can I keep a 10-year-old diesel car parked on the street outside my house in Delhi?

No. Under Delhi Government ELV enforcement rules upheld by the High Court, public streets and municipal parking spaces are public places; End-of-Life Vehicles parked on public streets are subject to towing and scrapping unless parked strictly within private gated premises or moved out of NCR via NOC.

### Q4. Are Electric Vehicles (EVs) or Strong Hybrids affected by the 10-year diesel rule?

Electric Vehicles face no tailpipe emissions bans, and Petrol-Strong Hybrids (like the Innova Hycross, Invicto, Grand Vitara, Hyryder, and City e:HEV) are registered as Petrol-Hybrid vehicles—enjoying the full 15-year registration lifespan in Delhi NCR with zero BS4 GRAP restrictions.

### Q5. Where should I verify the latest official NCR vehicle rules before paying for a used car?

Because air-quality directives and NOC relaxation windows can be updated by the courts, Delhi Transport Department, or the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM), always verify the vehicle's live status on `parivahan.gov.in` (mParivahan app) and check current directives on `transport.delhi.gov.in` and `caqm.nic.in` on the day of purchase.
