Daily livability depends on where you park and what you carry. The Bajaj Chetak highlights Pressed steel unibody shell, IP67 water resistance, onboard charger option, and 35-litre boot on the 3.5 kWh 35 Series, offering 21 litres (29/32 Series) / 35 litres (35 Series floorboard battery) of under-seat storage and a charging time of 3 hr 00 min (0–80% on 3501 with 950W onboard charger) / ~4 hr 30 min (standard trims).

Meanwhile, the Ather Rizta counters with 900mm longest family seat in segment, 34-litre under-seat storage, SkidControl traction control, Magic Twist regen, and Zip/SmartEco ride modes, providing 34 litres under-seat + optional 22-litre Frunk (56L total) of storage and charging in 5 hr 45 min (0–80% on 2.9 kWh with 350W charger) / 4 hr 30 min (0–80% on 3.7 kWh with 700W Ather Duo charger).
