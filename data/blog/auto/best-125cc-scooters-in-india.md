Stepping up from an entry-level 110cc scooter to a 125cc machine transforms the daily commute in Indian cities: you gain crucial roll-on power to accelerate away from traffic lights, a sturdier chassis that handles heavy grocery runs, and under-seat storage capable of holding a full-face helmet or laptop bag. In 2026, the 125cc segment caters to three distinct user groups: family practicality seekers (TVS Jupiter 125 and Honda Activa 125), ultra-efficient daily commuters (Suzuki Access 125 and Yamaha RayZR Hybrid), and performance enthusiasts (TVS NTORQ 125). This comparative review evaluates real-world mileage, storage ergonomics, and on-road Delhi pricing across the top five contenders.

## Detailed Model-by-Model Evaluation & Official Links

### 1. Suzuki Access 125

The Access 125 pairs a 124 cc SEP engine (8.7 PS @ 6,750 rpm / 10.0 Nm @ 5,500 rpm) with a lightweight 103 kg steel front fender and apron. Its instant throttle response off the line and comfortable 773 mm seat height make it the undisputed market favorite for family and solo commuters alike. Official Link: [Suzuki Access 125 Official Page](https://www.suzukimotorcycle.co.in/product-details/all-new-access-125-bluetooth-enabled)

### 2. TVS Jupiter 125

TVS engineered the Jupiter 125 with unprecedented utility: moving the fuel tank to the floorboard allows an external front fuel filler and an expansive 33-liter boot that fits two full-face helmets side by side. Its 765 mm seat is the longest in the segment, ensuring excellent pillion comfort. Official Link: [TVS Jupiter 125 Official Page](https://www.tvsmotor.com/tvs-jupiter-125/price-in-delhi)

### 3. Honda Activa 125

Featuring Honda's 123.92 cc eSP engine with silent ACG start and Idling Stop, the Activa 125 delivers bulletproof mechanical reliability. The top-tier H-Smart variant adds keyless ignition, remote lock, and anti-theft immobilizer technology. Official Link: [Honda Activa 125 Official Page](https://www.honda2wheelersindia.com/scooter/activa125-anniversary-edition)

### 4. Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid

Weighing an astonishingly light 99 kg, the RayZR Hybrid uses an electric Smart Motor Generator (SMG) to provide power assist when starting from standstill. It easily tops the 125cc segment for fuel economy, consistently recording 55–60 km/l in congested traffic. Official Link: [Yamaha RayZR 125 Fi Hybrid Official Page](https://www.yamaha-motor-india.com/yamaha-ray-zr125fihybrid.html)

### 5. TVS NTORQ 125 Race XP

With a 3-valve engine producing 10.2 PS and dual ride modes (Street and Race), the NTORQ 125 is tailored for riders who prioritize rapid acceleration, a throaty exhaust note, and full Bluetooth navigation telemetry. Official Link: [TVS NTORQ 125 Official Page](https://www.tvsmotor.com/tvs-ntorq)
