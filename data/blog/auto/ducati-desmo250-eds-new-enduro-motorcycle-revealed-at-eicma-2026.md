## 1. Introduction

Ducati has taken its ambitious off-road racing revolution to the next level at EICMA 2026 with the surprise global premiere of the Ducati Desmo250 EDS enduro prototype. Following the triumphant launch and commercial release of the larger Desmo450 MX motocross and Desmo450 EDS enduro machines, Borgo Panigale has officially unveiled its quarter-liter contender engineered to compete in the FIM EnduroGP World Championship and global A2/E1 enduro competitions. Featuring a dedicated 250cc single-cylinder engine with Ducati's signature desmodromic valvetrain, a lightweight perimeter aluminum chassis, and competitionspec suspension, the Desmo250 EDS proves that Ducati's Corse Off-Road division is determined to challenge the entrenched Austrian and Japanese dominance across all professional dirt disciplines.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Ducati Desmo250 EDS was officially previewed during Ducati's pre-EICMA premiere on November 2, 2026, and displayed to public audiences throughout the Milan show. Ducati confirmed that the motorcycle is shown as an advanced pre-production prototype, with active racing competition scheduled for the 2027 European Enduro Championship. Series commercial production for retail customers will commence in summer 2027 as a Model Year 2028 motorcycle across authorized Ducati Off-Road specialist dealerships in Europe. For the Indian market, Ducati India has not confirmed consumer availability, as domestic distribution will prioritize racing teams and specialized competition imports.

## 3. Engine and Performance

At the mechanical core of the Desmo250 EDS is an all-new 250cc, liquid-cooled, single-cylinder, four-stroke engine engineered from scratch by Ducati Corse. In a segment where all competitors rely on conventional valve springs, Ducati brings its legendary desmodromic valvetrain technology to the quarter-liter class. By utilizing mechanical arms to both open and close the titanium valves positively, desmodromic actuation eliminates valve float at extreme rpm, allowing an exceptionally wide and linear powerband that revs past 14,000 rpm. While final homologation figures remain unreleased, industry insiders expect peak power output to approach 44-46 hp. The engine is mated to a specialized wide-ratio six-speed enduro transmission featuring a hydraulic anti-hopping slipper clutch and quickshifter support.

## 4. Design and hardware

The Desmo250 EDS projects a functional, minimalist competition enduro stance finished in iconic Ducati Corse Red with white accents. The motorcycle utilizes a double-cradle aluminum perimeter frame that balances longitudinal rigidity for high-speed stability with lateral flex to absorb harsh root and rock deflections. Suspension duties are commanded by 48mm Showa closed-cartridge inverted front forks delivering 305mm of travel, paired with a linkage-assisted Showa rear monoshock offering 300mm of travel. Authentic enduro-spec Takasago Excel rims (21-inch front and 18-inch rear) are laced to lightweight billet hubs and shod in FIM-homologated Michelin Enduro Medium tires. Ground clearance reaches an outstanding 350mm, with an authentic 960mm competition seat height.

## 5. Features and technology

Despite its pure competition focus, the Desmo250 EDS incorporates sophisticated electronic management. A compact, impact-resistant micro-LCD display mounted behind the enduro headlight mask provides speed, lap times, engine temperature, and diagnostic codes. Engine management is handled by an advanced Keihin EFI system paired with an electronic throttle that offers two selectable engine maps (Dry and Mud), alongside dedicated multi-level Ducati Traction Control (DTC) tuned specifically for loose dirt. Full road-legal enduro lighting, an integrated cooling fan on the right radiator, a translucent 8.5-litre fuel cell, and an aluminum composite skid plate are fitted as standard.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

When commercial sales begin in summer 2027, the Ducati Desmo250 EDS is expected to carry a premium competition retail price between €11,800 and €12,500 in European showrooms, positioning it directly against top-tier Austrian and Swedish competition. In India, should specialized privateer racers or teams import the machine under competition-vehicle provisions, the landing price would exceed Rs 13.00 lakh. Ducati positions the Desmo250 EDS as an exotic, high-performance racing weapon for competitive enduro athletes and discerning off-road purists.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati Desmo250 EDS enters into direct competition with the gold standards of the E1 enduro class: the KTM 250 EXC-F, the Husqvarna FE 250, the Yamaha WR250F, and the Beta RR 250 4T. The Desmo250 EDS distinguishes itself as the only motorcycle in the class to feature desmodromic valvetrain engineering, delivering unmatched top-end over-rev capabilities and Italian racing prestige.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What makes the Ducati Desmo250 EDS engine unique?

It is the only 250cc single-cylinder enduro engine in the world to feature desmodromic valve actuation, eliminating valve float at high rpm.

### Q: When will the Ducati Desmo250 EDS be available to purchase?

Ducati has confirmed that European commercial availability will commence in summer 2027 as a Model Year 2028 motorcycle.

### Q: What suspension is fitted on the Desmo250 EDS?

The motorcycle features competition-spec 48mm Showa closed-cartridge front forks with 305mm of travel and a Showa linkage rear monoshock with 300mm of travel.

### Q: Will the Desmo250 EDS be sold in Indian showrooms?

No, there is currently no evidence of an Indian road-going launch; it is designed primarily as a specialized closedcourse racing motorcycle.
