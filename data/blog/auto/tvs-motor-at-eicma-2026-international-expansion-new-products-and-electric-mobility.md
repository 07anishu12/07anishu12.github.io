## 1. Introduction

TVS Motor Company delivered an authoritative demonstration of global industrial prowess and premium brand stewardship at EICMA 2026 in Milan. As one of the world's leading two-wheeler manufacturers, the Indian multinational utilized the premier European stage to showcase its three-pillared global strategy: revitalizing historic British icon Norton Motorcycles with accessible sub-500cc machinery, accelerating European distribution of its high-performance Apache motorcycle range, and demonstrating cutting-edge zero-emission mobility platforms through the futuristic TVS X electric crossover. By combining world-class manufacturing scale at its Hosur headquarters with European design studios and racing heritage, TVS demonstrated that it is uniquely positioned to capture both premium internal combustion enthusiasts and urban electric commuters worldwide.

## 2. Launch and Availability

TVS Motor Company's expansive presentation was held on November 3, 2026, across interconnected pavilions in Halls 11 and 15 at Fiera Milano. Managing Director Sudarshan Venu outlined the company's European expansion roadmap, confirming that TVS will establish direct subsidiary operations and multi-brand retail partnerships in the United Kingdom, Italy, France, and Germany by mid-2027. Series production of international products will be anchored at TVS's advanced manufacturing complex in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, alongside specialized final assembly at Norton's Solihull facility in England. Domestic Indian dispatches for related co-developed platforms are scheduled to roll out progressively throughout 2027.

## 3. Engine and Performance

The technological centerpiece of TVS's internal combustion presentation was the newly engineered 454cc, liquid-cooled, single-cylinder DOHC engine developed in collaboration with Norton. Producing a robust 47 bhp at 8,000 rpm and 45 Nm of peak torque at 6,000 rpm, this powerplant satisfies European A2 licensing regulations while delivering exceptional low-end torque. Additionally, TVS highlighted its racing-developed RT-Fi (Race Tuned Fuel Injection) 312cc reverse-inclined single-cylinder engine powering the Apache RR 310 and RTR 310, generating 35.6 PS. On the electric frontier, the flagship TVS X featured an advanced 11 kW permanent magnet synchronous motor producing an instantaneous 40 Nm of torque, rocketing from 0-40 km/h in 2.6 seconds.

## 4. Design and hardware

The design narrative across TVS's exhibits reflected functional aerodynamics and precision craftsmanship. The Norton 450 Urban exhibited understated British minimalism with an authentic teardrop fuel tank and lightweight aluminum subframe. The Apache range projected aggressive shark-inspired aerodynamics with wind-tunnel-tested bodywork and thermal management gills. The TVS X displayed an avant-garde cast aluminum perimeter frame (X-Tilt design) with single-sided swingarm geometry. Across all platforms, hardware components emphasized premium specifications, including inverted cartridge forks, radial Brembo/ ByBre calipers, and lightweight alloy wheels engineered to withstand demanding global road conditions.

## 5. Features and technology

TVS reaffirmed its reputation as a technology pioneer in two-wheeler electronics. The showcase highlighted the TVS SmartXonnect and NavPro telematics suites, anchored by a class-leading 10.2-inch HD customizable display on the TVS X and 5.0-inch TFT units on the Apache and Norton lines. Electronic rider aids, governed by multi-axis IMUs, included Lean-Sensitive Cornering ABS, Cornering Traction Control, Wheelie Control, and Cruise Control. Dynamic climate-controlled cooled and heated rider seats, tire pressure monitoring (TPMS), and live racetrack telemetry sharing demonstrated TVS's commitment to cutting-edge rider amenities.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, TVS will position its Apache 310 range as an accessible performance value between €5,400 and €6,000, while the Norton 450 Urban will occupy a premium boutique A2 space around €6,400. In India, TVS's domestic manufacturing dominance enables class-leading value, with the Apache range retailing between Rs 2.50 lakh and Rs 2.80 lakh, and the upcoming Norton 450 Urban projected between Rs 3.10 lakh and Rs 3.50 lakh ex-showroom. TVS positions itself as an engineering-driven powerhouse delivering premium technology at accessible price points.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

TVS's global product offensive confronts established international competitors across multiple fronts. In the roadster and sport segments, TVS and Norton rival the Triumph Speed 400, KTM 390 Duke, BMW G 310 R, and Yamaha MT-03. In the premium electric space, the TVS X competes directly against the BMW CE 02 and Ather 450 Apex.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What was TVS's headline announcement at EICMA 2026?

The premier announcement was the global debut of the TVS-developed Norton 450 Urban, marking Norton's entry into the high-volume A2 category.

### Q: Will Norton motorcycles be sold in India through TVS?

Yes, TVS Motor Company plans to launch Norton in India in late 2027 through exclusive boutique retail lounges in major metro cities.

### Q: What is the TVS X electric scooter?

The TVS X is a premium performance electric crossover featuring an 11 kW motor, 140 km range, aluminum perimeter frame, and a 10.2-inch TFT screen.

### Q: Where are international TVS and Norton models built?

Core powertrain and chassis manufacturing takes place at TVS's Hosur facility in India, with European-spec final assembly at Norton's Solihull plant in the UK.
