Swift offers the smaller documented exterior footprint; i20 is longer and wider with a longer wheelbase. Their two-pedal choices also differ: Swift AGS versus regular i20 iVT. Choose around parking, passenger fit and gearbox behaviour, then calculate running costs without treating claimed mileage as guaranteed.

## Define a comparable shortlist

The [Swift page](https://www.marutisuzuki.com/arena/swift) lists a 3,860 mm length, 1,735 mm width and 2,450 mm wheelbase. The [September 2026 i20 brochure](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/in/en/data/brochure/i20.pdf) lists 3,995 mm, 1,775 mm and 2,580 mm respectively. Swift therefore has the smaller exterior footprint; i20's longer wheelbase warrants a rear-seat check without proving more usable legroom.

The two-pedal options differ materially: Swift uses AGS, an automated manual, while the regular i20 offers iVT alongside its manual. Test low-speed response and incline control in the actual gearbox. Do not use a separately marketed i20 N Line to represent the regular i20.

The i20 brochure specifies a four-cylinder 1.2-litre petrol; Swift's current Z12E belongs to a different engine family. Cylinder count does not establish refinement or fuel costs. Compare demonstrated comfort, exact trim equipment and a conservative consumption budget, rather than transferring an older Swift's specifications.

## Judge city usability personally

Set the seat and steering for your normal driving posture. Check visibility when turning at a junction, reversing near a pillar and judging the front edge in a narrow lane. Body width and turning space matter, but confidence depends on sightlines and control placement too.

In traffic, assess clutch effort for a manual or low-speed response for an automatic. Different automatic designs can feel different when starting, climbing or changing speed. Experience that behaviour instead of assuming all two-pedal cars are equally comfortable.

Try the parking space you use, or measure the bay and access path carefully. Include mirrors and room to open doors. If several family members drive, the best choice should allow each to find a workable position without a complicated setup every time.

## Examine the extra equipment in context

Connect your own phone and check music, navigation prompts and call controls. A bigger display can be useful, but simple climate controls and readable instruments may improve daily use more. Ask which connected functions require payment later.

Test the rear seat with the front positioned correctly. Look at foot space, seat support and the centre passenger's restraints. Bring a stroller or suitcase to compare boot access. Hatchbacks can be versatile, but the height of the opening and the folded-seat arrangement deserve inspection.

Check the exact safety equipment and relevant crash-test evidence through [Bharat NCAP](https://www.bncap.in/) or the specified programme. Never infer equal safety from similar airbag counts alone or transfer an overseas score without matching specification and protocol.

## Convert mileage into a household budget

For a hypothetical example, assume one hatchback returns 16 km/l and another 18 km/l on the same use pattern. With petrol assumed at ₹100 per litre and 1,000 km of monthly travel, the fuel costs are ₹6,250 and about ₹5,556. The difference is approximately ₹694 a month.

These are not measured Swift or i20 results or a current pump-price claim. If the more economical car costs ₹60,000 extra, fuel-only recovery is around 86 months under these assumptions. Insurance, interest and resale can change the conclusion.

Use independent results only when engine, gearbox and conditions are comparable. Claimed efficiency is a laboratory or certification figure under its stated method, not your guaranteed consumption. Record your own full-tank results after purchase over several normal refills rather than trusting a single dashboard reading.

## Decide using total costs and genuine preferences

Ask both workshops for scheduled-service prices, tyre replacement costs and the terms of any prepaid plan. Free service may refer to labour rather than every part or fluid. Price the same insurance cover and keep accessories separate.

Choose the Swift if the version you can buy meets your space and equipment needs while keeping the ownership budget comfortable. Choose the i20 if its matched package provides comfort or convenience you value enough to justify the difference. A personal preference is valid when it is affordable; it should not be disguised as an unsupported technical superiority claim.

Before booking, confirm model year, equipment, delivery charges and cancellation terms. Review the vehicle's condition before final acceptance and retain all promised discounts in writing.

## Quick questions, clear answers

### Is the i20 N Line part of this comparison?

It is a separate derivative and should be identified explicitly if considered. Its price, engine, gearbox and equipment may create a different comparison. Do not use an N Line demonstration to claim the same driving behaviour for a regular i20 variant.

### Can dashboard mileage settle the choice?

No. A demonstration car's reading reflects its recent trips and may not match your use. Compare suitably matched independent tests and manufacturer claims with clear labels, then budget conservatively. Your own repeated full-tank calculations are more useful after purchase.

### Should I stretch the loan for more features?

First compare total repayments and keep an emergency buffer. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly payment while increasing interest. Pay extra for features that solve recurring needs, and avoid extending debt merely because a showroom upgrade looks attractive.
