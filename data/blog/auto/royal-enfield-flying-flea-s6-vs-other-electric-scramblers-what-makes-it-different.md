## 1. Introduction

The global unveiling of the Royal Enfield Flying Flea S6 at EICMA 2026 has electrified the rapidly growing retro-modern electric motorcycle category. In an emerging segment populated largely by generic commuter conversions and plastic-clad urban machines, the Flying Flea S6 stands as a radical, breath-of-fresh-air design statement. Drawing direct lineage from the heroic 125cc airborne Royal Enfield motorcycle of the 1940s, the S6 combines an authentic scrambler silhouette with groundbreaking lightweight metallurgy, including a forged aluminum frame, an articulating girder front fork, and a magnesium battery casing. But how does this Indian-engineered electric scrambler stack up against established international competitors like the British Maeving RM1S and the Super Soco TC Max? A comprehensive side-by-side analysis reveals what makes the Flying Flea S6 genuinely revolutionary.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Royal Enfield Flying Flea S6 celebrated its world premiere at EICMA 2026 on November 3, 2026, in Milan. Royal Enfield confirmed that commercial production is scheduled to begin at its specialized EV manufacturing facility in Cheyyar, Tamil Nadu, in early 2027. European market deliveries are confirmed for summer 2027, followed by domestic Indian availability in late 2027. In contrast, rivals like the Maeving RM1S are currently sold primarily in the UK and select US cities, while Super Soco models are distributed globally through thirdparty importer networks.

## 3. Engine and Performance

The powertrain dynamics of the Flying Flea S6 highlight its engineering sophistication. Powered by an inhouse developed permanent magnet synchronous motor delivering 11 kW (14.9 hp) of peak power and 48 Nm of torque, the S6 achieves a top speed of 95 km/h and a verified WMTC urban range of 110 km. By comparison, the British-built Maeving RM1S produces a continuous 7.0 kW (peak 10.5 kW) from a rear hub motor, delivering 105 km/h top speed and 130 km range from dual swappable batteries. The Super Soco TC Max produces a lower 5.1 kW peak output from a central motor, reaching 95 km/h and an 80-90 km real-world range. The Flying Flea S6 strikes an optimal balance, offering superior low-speed torque and acceleration feel over its competitors.

## 4. Design and hardware

The defining differentiator of the Flying Flea S6 is its breathtaking chassis architecture. While competitors like the Maeving RM1S and Super Soco use conventional steel tube frames and standard telescopic forks, the Flying Flea S6 features an aerospace-grade forged aluminum frame and an authentic articulating girder front fork. This vintage girder setup provides compliant damping without brake dive, creating an unforgettable visual signature. Furthermore, Royal Enfield's organic cooling fins cast into the magnesium battery housing transform the battery from an ugly black box into an artistic sculpture. The S6's 19-inch front and 18-inch rear wire-spoked wheels shod in knobby scrambler tires provide genuine light-trail versatility that pure roadsters like the Maeving lack.

## 5. Features and technology

In software and electronics, the Flying Flea S6 is generations ahead of the Super Soco and Maeving. The S6 features an in-house developed Vehicle Control Unit (VCU) that continuously monitors 200,000 data points per second. It incorporates lean-sensitive Cornering ABS, regenerative braking paddle controls, digital key access via phone proximity, crash detection alerts, and seamless over-the-air (OTA) updates. Cockpit instrumentation is a full-colour round touchscreen TFT display with integrated turn-by-turn navigation, eclipsing the basic analog and LCD gauges on the Maeving and Super Soco.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the Royal Enfield Flying Flea S6 is projected to retail between €4,800 and €5,300. This pricing undercuts the boutique Maeving RM1S (€8,800) by over 40 percent while providing superior technology and forged aluminum construction. In India, domestic manufacturing at Cheyyar enables an estimated ex-showroom price between Rs 1.80 lakh and Rs 2.10 lakh, positioning it as an irresistible, premium lifestyle commuter.

The Flying Flea S6 competes directly against the Maeving RM1S, the Super Soco TC Max, and the upcoming Husqvarna E-Pilen.
