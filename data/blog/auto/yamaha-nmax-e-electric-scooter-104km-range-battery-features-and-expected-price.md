## 1. Introduction

Yamaha has taken a massive stride into the zero-emission urban mobility landscape at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the all-new Yamaha NMAX E electric scooter. Transporting the iconic design language, ergonomic comfort, and dynamic chassis balance of the globally celebrated NMAX family into the electric era, the NMAX E represents Yamaha's flagship battery-electric two-wheeler for international markets. Built to comply with European L3e-A1 (125cc-equivalent) regulations, the NMAX E pairs a powerful mid-mounted electric motor with dual swappable lithium-ion battery packs, delivering a verified real-world range of 104 kilometers on a single charge. It provides modern city commuters with an emission-free, whisper-quiet alternative without sacrificing the premium maxi-scooter luxury that defines the MAX family.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Yamaha NMAX E made its official debut on November 3, 2026, at the Yamaha stand during EICMA in Milan. Yamaha confirmed that commercial retail sales will commence across European markets—including France, Germany, the Netherlands, and Italy—in April 2027. Production will be anchored at Yamaha's specialized EV manufacturing facilities, meeting strict Euro-market safety standards. For the Indian market, India Yamaha Motor has officially confirmed that it is conducting feasibility trials for the NMAX E, with an anticipated domestic debut in late 2027 to spearhead Yamaha's premium EV foray in India.

## 3. Engine and Performance

The NMAX E is driven by an advanced interior permanent magnet synchronous motor (IPMSM) mounted centrally within the chassis, driving the rear wheel via a low-maintenance, quiet carbon-reinforced belt drive. The motor generates a continuous power output of 5.5 kW (7.5 hp) and a peak output of 9.8 kW (13.3 hp), producing an instantaneous 46 Nm of torque from zero rpm. This powertrain enables brisk traffic light acceleration, completing the 0-50 km/h sprint in just 4.2 seconds, with an electronically governed top speed of 95 km/h. The powertrain offers two selectable ride modes: Eco Mode (optimizing battery preservation) and Power Mode (delivering full motor acceleration), alongside regenerative braking that recovers energy during deceleration.

## 4. Design and hardware

The aesthetic silhouette of the NMAX E remains unmistakably MAX. It features the signature boomerangshaped side body motifs, sleek aerodynamic apron contours, and twin-optic LED projector headlights with integrated horizontal DRLs. The dual battery packs, weighing 11.5 kg each, are housed centrally beneath the floorboard tunnel to optimize the center of gravity and ensure neutral handling. The chassis utilizes a highrigidity tubular steel underbone frame paired with standard 13-inch cast alloy wheels shod in low-rollingresistance tires. Braking is executed by 230mm discs front and rear, governed by a sophisticated dualchannel ABS system that prevents wheel lockup on wet city manhole covers.

## 5. Features and technology

A state-of-the-art 5.0-inch TFT instrument console serves as the nerve center of the NMAX E. Equipped with Yamaha's CCU (Communication Control Unit), the display pairs seamlessly with the MyRide and Y-Connect apps to show real-world state-of-charge (SOC), remaining range, nearest public charging stations, and turnby-turn navigation prompts. Additional features include Smart Key keyless operation, walk-assist reverse mode for easy parking in tight bays, full-LED lighting, and an emergency hazard warning system. Even with the dual battery architecture, the underseat storage provides 24 litres of usable space, sufficient for an openface helmet and daily shopping.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the Yamaha NMAX E is expected to retail between €5,400 and €5,800 (excluding regional government EV subsidies), competing against premium electric commuters. In India, where Yamaha aims to establish local battery pack assembly, the estimated ex-showroom price is projected between Rs 1.60 lakh and Rs 1.85 lakh. Yamaha will position the NMAX E as an aspirational, ultra-refined electric maxi-scooter for style-conscious urban commuters who desire proven Japanese engineering.
