In city commuting at current urban rates (~₹102/L petrol, ~₹87/kg CNG), a CNG car cuts your raw fuel bill by roughly ₹2.35 per kilometre—saving ₹3,525 per month on fuel if you drive 1,500 km/month (50 km/day). However, if you finance the CNG variant's ₹1.05 lakh higher on-road price on a 5-year loan, your monthly EMI increases by ~₹2,180—leaving a net monthly cashflow gain of ₹1,345 during the loan tenure (and the full ₹3,525/month once the loan ends). In return, you must factor in 7 to 8 CNG refills per month and a 12%–15% drop in engine power.


## The Monthly Cashflow Trap: Gross Fuel Savings vs Net Savings After EMI


When a showroom executive tells you, *'Sir, CNG will save you ₹3,000 every month,'* they are looking only at the fuel pump receipt and ignoring your bank auto-debit.


Because a factory-fitted CNG variant costs roughly **₹1,05,000 more on-road** than the identical petrol trim, financing that difference over 60 months at 9.2% interest adds **₹2,189 to your monthly EMI**. If your monthly driving is low (say, 750 km/month), your gross fuel saving is only ~₹1,780/month—meaning **your total monthly outflow (EMI + Fuel) is actually ₹400 HIGHER with a CNG car** for the first 5 years!

## The 5 Practical Trade-Offs You Live With Every Week on CNG


Before choosing CNG for a ₹1,300–₹3,500 net monthly saving, weigh these five daily ownership realities:

1. **Refuelling Frequency (Every 3 to 4 Days for Long Commuters):** A petrol tank holds 37 litres (~570 km city range = 2 fills/month). A 60L CNG tank holds ~8 kg of gas at typical city pressure (~180–200 km range = **7 to 12 fills/month**).
1. **Passengers Must Step Out During Refuelling:** For safety compliance at Indian CNG dispensing stations, all passengers must exit the vehicle and stand outside the bay while gas is being filled—inconvenient during monsoon downpours, late nights, or with sleeping children.
1. **12%–15% Lower Overtaking & Flyover Punch:** In naturally aspirated 1.0L/1.2L CNG cars, climbing steep mall ramps or overtaking on two-lane highways with four adults and the AC on requires shifting down to 2nd or 3rd gear.
1. **Spare Wheel Access or Boot Compromise:** Single-cylinder CNG cars lose ~80% of their boot; twin-cylinder CNG cars (Tata/Hyundai) keep a 200+ litre boot but relocate the spare tyre underneath the rear chassis or require removing a cover.
1. **Slightly Stiffer Unladen Rear Ride:** Heavier rear coil springs fitted to support the 65 kg steel cylinder can feel slightly firmer when driving solo over speed breakers.

## Clear Buying Verdict

- **Stick to Petrol if:** Your monthly commute is under 1,000 km (~35 km/day), you travel mostly with family and young children who dislike stepping out at CNG pumps, or you hate waiting 10–15 minutes at refuelling stations twice a week.
- **Choose Factory CNG if:** You drive 50 to 80+ km every single workday (1,500–2,400 km/month), pass an early-morning or late-evening low-queue CNG pump on your office route, and want to slash ₹3,500–₹5,700 off your monthly fuel bill.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why do all passengers have to get out of the car at Indian CNG stations?

Because CNG is dispensed at high pressure (around 200 bar), PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) safety protocols mandate that no occupants remain inside the cabin while the nozzle is connected.

### Q2. Does a turbo-CNG car like the Tata Nexon iCNG solve the CNG power-drop problem?

Yes. Because the Tata Nexon iCNG uses a 1.2-litre turbocharged engine, it still produces 100 PS and 170 Nm of torque in CNG mode—giving it far stronger flyover and highway performance than naturally aspirated 73 PS 1.2-litre CNG hatchbacks.

### Q3. Can I pay cash upfront for the CNG car to enjoy the full monthly fuel savings immediately?

If you pay the ₹1.05 lakh CNG premium in cash without a loan, your monthly bank balance increases by the full ₹2,370–₹3,555 fuel saving from month one, though you still give up ~₹650/month in fixed-deposit interest that the ₹1.05 lakh cash could have earned.

### Q4. Is CNG available consistently on Indian national highways now?

Along major corridors in Delhi NCR, Haryana, UP, Gujarat, Maharashtra (Mumbai–Pune–Nashik), and parts of Karnataka/Telangana, CNG stations are widely available, though highway pump pressure can drop in the afternoons when mother-station booster trucks are in transit.

### Q5. Do CNG engines wear out faster than petrol engines?

Factory-fitted S-CNG, iCNG, and Hy-CNG engines use hardened valve seats and dedicated fuel injectors designed specifically for dry gas operation, routinely lasting 1,50,000+ km when serviced on schedule with timely spark-plug and filter changes.
