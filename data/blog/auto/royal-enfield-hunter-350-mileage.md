## City Traffic Behaviour, Highway Cruising and Full-Tank Measurement

In urban traffic, accelerating 181 kg of motorcycle plus rider from repeated stops holds fuel economy to 32–35 km/l. Short-shifting into 3rd and 4th gear using the engine's 27.0 Nm @ 4,000 rpm torque curve prevents unnecessary fuel burn. On open highways at 80–95 km/h in top gear, the engine runs unstressed and achieves 36–39 km/l, whereas pushing toward its 114 km/h top speed increases aerodynamic drag and drops mileage by 4–6 km/l.
