## 1. Introduction

Ducati has redefined the middleweight supersport category at EICMA 2026 by unveiling the all-new 2027 Ducati Panigale V2 SP. Following the revolutionary debut of Ducati's clean-sheet, ultra-lightweight 890cc 90degree V2 engine—which completely replaced the heavy Superquadro platform—the 'SP' (Sport Production) edition represents the purest, most track-focused expression of this new twin-cylinder era. Weighing an astonishing 175 kg and producing 120 hp, the Panigale V2 SP bridges the gap between everyday road usability and razor-sharp track-day precision. Outfitted with fully adjustable mechanical Öhlins suspension, Brembo Stylema calipers, carbon-fiber aerodynamic components, and an exclusive Winter Test livery, the V2 SP delivers sublime middleweight track agility.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Ducati Panigale V2 SP made its official global debut on November 3, 2026, at the Ducati stand during EICMA in Milan. Production will commence at Borgo Panigale in spring 2027 as an individually numbered series. European showroom availability is confirmed for May 2027. For the Indian market, Ducati India has officially affirmed that the Panigale V2 SP will arrive in late 2027 as a specialized CBU import, catering to track-day enthusiasts who want exotic Italian craftsmanship without the overwhelming mass or power of a liter-bike.

## 3. Engine and Performance

Powering the Panigale V2 SP is Ducati's newly developed 890cc, liquid-cooled, 90-degree V2 engine. Engineered with conventional valve springs instead of desmodromic actuation, the engine weighs just 54.4 kg —making it the lightest multi-cylinder engine Ducati has ever produced. Equipped with variable valve timing (IVVT), the engine produces a punchy 120 hp (88 kW) at 10,750 rpm and 93.3 Nm of peak torque at 8,250 rpm. Over 70 percent of peak torque is accessible from just 3,000 rpm, delivering explosive corner-exit drive. The engine is mated to a close-ratio six-speed transmission featuring an assist-and-slipper clutch and the recalibrated Ducati Quick Shift (DQS) 2.0 system.

## 4. Design and hardware

The design of the Panigale V2 SP mirrors the svelte, aerodynamic profile of the flagship Panigale V4. The monocoque front frame attaches directly to the engine heads, keeping chassis weight exceptionally low. Unlike the standard V2, the SP edition is equipped with premium mechanical Öhlins hardware: 43mm NIX30 inverted front forks with titanium nitride (TiN) stanchion coating and an Öhlins TTX36 rear monoshock, paired with an adjustable Öhlins steering damper. Lightweight forged aluminum wheels reduce unsprung rotating mass, while braking is commanded by Brembo Stylema calipers biting 320mm discs. Billet aluminum adjustable rearsets, a carbon-fiber front mudguard, and an open carbon clutch guard complete the aesthetic.

## 5. Features and technology

The technological cockpit is anchored by a 5.0-inch TFT colour display featuring high-contrast Track and Road layouts. Electronic rider aids, powered by a six-axis IMU, include Cornering ABS with slide-by-brake functionality, Ducati Traction Control (DTC), Ducati Wheelie Control (DWC), and Engine Brake Control (EBC). Four riding modes—Race, Sport, Road, and Wet—allow riders to tailor throttle mapping and safety intervention. The SP edition includes the Ducati Data Logger (DDL) with integrated GPS lap timing as standard factory equipment.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Ducati Panigale V2 SP will retail at approximately €21,500, positioning it between the standard V2 (€16,800) and the entry Panigale V4. In India, imported via CBU channels, the anticipated ex-showroom price is estimated between Rs 22.00 lakh and Rs 24.50 lakh. Ducati positions the V2 SP as the ultimate track tool for riders who prioritize pure corner speed, accessible horsepower, and lightweight agility over overwhelming straight-line velocity.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati Panigale V2 SP competes in the premium middleweight supersport category against the Yamaha YZF-R9, the MV Agusta F3 Competizione, and the Triumph Daytona 660. Compared to the Yamaha R9, the Panigale V2 SP offers higher-spec Öhlins suspension, lighter dry weight (164 kg), forged wheels, and the bespoke exclusivity of Borgo Panigale craftsmanship.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What engine is used in the new Panigale V2 SP?

It is powered by Ducati's new 890cc liquid-cooled 90° V2 engine featuring IVVT variable valve timing, producing 120 hp and 93.3 Nm of torque.

### Q: How much does the Panigale V2 SP weigh?

The motorcycle has an extraordinary kerb weight of just 175 kg (164 kg dry), making it the lightest twin-cylinder Panigale ever built.

### Q: Does the V2 SP feature Öhlins suspension?

Yes, the SP edition comes equipped with fully adjustable Öhlins NIX30 front forks, an Öhlins TTX36 rear monoshock, and an Öhlins steering damper.

### Q: When will the Panigale V2 SP launch in India?

Ducati India has confirmed an official market launch scheduled for late 2027.
