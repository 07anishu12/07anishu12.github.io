In the real Indian used-car market (measured as a percentage of your original On-Road Invoice Price, not Ex-Showroom), a mainstream petrol car or popular SUV typically loses 28% to 35% of its value in the first 3 years (retaining 65%–72%), loses 42% to 50% by Year 5 (retaining 50%–58%), and loses 55% to 65% by Year 7 (retaining 35%–45%). Because the steepest rupee drop happens in Years 1 to 3 (when road tax and new-car registration are absorbed), the optimal financial window is to buy a used car at Year 3, or hold a brand-new car for at least 5.5 to 7 years before upgrading.


## Why Depreciation Is Non-Linear: The 3 Phases of an Indian Car's Value


A car does NOT lose a flat 10% every year. Instead, its market value follows a three-stage curve:

- **Phase 1: The Years 0 to 3 Steep Drop (-28% to -38% of On-Road):** The moment a car is registered, the 12%–18% state road tax and registration fees become non-recoverable, and the car shifts from 'New' to '1st-Owner Used'.
- **Phase 2: The Years 3 to 6 Plateau (-6% to -8% per year):** Once the initial tax hit is absorbed, depreciation slows down dramatically. Between Year 3 and Year 5, a ₹12 lakh on-road car loses only ~₹70,000–₹85,000 per year!
- **Phase 3: The Post-Year 7 Financing & Wear Cliff:** Why do values dip faster again after Year 7?

## The 5 Variables That Shift Your Car's Curve by ±12%

1. **Segment & Model Liquidity:** A Hyundai Creta, Maruti Brezza, Swift, WagonR, or Toyota Innova/Fortuner sits at the top of its retention band; discontinued or low-volume models sit 10%–15% lower.
1. **Variant Level (Mid vs Top):** As explained in Article 59, base and mid-variants retain a higher *percentage* of their invoice price than top-end variants loaded with fast-ageing electronics.
1. **Delhi NCR 10-Year Diesel vs 15-Year Petrol Rule:** In Delhi NCR, a diesel car's value holds well until Year 5, then plunges sharply at **Year 7** (retaining only 28%–35%) because only 3 years of legal NCR road life remain!
1. **Colour Choice:** White, Silver, and Grey cars sell 2–3 weeks faster and fetch 3%–5% more after 5 years than polarising bright yellows, greens, or reds.
1. **Stamps in the Authorised Service History:** A continuous dealer service record adds 6%–10% to Year 5 and Year 7 resale value because it proves genuine odometer mileage.

## Clear Timing Verdict for Buyers & Owners

- **If Buying Brand-New:** Hold the car for at least **6 to 7 years** so your high Year 1–3 depreciation is averaged down to a low annual cost—and sell just before Year 7 so your buyer can still get a 4-to-5-year bank loan.
- **If Buying Pre-Owned:** Buy at **Year 3** (paying ~65% of on-road price) and sell at **Year 6** (getting ~48% back)—driving a modern car for 3 years while losing less than 17% in depreciation!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why do people calculate depreciation from On-Road Price instead of Ex-Showroom Price?

Because as a private car buyer, your bank account paid the On-Road Price (including RTO tax and insurance). When you sell the car for ₹6.50 lakh after 5 years, subtracting ₹6.50 lakh from your actual ₹12.00 lakh on-road outlay shows your true ₹5.50 lakh wealth loss.

### Q2. What happens if a car is stolen or total-loss crashed in Year 1, 2, or 3?

Without a Return-to-Invoice (RTI) insurance add-on, the insurer deducts 5% to 30% IDV depreciation and ignores road tax. Buying the ₹1,800–₹3,500 RTI add-on during the first 3 years forces the insurer to refund 100% of your original On-Road Invoice (including road tax and registration!) in case of theft or total loss.

### Q3. How does corporate Income Tax depreciation (15% WDV) differ from market depreciation?

For businesses and self-employed professionals, Section 32 of the Income Tax Act allows claiming 15% annual Written Down Value (WDV) depreciation on the car's book value as a tax-deductible expense—which is purely an accounting tax shield and independent of what the car sells for in the market.

### Q4. Does selling my car in December hurt its resale price compared to selling in January?

Actually, selling in October–November is often better than waiting until January! Once the calendar flips to January 1st, every used-car valuation grid in India automatically ages your car by +1 full year.

### Q5. How do Electric Vehicles (EVs) depreciate at 3 and 5 years compared to petrol cars?

Currently in India, 3-to-5-year-old EVs depreciate roughly 8% to 12% faster than equivalent petrol cars (~52%–58% retention at Year 3 and ~38%–45% at Year 5) because used buyers factor in the remaining years of the 8-year high-voltage battery warranty and fast-evolving battery capacities.
