Under Indian safety mandates, two-wheelers up to 125cc must have at least Combined Braking System (CBS/IBS/SBT), while motorcycles above 125cc require Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS). However, CBS merely links front and rear brake force mechanically—it CANNOT prevent wheel lockup on wet or sandy.
