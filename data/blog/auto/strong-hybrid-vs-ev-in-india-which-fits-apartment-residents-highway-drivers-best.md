Choose between a Strong Hybrid and an Electric Vehicle based strictly on two lifestyle filters—not brochure slogans: (1) Can you install a 15A / 7.2 kW charger in your apartment parking slot? and (2) How often do you drive more than 350 km in a single day? If you lack a home/office charger OR frequently take spontaneous 500–800 km highway trips to rural towns and hills, buy a Strong Hybrid (Grand Vitara, Hyryder, City e:HEV, or Innova Hycross)—it delivers 22–24 km/l in city traffic and 850+ km highway range with 3-minute petrol refills. If you HAVE a home parking plug and 85% of your driving is within 250 km of your city, buy an EV—it cuts city running costs to ₹1.30/km, offers faster acceleration, and avoids the Strong Hybrid's smaller 265-litre boot.


## How Strong Hybrids and EVs Differ in Daily Indian Life

- **Strong Hybrid (Self-Charging Petrol + Electric):** You never plug it into a wall. You fill regular petrol at any Indian Oil/HP/BPCL pump in 3 minutes.
- **Pure Electric Vehicle (BEV):** Has no petrol engine at all. When charged overnight at home (~₹8/unit), it runs for **₹1.20–₹1.45/km** (one-third the cost of even a Strong Hybrid!), accelerates effortlessly with zero engine noise, and requires only ₹3,500/year in servicing.

Where the EV beats the Strong Hybrid decisively in the 4.3-metre SUV segment is **Boot Space and Power**:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why doesn't the Toyota Innova Hycross Strong Hybrid lose boot space like the Grand Vitara Hybrid?

Because the larger Innova Hycross (and Maruti Invicto) mounts its compact Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) hybrid battery pack underneath the front driver and co-driver seats rather than in the rear trunk—preserving the full 300-litre boot even with all three rows upright!

### Q2. Can a Strong Hybrid drive on pure electric power without the petrol engine running?

Yes, automatically for short bursts of 1 to 3 km at speeds below 40–50 km/h when crawling through traffic or parking lots (provided the hybrid battery is above 40% charge). The moment the battery dips or you press the accelerator harder for highway speeds, the petrol engine seamlessly starts up.

### Q3. Do Strong Hybrids qualify for green number plates and EV road-tax exemptions?

No. Green number plates and central 5% GST / full EV road-tax exemptions apply only to plug-in Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs). Strong Hybrids use regular white number plates and attract standard hybrid GST (though a few individual states periodically offer partial road-tax rebates on Strong Hybrids).

### Q4. Which is quieter inside the cabin at 100 km/h on the highway: a Strong Hybrid or an EV?

The Electric SUV is quieter under highway acceleration. When you press the accelerator hard to overtake a truck at 90 km/h in a Grand Vitara or Hyryder Strong Hybrid, its 3-cylinder petrol engine revs up noticeably via the e-CVT, whereas an EV surges silently from 80 to 120 km/h.

### Q5. What if our household already owns a petrol or diesel highway car and is buying a second car?

If you already have an ICE car in the garage for outstation family vacations and have a home parking socket, a pure Electric Car is the undisputed winner for your second car—delivering ₹1.25/km daily commuting with zero tailpipe emissions.
