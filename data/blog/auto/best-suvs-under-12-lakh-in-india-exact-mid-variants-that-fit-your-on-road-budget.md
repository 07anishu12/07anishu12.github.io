To keep your final on-road invoice at or below ₹12 lakh in most Indian states, you must target SUV variants priced up to ₹9.99–₹10.30 lakh ex-showroom (staying below the ₹10 lakh tax and 1% TCS cliff where possible). The smartest variant-level buys in this bracket are the Mahindra XUV 3XO MX3 / MX2 Pro AT, Tata Nexon Pure+ S, Maruti Brezza VXi, Skoda Kylaq Signature, and Kia Sonet HTK (O).


## Why the ₹9.99 Lakh Ex-Showroom Mark Is Crucial for a ₹12 Lakh Budget


In most Indian states, the moment an SUV's ex-showroom price crosses ₹10,00,000, road tax jumps to a higher percentage slab AND 1% Tax Collected at Source (TCS) is added. Consequently, a variant priced at ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom typically lands around **₹11.25–₹11.65 lakh on-road**, whereas a variant priced at ₹10.40 lakh jumps past ₹12.20 lakh.


Automakers know this rule well and engineer high-value 'sweet-spot' mid-variants right between ₹9.40 lakh and ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom.

## Variant-by-Variant Strengths and Trade-Offs

- **Mahindra XUV 3XO MX2 Pro AT / MX3 MT:** The only sub-4m SUV that gives you a proper **6-speed Aisin Torque Converter automatic** paired with a 111 PS turbo-petrol engine AND an electric sunroof for ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom (~₹11.35 lakh on-road). If you drive a manual, the MX3 adds a 10.25-inch HD screen with wireless Android Auto/CarPlay and cruise control.
- **Tata Nexon Pure+ / Pure+ S:** Priced just below the ₹10 lakh ex-showroom cliff, the Pure+ gives you a 10.25-inch Harman touchscreen, wireless smartphone projection, LED headlamps, rear camera, and a 5-star Bharat NCAP body shell with a 382-litre boot.
- **Maruti Suzuki Brezza VXi MT:** Gives you the segment's only four-cylinder 1.5-litre naturally aspirated petrol engine, automatic climate control, electrically foldable ORVMs, and height-adjustable driver's seat—though you miss cruise control and alloy wheels at this trim.
- **Skoda Kylaq Signature 1.0 TSI MT:** Built on the 5-star MQB-A0-IN platform with a punchy 115 PS 1.0L turbo-petrol engine, dynamic European suspension poise, alloy wheels, cruise control, and a massive 446-litre roof-rated boot.

## What If You Want Top-End Features (360-Cam, Alloys, Ventilated Seats) Under ₹12 Lakh?


If you dislike mid-variants with wheel covers and want top-trim luxuries under ₹12 lakh on-road, step down half a segment to the **Tata Punch Accomplished+ Sunroof** or **Maruti Fronx Delta+ (O) AGS** (~₹10.2–₹10.6 lakh on-road). You give up some cabin width, but you get a factory-complete car with ₹1.5 lakh left in your bank account.


## Clear Buying Decision

- **Choose the Mahindra XUV 3XO MX2 Pro AT if:** You need a smooth Torque Converter automatic SUV under ₹12 lakh on-road.
- **Choose the Tata Nexon Pure+ S or Skoda Kylaq Signature if:** You drive a manual and want turbo-petrol highway punch, 5-star safety, and factory touchscreen/cruise features.
- **Choose the Maruti Brezza VXi MT if:** You want a relaxed, ultra-reliable 4-cylinder engine for city family duties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can I buy a Hyundai Creta or Kia Seltos under ₹12 lakh on-road?

No. Even the base Hyundai Creta E (₹11.00 lakh ex-showroom) and Kia Seltos HTE (₹11.25 lakh ex-showroom) cross ₹12.75–₹13.15 lakh on-road after adding state road tax, 1% TCS, and insurance—and neither base trim includes a music system.

### Q2. What is the best automatic SUV under ₹12 lakh on-road?

The Mahindra XUV 3XO MX2 Pro AT (₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom / ~₹11.34 lakh on-road) is the standout automatic pick because it uses a genuine 6-speed Torque Converter automatic rather than a jerky AMT.

### Q3. Does the Maruti Brezza VXi Automatic fit under ₹12 lakh on-road?

The Brezza VXi AT is priced around ₹11.15 lakh ex-showroom, which takes its on-road price to roughly ₹12.85–₹13.10 lakh in Delhi. If your ₹12 lakh cap is strict, look at the Fronx Delta+ (O) AGS (~₹10.15 lakh on-road) or XUV 3XO MX2 Pro AT.

### Q4. Is the Kia Sonet 1.2L HTK (O) powerful enough for highway trips?

Inside the city, the Sonet's 83 PS 1.2-litre four-cylinder engine is very smooth and refined. On open highways with four adults and luggage, it feels adequate up to 90–100 km/h, whereas the 111–120 PS turbo engines in the Kylaq, XUV 3XO, and Nexon overtake much faster.

### Q5. Should I add alloy wheels later if my ₹12 lakh variant comes with steel wheels?

Sticking with factory 16-inch styled steel wheels (like those on the Nexon Pure+ and XUV 3XO MX3) is completely safe and gives you taller, pothole-resistant tyre sidewalls. You can always upgrade to OEM alloys later if desired.
