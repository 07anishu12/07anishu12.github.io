## 1. Introduction

Ducati has elevated its off-road credentials to competition-level heights at EICMA 2026 by showcasing the new Ducati DesertX Rally. Developed in the grueling crucible of international rally-raid events like the Erzbergrodeo and Africa Eco Race, the DesertX Rally is engineered for riders who find standard middleweight adventure bikes too compromised for extreme off-road terrain. Retaining the versatile 937cc Testastretta 11° powertrain, the Rally variant undergoes a radical chassis overhaul: closed-cartridge KYB suspension derived directly from professional motocross machinery, specialized Takasago Excel dirt rims with billet hubs, an Öhlins steering damper, and heavy-duty carbon-fiber protection, making it the most rugged production motorcycle ever created in Borgo Panigale.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The Ducati DesertX Rally was showcased at EICMA 2026 on November 3, 2026, in Milan. Ducati confirmed that global production is underway, with European and American showroom deliveries commencing in early 2027. For the Indian market, Ducati India has officially confirmed that the DesertX Rally will be imported in specialized allocations for serious adventure travelers starting in mid-2027, serviced through authorized Ducati dealerships across major metropolitan centers.

## 3. Engine and Performance

Powering the DesertX Rally is Ducati's proven 937cc, liquid-cooled, desmodromic four-valve Testastretta 11° L-twin engine. Euro 5+ compliant, the engine delivers 110 hp (81 kW) at 9,250 rpm and 92 Nm of peak torque at 6,500 rpm. The power delivery is governed by specialized off-road mapping that smooths initial throttle response across rocky singletracks while delivering intense midrange surge. The six-speed gearbox features short, rally-specific gear ratios in first through fifth gears to maximize low-speed obstacle hopping, paired with an assist-and-slipper clutch and the Ducati Quick Shift (DQS) bi-directional quickshifter.

## 4. Design and hardware

The hardware transformation of the DesertX Rally is breathtaking. Up front, it features a 48mm KYB closedcartridge inverted fork with Kashima coating on the outer tubes and DLC treatment on the stanchions, delivering an enormous 250mm of travel with hydraulic bottoming stops. The rear utilizes a fully adjustable KYB monoshock providing 240mm of travel. An adjustable Öhlins steering damper is bolted directly to the billet triple clamps to tame high-speed headshake across desert whoops. Wheels are authentic heavy-duty Takasago Excel rims (21-inch front, 18-inch rear) laced to machined billet aluminum hubs with tube-type Pirelli Scorpion Rally tires. Ground clearance rises to an astounding 280mm.

## 5. Features and technology

Rally-grade technology is embedded throughout the cockpit. A vertically oriented 5.0-inch TFT display features an exclusive 'Rally' screen layout that emulates an authentic Dakar roadbook, providing trip meters and navigation waypoints. Six distinct riding modes are available: Sport, Touring, Urban, Wet, Enduro, and Rally (which unleashes full 110 hp with minimized electronic intervention). The Cornering ABS system offers three levels, including a dedicated Off-Road setting that completely disengages rear-wheel ABS. A forged carbon-fiber sump guard, a high-mounted front rally fender, and machined aluminum gear and brake pedals come standard.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the Ducati DesertX Rally retails at approximately €22,500, commanding a noticeable premium over the standard DesertX (€17,200). In India, where CBU taxation applies, the estimated exshowroom price is projected between Rs 23.50 lakh and Rs 25.50 lakh. Ducati positions the DesertX Rally as an ultra-hardcore, no-compromise performance machine for affluent rally-raid competitors and off-road purists who demand the absolute finest hardware available.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Ducati DesertX Rally competes directly at the sharp end of the hardcore middleweight adventure category against the KTM 890 Adventure R Rally, the Yamaha Ténéré 700 World Raid, and the Husqvarna Norden 901 Expedition. Against the KTM 890 Rally, the Ducati counters with superior ground clearance (280mm), exquisite Italian rally styling, and the proven low-maintenance reliability of the Testastretta 11° engine.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What makes the DesertX Rally different from the standard DesertX?

The Rally features professional 48mm KYB closed-cartridge forks, 250mm front/240mm rear travel, Takasago Excel rims with billet hubs, an Öhlins steering damper, a high front fender, and 280mm ground clearance.

### Q: What is the seat height of the DesertX Rally?

The motorcycle has an uncompromising rally seat height of 910 mm, optimized for standing off-road control.

### Q: Can the DesertX Rally run tubeless tires?

The heavy-duty Takasago Excel competition wheels are designed for tube-type off-road racing tires to resist harsh rock impacts without rim failure.

### Q: When will the DesertX Rally be available in India?

Ducati India will open specialized import allocations for the DesertX Rally starting in mid-2027.
