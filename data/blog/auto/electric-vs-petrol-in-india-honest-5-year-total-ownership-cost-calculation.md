When you compare an Indian Electric SUV (such as the Tata Punch EV or Nexon EV) against its equivalent Petrol Automatic sibling (Punch AMT or Nexon Turbo DCA) over 5 years, the on-road upfront price gap is only ₹1.10 lakh to ₹1.70 lakh in states with EV road-tax exemptions. At a typical city commute of 1,250 km/month (75,000 km over 5 years) using 85% home charging at ₹8/kWh and petrol at ₹102/L, the EV saves ~₹4,85,000 in energy and ~₹22,000 in servicing. Even after accounting for slightly higher EV insurance and ~8% softer 5-year resale retention, the EV comes out ₹2,10,000 to ₹2,60,000 cheaper over 5 years if you drive at least 1,000+ km per month.


## Rule #1: Always Compare EV Against the Equivalent Petrol Automatic (On-Road)


Every Electric Vehicle is inherently an effortless, zero-shift automatic with standard automatic climate control and push-button start. Comparing a ₹14.5 lakh EV against a ₹9.5 lakh base manual petrol is an apples-to-oranges mistake.


When you compare a **Tata Nexon Creative+ 1.2 Turbo DCA (Petrol Automatic)** against a **Tata Nexon EV Creative+ / Fearless**, notice what happens to the on-road price: the Petrol DCA attracts **29% GST + 10%–14% state RTO road tax + 1% TCS**, whereas the EV attracts **only 5% GST and ₹0 (or reduced) RTO road tax in many states**! That shrinks a ₹2.40 lakh ex-showroom gap down to just **₹1.15–₹1.55 lakh on the road**.

## How Your Monthly Driving Distance Changes the 5-Year Verdict


Using the exact same ₹7.95/km net energy + service saving (₹9.55/km Petrol Auto vs ₹1.60/km Blended EV charging), here is how the 5-year bottom line shifts across three driving patterns:

- **Low Running (600 km/month = 36,000 km in 5 Yrs):** Energy saved = ₹2.86L. Minus extra loan interest, insurance, and lower resale (-₹2.75L), **Net 5-Yr Saving = ~₹11,000 (Essentially a Tie — Buy Petrol for simplicity)**.
- **Moderate Commute (1,250 km/month = 75,000 km in 5 Yrs):** Energy + service saved = ₹5.07L. **Net 5-Yr Wealth Saved by EV = ₹2,27,000**.
- **High Commute (2,000 km/month = 1,20,000 km in 5 Yrs):** Energy + service saved = ₹8.10L. **Net 5-Yr Wealth Saved by EV = ₹5,30,000+**!

## What About Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) Pricing on MG EVs?


JSW MG Motor India offers its Comet EV, Windsor EV, and ZS EV with optional **Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS)**—lowering the upfront ex-showroom vehicle price by ₹2.5 to ₹3.5 lakh while charging ₹3.30 to ₹4.50 per km as battery rental (plus ~₹1.30/km electricity = ₹4.60–₹5.80/km total). For most private buyers keeping the car 5+ years, **buying the EV outright with the battery included is cheaper per km than BaaS** unless you want the lowest possible day-one down payment.


## Clear Buying Verdict

- **Buy the Electric Car if:** You have a dedicated 15A/7.2kW home charger in your parking spot, drive at least 1,000+ km per month (35+ km/day) in city/suburban traffic, and want silent, jerk-free driving that saves ₹2.2–₹5.0 lakh over 5 years.
- **Buy the Petrol Automatic if:** You drive less than 750 km/month, lack home parking/charging access, or use this single car primarily for spontaneous 600 km rural highway road trips.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why did we assume ₹1.60/km for the EV instead of ₹1.20/km in the 5-year table?

Because over 5 years (75,000 km), most EV owners do ~85% of their charging at home (~₹1.30/km including AC charging loss at ₹8/unit) and ~15% of their charging at commercial ₹16–₹19/unit highway DC fast chargers (~₹3.25/km) on weekend trips—yielding a realistic blended cost of ₹1.60/km.

### Q2. Will I have to pay to replace the EV battery within the first 5 years?

No. Every major EV sold in India includes at least an 8-year / 1,60,000 km standard factory warranty on the high-voltage battery pack (and Tata/MG/Mahindra offer lifetime/15-year first-owner battery warranties on select models like Curvv EV, Nexon EV 45, Windsor EV, and BE 6/XEV 9e).

### Q3. Do EV tyres wear out faster than petrol car tyres?

If you launch aggressively at every traffic signal using instant electric torque, front tyres can wear out 5,000 km earlier (around 35,000–40,000 km instead of 45,000 km). Accelerating smoothly in City/Normal mode gives virtually identical tyre life to a petrol SUV.

### Q4. What if my state reintroduces road tax on Electric Vehicles?

States periodically review EV road-tax exemptions (some cap full exemption to EVs under ₹10L or ₹25L, while others offer a 50%–100% rebate). Always check your state's current RTO tax slab on the proforma invoice before booking.

### Q5. Is an EV cheaper over 5 years than a Factory CNG car?

On a ₹9–₹10 lakh hatchback/micro-SUV (like Tiago EV / Punch EV vs Tiago iCNG / Punch iCNG), a home-charged EV saves an additional ₹2.50 per km over CNG (~₹1.30/km vs ~₹3.80–₹4.20/km) with zero CNG pump queues and standard automatic convenience!
