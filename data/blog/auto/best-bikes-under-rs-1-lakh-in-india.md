## Buyer-Specific Recommendations & Use-Case Verdict

Match your riding profile to the right motorcycle:
1. **Daily Office Commuters (25–50 km/day):** Pick the Honda SP 125 or TVS Raider 125 for 56–60 km/l real-world efficiency, light clutch action, and low maintenance.
2. **Family & Pillion Riders:** Choose the Honda Shine 125 or TVS Raider 125 (Single-seat variant) for flat two-up seating comfort and plush rear suspension over broken roads.
3. **Safety-Focused Riders:** Choose the Hero Xtreme 125R (Single-Channel ABS) for superior wet-weather stopping distance without front-wheel lockup.
4. **College & Young Riders:** Choose the Bajaj Pulsar N125 or TVS Raider 125 for bold naked-street styling and smartphone connectivity.

## Pre-Purchase Showroom Checklist & Frequently Asked Questions

**Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Checklist:**
1. Check the 17-digit VIN on the steering headstock in daylight to confirm a fresh 2026 manufacturing month.
2. Demand an itemized proforma invoice separating ex-showroom price, state road tax, and mandatory insurance. Reject unauthorized dealer handling or logistics charges.
3. Verify that the fuel tank and documentation confirm OBD2B and E20 ethanol compliance.
