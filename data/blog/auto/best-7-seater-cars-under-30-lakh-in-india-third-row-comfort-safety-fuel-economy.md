If third-row adult comfort, luggage capacity with all seats up (300 litres), and low monthly fuel bills (16–18 km/l in city traffic) are your top priorities under ₹30 lakh on-road, the Maruti Suzuki Invicto Zeta+ Strong Hybrid and Toyota Innova Hycross VX Strong Hybrid are in a league of their own. If you specifically want an SUV silhouette with 5-star Bharat NCAP crash safety, the Tata Safari (Adventure+ A / Accomplished) offers a noticeably roomier third row for adults than the Mahindra XUV700 AX7.


## Ranking the Third Row: Who Actually Fits Adults in Row 3?


When spending ₹24–₹30 lakh on a three-row vehicle, do not judge the cabin solely from the driver's seat. Climbing into the third row reveals a clear hierarchy:

1. **Tier 1 — True 3-Row Adult Lounge (Innova Hycross & Maruti Invicto):** A 2,850 mm wheelbase and flat monocoque floor allow three medium-built adults (or two tall 6-footers) to sit in the third row with reclining backrests, large glass windows, roof AC vents, AND a **300-litre boot** that still holds three suitcases.
1. **Tier 2 — Usable Adult Third Row in an SUV (Tata Safari):** Unlike the XUV700, the Safari's stepped roof and deeper rear footwell give third-row passengers proper under-thigh support, adult headroom, USB ports, and AC vents—though boot space with all rows up drops to ~73–110 litres (two cabin bags).
1. **Tier 3 — Occasional Adult / Kids-First Third Row (Mahindra XUV700, Scorpio-N & Hyundai Alcazar):** The XUV700 is a phenomenal 5-seater with 2 folding rear seats for children or short adult city hops, because its high third-row floor pushes adult knees upward and its second row does not slide on 7-seater bench trims.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why is the Tata Safari's third row more comfortable for adults than the Mahindra XUV700's?

The Tata Safari is 66 mm longer and 30 mm taller than the XUV700, features a stepped stadium-style roof, has a deeper floor pan under the third row for better knee angle, and allows the second-row bench to slide forward and backward to share legroom—whereas the XUV700's 7-seater second row has a fixed base.

### Q2. Should I buy the Innova Hycross GX(O) non-hybrid if my budget is ₹25 lakh on-road?

If your monthly running is under 700–800 km (mostly weekend family outings), the Hycross GX(O) 2.0L Petrol CVT (~₹24.6 lakh on-road) is a brilliant value buy—giving you the exact same cabin space, LED lights, 10.1-inch screen, and 360-degree camera while saving ₹5.5 lakh upfront over the VX Hybrid.

### Q3. Is the Maruti Invicto really a rebadged Toyota Innova Hycross?

Yes. Toyota manufactures the Maruti Invicto alongside the Innova Hycross at its Bidadi plant near Bengaluru using the exact same chassis, 2.0L Strong Hybrid powertrain, and sheet metal, and both achieved a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating.

### Q4. Which 6-seater under ₹30 lakh offers ventilated seats in the middle captain row?

Both the Hyundai Alcazar Signature 6-Seater and the higher Accomplished trims of the Tata Safari 6-Seater offer cooled/ventilated captain chairs in the second row.

### Q5. Can I fit a roof carrier legally on a 7-seater SUV if the boot is full?

Regulations regarding external roof luggage carriers vary by state RTO in India; instead of permanent metal roof racks over a panoramic glass sunroof, many families use a tow-hitch cargo basket or fold one half (50:50 split) of the third row when carrying 6 people.
