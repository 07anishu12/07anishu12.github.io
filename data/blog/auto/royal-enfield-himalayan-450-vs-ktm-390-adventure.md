## City Traffic Tractability, Highway Touring Range and Performance

In urban traffic, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 leverages Sherpa 450 liquid-cooled engine, 6-speed gearbox, 17-litre tank, 43mm Showa USD forks (200mm travel), and Performance/Eco ride modes to return 25–28 km/l, while the KTM 390 Adventure uses Cornering ABS, Motorcycle Traction Control (MTC), Offroad mode, WP APEX suspension, and lighter 177–182 kg wet weight to achieve 24–27 km/l.

On open highways, the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 reaches 151 km/h and cruises best at 95–115 km/h (29–33 km/l, ~476 km tank range), whereas the KTM 390 Adventure reaches 155 km/h and cruises cleanly at 100–120 km/h (28–31 km/l, ~392 km tank range).
