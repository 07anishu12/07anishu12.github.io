## 1. Introduction

Triumph Motorcycles has unveiled the extensively refined 2027 Triumph Tiger 1200 flagship adventure touring range at EICMA 2026 in Milan. Engineered to conquer the most demanding transcontinental journeys and challenging off-road wilderness terrain, the updated Tiger 1200 family receives substantial mechanical and technological enhancements designed to elevate low-speed maneuverability, long-distance two-up comfort, and overall touring refinement. Retaining the distinctive 150 PS T-Plane three-cylinder engine and maintenance-free shaft drive, the 2027 lineup—spanning the road-focused GT Pro and GT Explorer alongside the all-terrain Rally Pro and Rally Explorer—introduces a refined crankshaft balance for silky low-speed crawling, upgraded Active Preload Reduction suspension software, revised touring seats, and expanded cornering electronic rider aids.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Triumph Tiger 1200 lineup was officially showcased on November 3, 2026, during Triumph's keynote presentation at EICMA. Triumph confirmed that global customer deliveries across the UK, Europe, and North America will commence in February 2027. For the Indian market, Triumph Motorcycles India has officially confirmed that the updated 2027 Tiger 1200 range will be introduced in the third quarter of 2027 as a completely knocked-down (CKD) assembled model at its Manesar facility, available through its expanding network of premium Triumph dealerships nationwide.

## 3. Engine and Performance

At the core of the Tiger 1200 is Triumph's unique 1,160cc, liquid-cooled, 12-valve DOHC inline three-cylinder engine featuring the acclaimed T-Plane crankshaft with an uneven 1-3-2 firing order. The engine delivers an exhilarating 150 PS (148 bhp / 110.4 kW) at 9,000 rpm and 130 Nm of peak torque at 7,000 rpm. For the 2027 model year, Hinckley engineers have increased crankshaft and alternator rotor inertia, smoothing out low-rpm torque delivery to eliminate previous complaints of abrupt low-speed snatchiness in technical off-road ruts. Power is delivered to the rear wheel through an ultra-smooth six-speed gearbox, a slip-and-assist clutch, Triumph Shift Assist (bi-directional quickshifter), and a lightweight enclosed cardan shaft drive housed within the Tri-Link aluminum swingarm.

## 4. Design and hardware

The 2027 Tiger 1200 lineup is tailored across two distinct disciplines: the road-biased GT family (riding on 19inch front and 18-inch rear cast aluminum wheels) and the dual-sport Rally family (equipped with 21-inch front and 18-inch rear tubeless cross-spoked wheels). Explorer variants feature massive 30-litre fuel tanks delivering over 550 kilometers of range, alongside Triumph's Blind Spot Radar system developed with Continental. The chassis comprises a lightweight tubular steel frame with a bolt-on aluminum subframe. Suspension is commanded by Showa semi-active electronic suspension offering up to 220mm of travel on Rally models, while braking is handled by top-tier Brembo Stylema calipers with cornering-optimized ABS.

## 5. Features and technology

The technological highlight of the 2027 model is the enhanced Active Preload Reduction feature. Activated via an intuitive handlebar button, the system lowers the rear suspension preload as the motorcycle slows to a stop, reducing seat height by up to 20mm (down to 830mm on GT and 855mm on Rally), allowing riders to comfortably plant both feet on the tarmac. Instrumentation is commanded by a 7.0-inch TFT display featuring full smartphone connectivity, turn-by-turn Google navigation, and GoPro camera control. Up to six riding modes are available (Road, Rain, Sport, Rider-Configurable, Off-Road, and Off-Road Pro). Keyless ignition, cruise control, heated grips, and heated rider and pillion seats on Explorer models come standard.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European showrooms, the 2027 Triumph Tiger 1200 range starts at approximately €19,200 for the GT Pro, rising to €23,800 for the range-topping Rally Explorer. In India, local CKD assembly enables Triumph India to price the lineup competitively, with expected ex-showroom prices ranging from Rs 19.50 lakh for the GT Pro to Rs 22.80 lakh for the Rally Explorer. Triumph positions the Tiger 1200 as the definitive heavyweight multicylinder overland explorer, undercutting the BMW R 1300 GS while delivering standard semi-active suspension and shaft-drive reliability.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Triumph Tiger 1200 competes directly at the summit of the heavyweight adventure tourer class against the BMW R 1300 GS, the Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin Adventure Sports, the Ducati Multistrada V4 S, and the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S / R. The Tiger 1200 distinguishes itself through its unique 150 PS T-Plane triple engine character, standard shaft drive, and the massive 30-litre range of its Explorer variants.
