## 1. Introduction

Honda has redefined the heavyweight adventure touring benchmark at EICMA 2026 with the world premiere of the all-new 2027 Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin. Commemorating decades of Paris-Dakar rally glory and overland dominance, the updated Africa Twin receives an enlarged 1,150cc parallel-twin engine, substantial chassis enhancements, and the global debut of Honda SENSING RIDE—an advanced radar-powered active safety suite. Recognizing the diverse requirements of modern adventure riders, Honda has structured the 2027 Africa Twin lineup across three specialized variants: the off-road-focused Standard model, the transcontinental Adventure Sports ES, and the newly introduced hardcore rally-inspired Enduro Sports variant. With increased horsepower, elevated low-end torque, and cutting-edge electronic wizardry, the CRF1150L reclaims Honda's position at the summit of global adventure touring.

## 2. Launch and Availability

The 2027 Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin made its official world debut on November 3, 2026, at the Honda Motor Europe pavilion during EICMA in Milan. Honda confirmed that commercial European deliveries will begin in February 2027 across all three variants, available with both a conventional six-speed manual gearbox and Honda's refined Dual Clutch Transmission (DCT). For the Indian market, Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India (HMSI) has officially confirmed that the CRF1150L will arrive in the third quarter of 2027, imported as a completely knocked-down (CKD) kit assembled at Honda's premium facility in Manesar, Haryana.

## 3. Engine and Performance

At the core of the 2027 model is an enlarged 1,150cc, liquid-cooled, four-stroke, Unicam 8-valve parallel-twin engine, up from the previous 1,084cc displacement. The increased capacity is achieved through a 2mm bore increase and revised piston crowns, working in tandem with an optimized combustion chamber and larger intake throttle bodies. The engine produces a commanding 119 hp (87.5 kW) at 7,750 rpm and 122 Nm of peak torque at 5,750 rpm, delivering a dramatic 15 percent boost in midrange torque between 3,000 and 5,000 rpm. Power is delivered through either a six-speed manual transmission with an assist-and-slipper clutch or a 7-speed Dual Clutch Transmission (DCT) featuring updated hydraulic shift logic for ultra-smooth low-speed trail crawls.

## 4. Design and hardware

The 2027 Africa Twin showcases sharpened Dakar-inspired bodywork with an aggressive split LED headlight assembly and integrated cornering lights. The newly introduced Enduro Sports variant features a highmounted front rally fender, a flatter competition-style enduro saddle, a lightweight titanium Akrapovič exhaust canister, and long-travel 240mm suspension for extreme off-road terrain. The Adventure Sports ES variant is tailored for transcontinental highway touring, equipped with a 19-inch front wheel for sharper asphalt tracking, an electronically adjustable windscreen, Showa EERA semi-active suspension, and a massive 24.8litre fuel tank. All models utilize a high-rigidity semi-double cradle steel frame with a bolt-on aluminum subframe and an aluminum swingarm.

## 5. Features and technology

The technological highlight of the CRF1150L is the debut of Honda SENSING RIDE, utilizing front and rear millimeter-wave radar units. The system provides Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC), Forward Collision Warning (FCW), and Blind Spot Detection (BSD) with visual mirror indicators. Instrumentation is anchored by a 6.5-inch TFT colour touchscreen compatible with Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and Bluetooth headsets. Electronic aids, governed by a six-axis IMU, include Cornering ABS, 7-level Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), 3level Wheelie Control, Rear Lift Control, and six riding modes: Tour, Urban, Gravel, Off-Road, and two customizable User modes.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

In European markets, the 2027 Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin starts at approximately €15,600 for the standard manual model, climbing to €19,800 for the Adventure Sports ES DCT with full radar technology. In India, local CKD assembly will allow HMSI to price the range competitively against imported European competitors. Anticipated ex-showroom prices in India range from Rs 16.50 lakh for the Standard manual model to Rs 19.50 lakh for the fully loaded Adventure Sports DCT, cementing its position as the premier luxury ADV value.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

The Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin competes at the pinnacle of the adventure category against the BMW R 1300 GS, the Triumph Tiger 1200, the Ducati DesertX Rally, and the KTM 1290 Super Adventure. Compared to the BMW R 1300 GS, the Africa Twin maintains superior authentic off-road agility with its 21-inch front wheel on standard and Enduro variants, while offering the unmatched convenience of its battle-tested Dual Clutch Transmission.
