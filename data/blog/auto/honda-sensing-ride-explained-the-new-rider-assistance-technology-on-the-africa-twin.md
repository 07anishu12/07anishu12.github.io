## 1. Introduction

At EICMA 2026 in Milan, Honda Motor Company unveiled the technological crown jewel of its 2027 adventure touring lineup: Honda SENSING RIDE, debuted globally as standard equipment on the flagship 2027 Honda CRF1150L Africa Twin Adventure Sports ES. While automotive active radar assistance has been common in passenger cars for a decade under Honda SENSING, adapting radar sensors and automated throttle-andbrake intervention to a high-performance two-wheeler poses immense physical challenges due to dynamic lean angles, pitch variation under braking, and rider weight shifts. With Honda SENSING RIDE, Honda has developed the world's most sophisticated dual-radar motorcycle safety suite. By integrating front and rear 77 GHz millimeter-wave radar units with a six-axis IMU and the Dual Clutch Transmission, Honda provides unprecedented levels of highway touring safety without diminishing rider autonomy.

## 2. Launch and Availability

Honda SENSING RIDE was officially presented during Honda's technical press conference at EICMA on November 3, 2026. The technology will debut commercially on the 2027 Africa Twin Adventure Sports ES in February 2027 in Europe and North America. For the Indian market, Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India (HMSI) confirmed that the radar-equipped Africa Twin will be launched in the third quarter of 2027 via CKD assembly at its Manesar plant, with subsequent expansion to other flagship Honda models planned for 2028.

## 3. Engine and Performance

The brilliance of Honda SENSING RIDE lies in its symbiotic integration with the Africa Twin's enlarged 1,150cc parallel-twin engine (119 hp, 122 Nm) and the 7-speed Dual Clutch Transmission (DCT). When Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) is engaged, the front radar monitors traffic up to 120 meters ahead. If a vehicle decelerates, the system communicates with the throttle-by-wire and DCT shift computer, smoothly downshifting gears and applying engine braking or hydraulic caliper pressure to maintain a preset following distance. When the lane clears, the system automatically upshifts and accelerates back to the cruising speed with seamless composure.

## 4. Design and hardware

Integrating radar units without compromising off-road styling or aerodynamics required meticulous packaging. The front 77 GHz radar sensor is housed discreetly behind a radio-transparent dark polycarbonate shroud situated directly beneath the twin LED headlights, impervious to mud, rain, and roost deflections. The rear radar is tucked cleanly beneath the taillight cluster inside the license plate hanger. The system feeds visual alerts to amber warning LEDs laser-etched directly into the outer glass of both rearview mirrors, ensuring riders notice blind-spot hazards without taking their eyes off the road ahead.

## 5. Features and technology

Honda SENSING RIDE delivers four core active assistance functions. First is Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC), offering four user-selectable following distance intervals. Second is Forward Collision Warning (FCW), which flashes a high-visibility warning banner across the 6.5-inch TFT touchscreen and delivers a subtle haptic pulse through the throttle grip if an impending impact is detected. Third is Blind Spot Detection (BSD), which monitors adjacent highway lanes up to 50 meters back. Fourth is Rear Collision Warning, which rapidly flashes the rear hazard lights at 4 Hz to alert inattentive following motorists if they approach the motorcycle too quickly.

## 6. Expected price and market positioning

Honda SENSING RIDE will be offered as standard equipment on the flagship Africa Twin Adventure Sports ES and as an optional package on standard models. In Europe, the radar package adds approximately €1,200 to the base price. In India, where it will be packaged into the top-tier CKD Adventure Sports DCT model, the anticipated ex-showroom price is estimated around Rs 19.50 lakh. Honda positions SENSING RIDE as an essential touring luxury for long-distance iron-butt riders navigating crowded highway networks.

## 8. Rivals and alternatives

Honda SENSING RIDE directly rivals radar systems developed by Bosch for the Ducati Multistrada V4 S, BMW R 1300 GS, and KTM 1290 Super Adventure. Honda's system stands apart through its proprietary integration with the Dual Clutch Transmission, providing fully automated downshifting and upshifting during adaptive cruise control without requiring any manual rider intervention.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Will Honda SENSING RIDE slam the brakes on automatically?

No. Unlike car systems, the motorcycle system uses gentle decelerative braking and throttle reduction to avoid upsetting motorcycle stability, prioritizing rider balance.

### Q: Does the radar work in heavy rain or mud?

Yes, 77 GHz millimeter-wave radar waves easily penetrate rain, fog, and dust, though an alert notifies the rider if thick mud physically encrusts the sensor face.

### Q: Can the radar system be turned off for off-road riding?

Yes, selecting 'Off-Road' or 'Gravel' riding modes automatically disables Adaptive Cruise Control and Forward Collision Warning to prevent false interventions on trails.

### Q: Will Honda expand SENSING RIDE to smaller bikes?

Honda plans to progressively introduce SENSING RIDE to other touring and commuter models, including the NT1100, Gold Wing, and future middleweights.
