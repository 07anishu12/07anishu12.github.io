## Detailed Segment Comparison and Speed / Traffic Impact Analysis

Real-world efficiency shifts across traffic density and riding speeds. In stop-and-go city congestion averaging 18–25 km/h, repeated acceleration and signal idling penalize heavier motorcycles and CVT scooters. Technologies such as Hero's i3S, TVS's IntelliGO and Glide Through Technology (GTT), Honda's Idling Stop System, and Yamaha's Smart Motor Generator (SMG) Hybrid assist recover 4%–8% of urban fuel economy.

On open highways, aerodynamic drag rises with the square of velocity. Maintaining top gear in the engine's peak thermal efficiency window (45–55 km/h on 100cc bikes, 55–65 km/h on 125–160cc bikes, and 70–85 km/h on 350cc bikes) maximizes tank range, whereas riding near full throttle increases fuel burn by 25%–40%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most accurate way to verify petrol vs electric two wheeler 3 year ownership cost?

Use the full-tank auto-cut to auto-cut method over 100–150 km of normal riding, dividing kilometres travelled by litres dispensed.

### Why is real-world mileage lower than ARAI or IDC claimed figures?

ARAI and IDC tests run on indoor dynamometers at controlled temperatures with zero wind drag, no potholes, and gentle throttle ramps, whereas real Indian traffic involves signal idling, gradients, and pillion loads.

### How do you calculate per-kilometre petrol and EV charging costs in India?

For petrol: divide petrol price per litre by real-world km/l (e.g., ₹105 ÷ 60 km/l = ₹1.75/km). For EVs: multiply wall kWh per km (including ~13% charging loss) by your electricity tariff per kWh (e.g., 0.042 kWh/km × ₹7.00 = ₹0.29/km).

### What cruising speed gives the best fuel efficiency on Indian roads?

Riding steadily in top gear at 45–55 km/h on 100cc–110cc bikes, 50–60 km/h on 125cc bikes, 55–65 km/h on 160cc bikes, and 40–50 km/h on CVT scooters delivers peak fuel economy.

### How much does low tyre pressure or a dirty air filter reduce mileage?

Riding with tyres 5–7 PSI under-inflated or a clogged air filter increases rolling resistance and pumping losses, reducing real-world mileage by 4–7 km/l.

### When does an electric scooter become cheaper than a petrol two-wheeler over 3 years?

When daily riding exceeds 20–25 km/day (18,000–22,000+ km over 3 years), the ₹1.50–₹1.80/km energy savings of an electric scooter offset its higher purchase price.
