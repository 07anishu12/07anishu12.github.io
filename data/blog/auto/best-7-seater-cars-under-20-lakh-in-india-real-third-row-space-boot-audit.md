If you need a genuinely usable third row for adults or tall teenagers under ₹20 lakh on-road, three-row MPVs beat mid-size SUVs every time: the Kia Carens (Prestige Plus / Luxury) and Maruti Suzuki Ertiga ZXi+ / XL6 Alpha+ are the top choices—offering one-touch second-row tumble entry, dedicated third-row AC vents, reclining third-row backrests, and 209–216 litres of boot space even with all three rows up. If you prioritise rugged SUV road presence and only use the third row for children, look at the Mahindra Scorpio-N Z4 or XUV700 AX3 7-seater.


## Why Dedicated MPVs Beat '5+2' SUVs on Third-Row Comfort Under ₹20 Lakh


A car's wheelbase and rear floor height dictate whether the third row is a usable lounge or a punishment seat. The **Kia Carens boasts a 2,780 mm wheelbase** and the **Maruti Ertiga/XL6 sits on a 2,740 mm wheelbase**—both longer than a ₹25 lakh Mahindra XUV700 (2,750 mm) or Tata Safari (2,741 mm).


More importantly, Carens and Ertiga/XL6 engineers recessed the rear floor pan downward so third-row passengers sit with natural knee angles rather than knees pushed toward their chest, and carved a deep well behind the third row that still holds **two upright cabin trolley bags (209–216 litres)** when all seven seats are occupied.

## Contender-by-Contender Third-Row & Family Evaluation

- **Kia Carens (Prestige (O) / Prestige+ / Luxury):** The most complete family 7-seater under ₹20 lakh. Pressing an electric button on the second-row shoulder tumbles the seat effortlessly for elderly entry; the third row has roof-mounted diffused AC vents, USB-C ports, cupholders, and a reclining backrest; and you can choose between 1.5L NA Petrol, 1.5L Turbo-Petrol (160 PS), or 1.5L Diesel (MT/AT).
- **Maruti Ertiga ZXi+ (7-Seater) vs Maruti XL6 Alpha+ (6-Seater):** Mechanically identical (1.5L 4-cylinder DualJet with 5MT or 6-speed Torque Converter AT). Pick the **Ertiga ZXi+ or VXi/ZXi S-CNG** if you need 7 seats and lowest running cost. Pick the **XL6 Alpha+** if you only need 6 seats and want plush second-row captain chairs, ventilated front seats, a 360-degree camera, and SUV-inspired body cladding.
- **Mahindra Scorpio-N Z4:** Gives you a towering 2.0L Turbo-Petrol (203 PS) or 2.2L Diesel ladder-frame SUV under ₹20 lakh on-road, **WAIT—check the third row carefully:** the Scorpio-N's third row lacks sliding second-row adjustment, has no dedicated third-row AC vents, and leaves virtually zero luggage space unless you fold the bench or use a roof carrier.

## 6-Seater Captain Seats vs 7-Seater Bench: Which Should You Pick?


Buy a **6-seater (Captain Seats)** only if your family has 5 or 6 members—it gives second-row passengers individual armchairs and allows children to walk straight between the seats into the third row. Buy a **7-seater (Bench Row 2)** if you ever need to carry 5 people WITH the third row folded flat for full vacation luggage.


## Clear Buying Verdict

- **Choose the Kia Carens (Prestige+ / Diesel AT / Turbo) if:** You want the best third-row comfort, electric one-touch entry, standard 6 airbags, and Diesel or Turbo options under ₹20 lakh.
- **Choose the Maruti XL6 Alpha+ AT or Ertiga ZXi+ AT if:** You want a smooth 4-cylinder petrol with a reliable 6-speed Torque Converter automatic, 15–17 km/l mileage, and Maruti service reach.
- **Choose the Mahindra XUV700 AX3 7-Seater if:** You want a 5-star crash-tested highway SUV and only use the third row for children on short outings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Can two adults actually sit in the third row of a Kia Carens or Maruti Ertiga for a 3-hour highway trip?

Yes. Because the second row in both the Carens and Ertiga slides forward and back on rails, you can share legroom evenly across all three rows so two adults up to 5 feet 8 inches tall sit comfortably in the third row with reclining backrests and overhead AC.

### Q2. What happens to luggage space in a Maruti Ertiga S-CNG?

In the Ertiga S-CNG, the 60-litre CNG cylinder sits inside the lower luggage well behind the third row. A carpeted lid covers the cylinder, leaving a shallow tray above it that fits two soft duffle bags with all 7 seats up, or full luggage when the third row is folded forward.

### Q3. Does the Kia Carens come with 6 airbags across all variants?

Yes. Every variant of the Kia Carens includes 6 airbags, Electronic Stability Control (ESC), Vehicle Stability Management, Hill-Start Assist, Downhill Brake Control, all-wheel disc brakes, and TPMS as standard.

### Q4. Is the Citroen Aircross 5+2 a good alternative under ₹16 lakh?

The Citroen Aircross (formerly C3 Aircross) 5+2 has clever completely removable third-row seats (leaving a 511-litre boot when taken out) and plush suspension comfort, though its third row itself is best suited for children rather than adults.

### Q5. Why do many private families prefer the Maruti XL6 over the Ertiga?

Because the Ertiga is heavily used by commercial yellow-plate taxi fleets across India, private buyers often pay ₹1.2–₹1.5 lakh more for the Nexa-sold XL6 to get SUV styling, an all-black cabin, captain seats, ventilated front seats, and a 360-degree camera.
