Unlike new-car loans (which cost 8.8%–9.5%), dedicated Used-Car Loans in India carry steep interest rates of 12.0% to 16.0% p.a. plus ₹5,000 to ₹9,000 in mandatory vehicle valuation, RTO hypothecation endorsement, and later Form 35 hypothecation-removal fees. If you are a salaried buyer with a 760+ credit score and a pre-approved Personal Loan offer between 10.50% and 11.75% p.a. (or an overdraft against Fixed Deposits/Mutual Funds at 8.5%–10%), using the Personal/OD Loan is faster, saves ₹25,000 to ₹48,000 on a ₹5 lakh loan, and registers the car 100% in your name with zero bank hypothecation on the RC.


## Why Used-Car Loans Cost So Much More Than New-Car Loans


When you buy a brand-new car, the invoice value is fixed and the asset is 0 years old, so banks lend at ~9.0%. On a used car, the bank must send a field valuator to inspect the car, verify past title, cap the Loan-to-Value (LTV) at 70%–80% of *their* conservative grid valuation (not your purchase price!), and price in higher default risk at **12.5% to 16.0% p.a.**


Even worse, dealerships and online platforms earn hefty origination commissions for steering buyers into 14.5%–15.5% in-house NBFC used-car loans.

## 3 Hidden Advantages of Using a Personal / OD Loan Over a Used-Car Loan

1. **Zero RTO Hypothecation Hassle (Saves ₹4,500 & 2 RTO Trips):** A Used-Car Loan stamps *'Hypothecated to XYZ Bank'* on your RC (costing ₹1,500 at purchase) and forces you to obtain a Bank NOC + Form 35 and pay another ₹1,500–₹2,500 to remove the hypothecation at the RTO when the loan ends. With a Personal Loan, the car is bought as a **100% cash transaction**—the RC is clean in your name from Day 1!
1. **Instant Cash Bargaining Power with Direct Owners:** Individual sellers hate waiting 8–12 working days for a buyer's used-car loan bank to inspect the car and disburse a demand draft after RC transfer. A Personal Loan drops ₹5,00,000 into your own bank account in 2 hours, letting you negotiate like a cash buyer.
1. **No Age or Model Restrictions:** Banks refuse used-car loans on cars older than 7–8 years or at loan finish past 10–12 years; a Personal Loan has zero vehicle restrictions.

## When a Dedicated Used-Car Loan Still Makes Sense

- **If your Personal Loan quote is >13.5% p.a.** (common for self-employed borrowers without corporate salary tie-ups, where a secured PSU bank used-car loan at 11.5%–12.5% from SBI/Bank of Baroda/Canara Bank is cheaper).
- **If you want to preserve your unsecured borrowing limit** for medical or family emergencies.

## Clear Financing Action Plan

- **Check 1 (Cheapest):** Do you have a Fixed Deposit or Debt/Equity Mutual Fund portfolio? A **Loan/Overdraft Against FD (+1% over FD rate = ~8.0%–8.5%)** or **Loan Against Mutual Funds (~10.0%–10.5%)** has zero processing fee, zero foreclosure penalty, and the lowest interest rate in India.
- **Check 2:** Compare your salary-account bank's **Pre-Approved Personal Loan APR** against a **PSU Bank (SBI/BoB) Used-Car Loan APR**—never sign a 15% dealer NBFC loan without checking your own bank first!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why do used-car dealers push their in-house Used-Car Loan even if I want to pay cash?

Because NBFCs and private banks pay used-car dealerships a 2% to 4% payout commission on the financed amount. Sometimes a dealer will offer a ₹15,000 'discount on the car' if you take their loan—always check the loan's interest rate and foreclosure lock-in clause before falling for that discount!

### Q2. What is the maximum age of a car that banks will finance with a Used-Car Loan?

Most Indian banks enforce two rules: (1) the car cannot be more than 7 to 8 years old at the time of buying, and (2) the Car's Age + Loan Tenure cannot exceed 10 years (or 12 years at select NBFCs). So on a 6-year-old car, you can only get a 4-year Used-Car Loan.

### Q3. Why does the bank approve only ₹4.2 lakh when I am buying the used car for ₹5.5 lakh?

On a Used-Car Loan, banks calculate their 80% Loan-to-Value (LTV) based on their internal depreciated grid value or Insurance IDV—not your agreed purchase price. If their grid values the car at ₹5.0 lakh, 80% LTV is ₹4.0 lakh, forcing you to pay ₹1.5 lakh as down payment.

### Q4. Are there prepayment or foreclosure penalties on Used-Car Loans and Personal Loans?

Private banks and NBFCs typically charge a 3% to 5% + GST foreclosure fee on the outstanding principal if you close a fixed-rate Used-Car Loan or Personal Loan early (and often prohibit closure in the first 6–12 months). PSU banks (like SBI) on floating-rate loans or Overdrafts against FD/Mutual Funds usually charge zero prepayment penalty.

### Q5. Does a Personal Loan or a Used-Car Loan give any income tax benefit for salaried employees?

Neither a Personal Loan nor a Used-Car Loan offers income tax deductions for a salaried employee buying a private combustion car (Section 80EEB applied only to EV loans sanctioned up to March 2023). Only registered businesses/professionals can claim loan interest as a business expense.
