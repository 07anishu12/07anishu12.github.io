For senior citizens—especially those with knee osteo-arthritis or back stiffness—the single most important measurement is 'Hip-Point (H-Point) Seat Height': avoid both low-slung sedans (where you have to drop down and push up from a squat) and tall ladder-frame SUVs like the Scorpio-N or Fortuner (where you have to climb a high side-step). Instead, choose a 'walk-in chair-height' crossover or tall-boy vehicle where the seat cushion sits right at hip level. For self-driving seniors, the Maruti WagonR ZXi AGS, Maruti Ignis Zeta AGS, Hyundai Exter AMT, and Maruti Brezza VXi/ZXi AT are easiest to see out of and park. For elderly parents riding in the back seat, the Kia Carens (6/7-str), Maruti XL6 (captain seats), Hyundai Creta IVT, and Toyota Innova Hycross are the gold standard.

## 3 Showroom Checks Before Buying for Seniors

1. **Insist on Physical AC & Volume Knobs:** Avoid cars that bury fan speed and defoggers inside multi-layer touchscreen menus—tactile rotary dials are far safer for older eyes.
1. **Check for a Cooled Glovebox:** Helpful for keeping insulin pens or temperature-sensitive medications cool on long trips.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Why are tall ladder-frame SUVs like the Mahindra Scorpio-N and Toyota Fortuner difficult for elderly parents?

Because a body-on-frame SUV mounts its cabin floor on top of a thick steel ladder chassis, placing the seat cushion 780 to 850 mm above the ground. Entering requires stepping up onto a narrow metal running board with one leg while pulling your body weight upward via the A-pillar grab handle—which is painful and risky for anyone with knee arthritis or balance issues.

### Q2. What simple ₹400 accessory makes getting in and out of any car much easier for seniors?

A portable 'Car Door Latch Assist Handle' (HandyBar)! It is a forged metal/rubber handle that slips temporarily into the U-shaped metal striker latch on the B-pillar door frame when the door is open—giving an elderly person a rock-solid waist-level support bar to push up from when standing up.

### Q3. Why should self-driving seniors always choose a car with a Height-Adjustable Driver's Seat and Auto-Dimming Mirror?

As we age, spine posture compresses slightly and night-time pupil recovery from oncoming high-beam glare slows down. A height-adjustable seat raises the driver's eye line clearly over the dashboard, and an Auto-Dimming Inside Rearview Mirror (Electrochromic IRVM) automatically darkens blinding LED high beams from cars behind.

### Q4. Is the MG Windsor EV or Comet EV good for senior citizens?

The MG Windsor EV has a flat floor, wide-opening rear doors, and ultra-spacious reclining rear seats that are wonderful for elderly rear-seat passengers, though self-driving seniors may find its touchscreen-heavy controls less intuitive than physical buttons in a Brezza or Exter.

### Q5. Should we choose 15/16-inch tyres over 17/18-inch tyres if our parents have lower-back pain?

Yes. Mid-variants riding on 15-inch or 16-inch wheels with taller 60- or 65-profile tyre sidewalls absorb sharp municipal road joints and potholes far more gently than top-variants on low-profile 17-inch or 18-inch wheels.
