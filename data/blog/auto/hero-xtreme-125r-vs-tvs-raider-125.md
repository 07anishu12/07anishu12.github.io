## City Traffic Usability and Highway Cruising Behaviour

In stop-and-go city traffic, kerb weight and idle-saving tech determine fuel burn. The Hero Xtreme 125R features Segment-first single-channel ABS option, 37mm telescopic forks, 120/80-17 wide radial rear tyre, and Showa rear monoshock, returning 54–58 km/l in urban use. The TVS Raider 125 counters with 3-valve air-and-oil-cooled engine, two ride modes (Eco with IntelliGO stop-start and Power), under-seat storage, and 5-step monoshock, achieving 55–59 km/l.
