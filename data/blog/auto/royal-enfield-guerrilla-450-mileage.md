## City Traffic Behaviour, Highway Cruising and Full-Tank Measurement

In urban traffic, accelerating 184 kg of motorcycle plus rider from repeated stops holds fuel economy to 25–28 km/l. On open highways at 95–115 km/h in top gear, the engine runs unstressed and achieves 29–32 km/l, whereas pushing toward its 150 km/h top speed increases aerodynamic drag and drops mileage by 4–6 km/l.
