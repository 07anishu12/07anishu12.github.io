To transfer ownership of a used car safely in India, complete the process in three synchronized stages: first, at the moment of payment and delivery, log into `parivahan.gov.in` with the seller to submit the online Transfer of Ownership application (Form 29 and Form 30) using Aadhaar OTP authentication and sign a time-stamped Delivery Note; second, submit the physical original RC, PUC, Insurance, and Pencil Chassis Print to the RTO (if your state requires physical file submission); and third, immediately apply to transfer the Motor Insurance Policy into your name within 14 days so your Own-Damage crash coverage remains legally valid.


## Stage 1: The Complete Document & RTO Forms Checklist


Before initiating payment, assemble this exact document folder with the seller:

- **Original Registration Certificate (Smart Card RC):** Verify no active bank hypothecation is printed on it (if hypothecated, you also need **2 copies of Form 35 + Original Bank NOC**).
- **Form 29 (2 Copies):** *Notice of Transfer of Ownership of a Motor Vehicle* (signed by Seller).
- **Form 30 (2 Copies):** *Application for Intimation and Transfer of Ownership* (signed by BOTH Seller and Buyer).
- **Form 28 (3 Copies — ONLY if Inter-State / Different RTO where required):** *No Objection Certificate (NOC)*.
- **Pencil Print of Chassis Number (3 Tracings):** Rubbed with a lead pencil on tracing paper directly over the stamped metal VIN/Chassis number on the car's body and pasted onto Form 29/30.
- **Valid Insurance Policy Copy + Valid PUC Certificate**.
- **Self-Attested KYC of Seller & Buyer:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card (or Form 60), and 2 Passport Photos each.

A FASTag is linked to the seller's personal PAN/KYC wallet. At delivery, have the seller close their FASTag account via their issuing bank app, peel off the old windshield tag, and apply for a fresh FASTag using your own RC/Transfer Receipt and KYC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. What if the seller's mobile number is not updated on the Parivahan portal?

Before starting the Transfer of Ownership application, click 'Update Mobile Number' under Online Services on `parivahan.gov.in`. The seller can update their Aadhaar-linked mobile number online in 2 minutes using their Engine No., Chassis No., and Aadhaar OTP.

### Q2. Can I buy a completely new comprehensive insurance policy in my own name on Day 1 instead of transferring the old owner's policy?

Yes! And if the seller's existing policy has only 1 to 2 months left (or is from an obscure insurer), buying a brand-new 1-year Comprehensive Policy in your own name starting on Delivery Day (using the Form 29/30 + Parivahan Transfer Fee Receipt) is often cleaner, cheaper, and gives you full 12-month coverage immediately.

### Q3. How can I use my own existing 50% No Claim Bonus (NCB) when buying a used car?

If you recently sold your previous car, ask your old insurer for an NCB Retention Certificate (valid for 3 years) after transferring the old car's policy to its buyer. You can submit that NCB certificate to claim up to 50% off the Own-Damage premium on your newly purchased used car!

### Q4. Do signatures on Form 29 and Form 30 have to match the seller's old signature in RTO records?

If your state uses non-Aadhaar physical verification at the RTO, the seller's signature on Form 29 and Form 30 must strictly match the signature uploaded when they originally bought the car (usually matching their PAN card or Driving Licence signature). Using Aadhaar e-Sign / OTP faceless transfer bypasses manual signature-mismatch rejections.

### Q5. How do I check when the RC has officially transferred into my name?

Download the official mParivahan or DigiLocker app and search the vehicle registration number. The moment the RTO clerk approves the file (often within 3–10 working days on faceless portals, even before the physical plastic card arrives by speed post), the digital RC on mParivahan updates to show your name as Owner.
