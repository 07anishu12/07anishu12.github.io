## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Do I need to upgrade my home electricity meter load for a 45 kWh to 59 kWh EV?

If you drive 40–70 km per day, you can still use a 3.3 kW 15-Amp socket overnight (adding ~22 kWh in 7 hours = ~150 km recovered every night) without upgrading your meter load! However, if you regularly return from weekend trips at 15% and want a full 100% charge by Monday morning, installing the included 7.2 kW AC Wallbox Charger (which requires a 3-phase meter with ~7–8 kW sanctioned load) charges a 45–55 kWh pack in 6.5 to 7.5 hours.

### Q2. What is V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) and V2V (Vehicle-to-Vehicle) on the Nexon EV 45, Curvv EV, and Windsor?

Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) lets you plug a standard 3-pin extension board into your car's charging port to output up to 3.3 kVA of 230V AC power—capable of running laptops, camping lights, a coffee maker, or even home fans/Wi-Fi during a power cut. V2V lets you send emergency charge to another stranded EV.

### Q3. Why is the Mahindra BE 6 faster to charge on highways than older EVs?

Because the BE 6 is built on a dedicated high-voltage Born-EV platform using BYD Blade LFP cells that accept up to 140 kW DC input (on the 59 kWh pack). If you plug into a 120 kW highway fast charger, it jumps from 20% to 80% in just 20 minutes—roughly the time it takes to order tea and use the washroom.

### Q4. Is the rear seat of the Tata Curvv EV comfortable for tall 6-foot passengers?

Because the Curvv EV has a sloping coupe roofline and a battery floor underneath, passengers up to 5 feet 9 inches sit comfortably, while 6-foot+ passengers will find rear headroom and under-thigh angle slightly tighter than in the upright MG Windsor EV or MG ZS EV.

### Q5. How does the Maruti Suzuki e Vitara fit into the sub-₹20 lakh EV segment?

Built on a dedicated Born-EV skateboard platform with 49 kWh and 61 kWh BYD Blade LFP battery packs and backed by Maruti's nationwide service reach, the 49 kWh e Vitara (~₹13.6–₹17.5 lakh ex-showroom) is an appealing choice for conservative family buyers who prioritise Maruti service support.
