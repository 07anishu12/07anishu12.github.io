The TVS Ronin features a 225.9cc powertrain (20.1 bhp @ 7,750 rpm, 19.93 Nm @ 3,750 rpm) weighing 160 kg and claiming 42.95 km/l. Their 21 kg weight difference and gearing (5-speed constant mesh vs 5-speed with Assist and Slipper Clutch) drive their real-world efficiency gap.
