## Speed-Wise Fuel Economy, Top Speed (130 km/h) and Gearing

Aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed, rapidly increasing fuel demand above 65 km/h. At 35–45 km/h in 4th or top gear, the Yamaha MT-15 V2 operates under light load and returns 50–52 km/l unless traffic forces frequent downshifts.
