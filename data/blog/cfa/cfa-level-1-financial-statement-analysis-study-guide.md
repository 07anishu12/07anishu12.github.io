## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Reconciling Accrual Net Income, Dividends, and Balance Sheet Equity

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A firm starts the year with Total Assets = $800M and Total Liabilities = $500M. During the year, Revenue = $450M, Total Expenses = $370M, Dividends Declared = $20M, and Other Comprehensive Income (OCI) = −$5M (no share issuance/buybacks). Find Ending Total Equity.

```formula
Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Ending Retained Earnings = Beginning RE + Net Income − Dividends
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Beginning Equity = $800M − $500M = $300M. Net Income = $450M − $370M = $80M. Addition to Retained Earnings = $80M − $20M = $60M. Total Comprehensive Income = $80M − $5M = $75M. Ending Equity = $300M + $80M − $20M − $5M = $355M.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Total Comprehensive Income ($75M) minus dividends ($20M) accounts for the exact $55M net expansion in shareholders' equity.

### Worked Example 2: Computing the Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) from Working Capital Turnover

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A retailer reports Inventory Turnover = 6.0x, Receivables Turnover = 12.17x, and Payables Turnover = 7.30x (using a 365-day year).

```formula
CCC = DOH + DSO − DPO

g = ROE × (1 − Dividend Payout Ratio)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

DOH = 365 / 6.0 = 60.83 days; DSO = 365 / 12.17 = 30.00 days; DPO = 365 / 7.30 = 50.00 days. CCC = 60.83 + 30.00 − 50.00 = 40.83 days.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> The firm must finance 40.83 days of operating working capital between paying suppliers and collecting cash from customers.

## Comparison & Reference Analysis

| Reporting Area | IFRS Treatment (IASB) | US GAAP Treatment (FASB) | CFA Analytical Adjustment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inventory Costing | LIFO strictly prohibited (IAS 2) | LIFO permitted (requires LIFO reserve) | Add LIFO reserve to FIFO inventory |
| Inventory Write-Downs | Reversal permitted up to original cost | Reversal strictly prohibited | Normalize gross margin on reversals |
| Long-Lived Asset Valuation | Cost model or Revaluation model (IAS 16) | Cost model only (revaluation barred) | Adjust equity & depreciation for reval |

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** Under IFRS (IAS 1), which of the following items is classified within Other Comprehensive Income (OCI) rather than the income statement?

- **[A]** Realized gains on trading securities

- **[B]** Actuarialremeasurement gains and losses on defined benefit pension plans

- **[C]** Restructuring expenses from plant closures

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Remeasurements of defined benefit pension plans (and revaluation surpluses on PP&E or FVOCI securities) bypass net income and flow directly into OCI.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** An independent auditor discovers a material misstatement isolated to inventory valuation, while the rest of the financial statements are fairly presented. The auditor will most likely issue a(n):

- **[A]** Unqualified (unmodified) opinion

- **[B]** Qualified opinion ('except for')

- **[C]** Adverse opinion

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** A qualified opinion is issued when a material—but not pervasive—departure from accounting standards or scope limitation exists. An adverse opinion applies only when misstatements are both material and pervasive.

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** A company has a Return on Equity (ROE) of 16.0% and pays out 35.0% of its net income as cash dividends. Its sustainable growth rate (g) is closest to:

- **[A]** 5.60%

- **[B]** 10.40%

- **[C]** 16.00%

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Retention ratio b = 1 − 0.35 = 0.65. Sustainable growth rate g = ROE × b = 16.0% × 0.65 = 10.40%.

## Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

- **Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Fundamental Accounting Identity & Accrual Net Income.**

- **Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) & Sustainable Growth Rate.**

- **Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.**
