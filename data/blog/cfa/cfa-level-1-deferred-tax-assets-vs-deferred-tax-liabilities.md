## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Calculating Tax Base, DTL, DTA, and Total Income Tax Expense

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A firm buys machinery for $300,000. In Year 1, financial accounting uses 5-year straight-line depreciation ($60,000), while the tax code allows 40% accelerated tax depreciation ($120,000). The firm also records $20,000 of warranty expense on the income statement (zero cash paid yet, so tax deduction is $0). Taxable income (after tax depreciation) is $500,000, and the statutory tax rate is 25%.

```formula
Income Tax Expense = Taxes Payable + ΔDTL − ΔDTA

DTL or DTA = |Carrying Amount − Tax Base| × Enacted Tax Rate
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Machinery: Carrying Amount = $300,000 − $60,000 = $240,000; Tax Base = $300,000 − $120,000 = $180,000. Since Asset Carrying ($240k) > Tax Base ($180k), DTL = ($240,000 − $180,000) × 25% = $15,000. Warranty Liability: Carrying = $20,000; Tax Base = $0. Since Liability Carrying > Tax Base, DTA = $20,000 × 25% = $5,000. Current Taxes Payable = $500,000 × 25% = $125,000. Total Income Tax Expense = $125,000 + $15,000 − $5,000 = $135,000 (matching Pre-Tax Income $540,000 × 25%).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Accelerated tax depreciation creates a $15,000 DTL, while accrued warranty expense creates a $5,000 DTA.

### Worked Example 2: Effect of a Statutory Corporate Tax Rate Reduction on Existing DTL and DTA

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A firm holds an existing DTL of $60M and a DTA of $24M calculated at an old statutory tax rate of 30% (implying $200M taxable temporary difference and $80M deductible temporary difference). Parliament enacts a new corporate tax rate of 25%.

```formula
Asset: Carrying > Tax Base => DTL (Carrying < Tax Base => DTA)

Liability: Carrying > Tax Base => DTA
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

New DTL = $200M × 25% = $50M (ΔDTL = −$10M). New DTA = $80M × 25% = $20M (ΔDTA = −$4M). Net impact on current-year Income Tax Expense = ΔDTL − ΔDTA = −$10M − (−$4M) = −$6M (increases Net Income by +$6M).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Because the firm's DTL exceeded its DTA, cutting the statutory tax rate reduces net deferred tax liabilities by $6M and immediately lowers current-period Income Tax Expense by $6M.

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** A company collects $40,000 of subscription cash in advance, which is taxed immediately upon receipt by the tax authority but deferred as Unearned Revenue on the accounting balance sheet. At a 25% tax rate, this creates a:

- **[A]** Deferred Tax Liability of $10,000

- **[B]** Deferred Tax Asset of $10,000

- **[C]** Permanent tax difference with an effective tax rate change

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** For a liability paid/taxed in advance, the accounting Carrying Amount is $40,000 while the Tax Base is $0 (Carrying Amount − future non-taxable revenue = $40k − $40k = $0). Liability Carrying > Tax Base creates a DTA of $40,000 × 25% = $10,000.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** If management increases the Valuation Allowance against its Deferred Tax Assets by $12 million during the year, what is the immediate effect on the financial statements?

- **[A]** Income tax expense increases by $12 million and net income decreases by $12 million

- **[B]** Taxes payable to the government increase by $12 million

- **[C]** Deferred tax liabilities decrease by $12 million

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Increasing the contra-asset Valuation Allowance reduces net DTAs, which directly increases Income Tax Expense and reduces Net Income (with no effect on current cash Taxes Payable).

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** A corporation has $200 million of Pre-Tax Accounting Income, which includes $20 million of tax-exempt government bond interest and $10 million of non-deductible regulatory fines. If the statutory tax rate is 25%, the company's effective tax rate is closest to:

- **[A]** 23.75%

- **[B]** 25.00%

- **[C]** 26.25%

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Taxable balance after permanent differences = $200M − $20M + $10M = $190M. Income Tax Expense = $190M × 25% = $47.5M. Effective Tax Rate = $47.5M / $200M = 23.75%.
