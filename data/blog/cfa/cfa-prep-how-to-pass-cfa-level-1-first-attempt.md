## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** Why is Quantitative Methods universally recommended as the FIRST subject to study when beginning CFA Level I preparation?

- **[A]** Because Quantitative Methods has the highest exam weight

- **[B]** Because Time Value of Money (TVM), probability, and statistics are prerequisite building blocks used directly inside Fixed Income bond pricing, Equity DCF valuation, Corporate Issuers capital budgeting, Derivatives, and Portfolio Management

- **[C]** Because Quantitative Methods has no formulas

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Mastering TVM, EAR, discounting, and standard deviation/covariance in Week 1 makes Fixed Income, Equity, Corporate Issuers, and Portfolio Management much faster to learn.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** What is the single most effective cure for the 'forgetting curve' (forgetting early subjects like Quant and FSA while studying later subjects 3 months later)?

- **[A]** Waiting until the night before the exam to review Quant and FSA

- **[B]** Scheduling a weekly or bi-weekly cumulative mixed Q-Bank quiz (30–50 questions) covering all previously completed subjects throughout the study period

- **[C]** Re-reading the entire textbook three times

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Interleaved spaced repetition—testing yourself on earlier topics every 1–2 weeks—forces active memory retrieval and locks formulas into long-term memory.

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** When solving practice questions in the CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem (LES), what should a candidate do immediately after getting a question wrong or guessing a question right?

- **[A]** Move on immediately to maximize question count

- **[B]** Read the full solution explanation for all three options (A, B, and C), write the missed concept or formula in a dedicated Error Log, and re-solve a similar problem within 48 hours

- **[C]** Delete the question from the Q-Bank

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Notice that lucky guesses must be treated just like wrong answers during review: logging every missed or guessed concept in an Error Notebook turns weaknesses into guaranteed points.
