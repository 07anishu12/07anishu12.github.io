### Formula 1: CFA Level I Time Allocation & Pacing Equation

```formula
Total Questions = 180 MCQs (90 in Session 1 + 90 in Session 2)

Total Exam Time = 270 Minutes (135 Min/Session)

Average Time per Question = 135 × 60 / 90 = 90 Seconds (1.5 Minutes)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Of the 180 questions, 160 are scored operational items and 20 are unscored trial (pretest) items distributed indistinguishably across both sessions.

### Formula 2: Expected Score Under Zero Negative Marking (3-Option A, B, C Format)

```formula
Penalty for Wrong Answer = 0 Points

Expected Value of Pure Random Guess = (1 / 3) × (+1) + (2 / 3) × (0) = +0.333 Points

Expected Value after Eliminating 1 Option = +0.500 Points
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Because CFA Institute deducts zero marks for incorrect answers, leaving any question blank on exam day is a mathematical error.

### Worked Example 2: Expected Score Gain from Strategic Elimination vs. Blank Responses

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A candidate is 100% confident on 140 of the 180 questions (getting 85% = 119 right). On the remaining 40 difficult questions, the candidate eliminates 1 clearly wrong option out of {A, B, C} on each question and guesses between the remaining 2 options.

```formula
Penalty for Wrong Answer = 0 Points

Expected Value of Pure Random Guess = (1 / 3) × (+1) + (2 / 3) × (0) = +0.333 Points

Expected Value after Eliminating 1 Option = +0.500 Points
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Expected additional correct answers from 40 two-option educated guesses = 40 × (1 / 2) = +20.0 questions (vs +13.3 for pure 3-option guesses and +0.0 if left blank). Total Expected Raw Score = (119 + 20) / 180 = 139 / 180 = 77.22% (comfortably above historical Minimum Passing Scores of ~65%–70%).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Eliminating just one distractor raises your probability of a correct answer from 33.3% to 50.0%, adding +6.7 raw score points across 40 tough questions.
