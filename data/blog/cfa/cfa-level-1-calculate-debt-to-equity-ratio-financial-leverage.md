## Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

> [!NOTE]
> The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

### Formula 1: Debt-to-Equity, Debt-to-Capital & Financial Leverage

```formula
D/E = Total Debt / Total Equity

Debt-to-Capital = Total Debt / (Total Debt + Total Equity)

Leverage = Avg Assets / Avg Equity
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Total Debt equals Short-Term Interest-Bearing Debt + Current Portion of Long-Term Debt + Long-Term Interest-Bearing Debt (plus lease liabilities).

### Formula 2: Interest Coverage & Fixed Charge Coverage Ratios

```formula
Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense

FCCR = (EBIT + Lease Payments) / (Interest + Lease Payments)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Measures how many times operating profit covers mandatory debt service and fixed lease obligations.

## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Computing Solvency and Coverage Ratios from a Corporate Balance Sheet

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A firm reports: Accounts Payable = $80M, Accrued Taxes = $30M, Short-Term Notes Payable = $50M, Long-Term Bonds = $350M, Total Shareholders' Equity = $400M. Total Assets = $910M. EBIT = $112M, Interest Expense = $28M, and Lease Payments = $12M.

```formula
D/E = Total Debt / Total Equity

Debt-to-Capital = Total Debt / (Total Debt + Total Equity)

Leverage = Avg Assets / Avg Equity
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Total Interest-Bearing Debt = $50M + $350M = $400M (exclude non-interest AP and Accrued Taxes). Debt-to-Equity = $400M / $400M = 1.00x (100%). Debt-to-Capital = $400M / ($400M + $400M) = 50.0%. Financial Leverage Multiplier = $910M / $400M = 2.275x. Interest Coverage = $112M / $28M = 4.00x. Fixed Charge Coverage (FCCR) = ($112M + $12M) / ($28M + $12M) = $124M / $40M = 3.10x.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Adding $12M of fixed lease payments reduces coverage from 4.00x (Interest Coverage) to 3.10x (Fixed Charge Coverage).

### Worked Example 2: Balance Sheet and Solvency Impact of Lessee Accounting (IFRS 16 / ASC 842)

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A lessee signs a 4-year equipment lease requiring $10M annual end-of-year payments at an implicit discount rate of 6.0%. Compute the initial Right-of-Use (ROU) asset and lease liability, and compare Year 1 expense under an IFRS 16 finance lease vs. US GAAP operating lease.

```formula
Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense

FCCR = (EBIT + Lease Payments) / (Interest + Lease Payments)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Initial PV of Lease = $10M × [1 − 1.06^(−4)] / 0.06 = $34.65M (both ROU Asset and Lease Liability = $34.65M). Year 1 Interest = 6% × $34.65M = $2.08M; Lease Principal Repayment = $10.00M − $2.08M = $7.92M. Under IFRS 16 (all leases treated like finance leases): ROU Depreciation = $34.65M / 4 = $8.66M (in EBIT) + $2.08M Interest = $10.74M total Year 1 expense (EBIT is higher by $10M − $8.66M = $1.34M). Under US GAAP Operating Lease: Single lease expense = $10.00M inside EBIT (ROU amortization = $10.00M − $2.08M = $7.92M).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Both IFRS 16 and US GAAP ASC 842 capitalize operating leases onto the balance sheet (+$34.65M Debt and Assets), increasing D/E and Leverage.

## Comparison & Reference Analysis

| Solvency / Coverage Metric | Exact Formula | Direction Indicating Stronger Credit | Treatment of Trade Payables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Debt-to-Equity (D/E) | Total Debt / Total Equity | Lower | Excluded from Total Debt numerator |
| Debt-to-Capital | Total Debt / (Total Debt + Equity) | Lower | Excluded from both Debt & Capital |
| Financial Leverage Multiplier | Average Total Assets / Average Equity | Lower | Included inside Total Assets |
| Interest Coverage Ratio | EBIT / Interest Payments | Higher | Unaffected by non-interest payables |

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** If a company's Debt-to-Capital ratio is 40.0%, its Debt-to-Equity ratio is closest to:

- **[A]** 0.40x

- **[B]** 0.60x

- **[C]** 0.67x

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option C.**
>
> **Explanation:** Debt-to-Capital = D / (D + E) = 0.40, which means Debt = 40% of capital and Equity = 60% of capital. Thus D/E = 0.40 / 0.60 = 0.6667x ≈ 0.67x.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** Under IFRS 16, how does a lessee account for a 5-year warehouse lease compared to a US GAAP operating lease?

- **[A]** IFRS 16 splits the expense into ROU depreciation (in EBIT) and interest expense, resulting in higher EBIT and higher CFO than a US GAAP operating lease

- **[B]** IFRS 16 keeps operating leases off the balance sheet

- **[C]** IFRS 16 records a single straight-line lease expense in operating expenses

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Under IFRS 16, all non-exempt leases are accounted for under the finance lease model: depreciation is in EBIT, interest is below EBIT, and principal payments are in CFF (whereas US GAAP operating leases put the entire cash outflow in CFO and single lease expense in EBIT).

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** A corporation has EBIT of $90 million, Depreciation of $25 million, Interest Expense of $18 million, and Lease Payments of $12 million. Its Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio is closest to:

- **[A]** 3.40x

- **[B]** 4.23x

- **[C]** 5.00x

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio = (EBIT + Lease Payments) / (Interest Expense + Lease Payments) = ($90M + $12M) / ($18M + $12M) = $102M / $30M = 3.40x.

## Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

- **Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in Debt-to-Equity, Debt-to-Capital & Financial Leverage.**

- **Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in Interest Coverage & Fixed Charge Coverage Ratios.**

- **Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.**
