## Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

> [!NOTE]
> The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

### Formula 1: Cash-to-Income Ratio & Balance Sheet Accruals Ratio

```formula
Cash-to-Income = CFO / Operating Income

Accruals = Net Income − CFO
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** When Net Income consistently exceeds CFO (Cash-to-Income < 1.0 or rising positive accruals), earnings are driven by accounting estimates rather than cash.

### Formula 2: Receivables & Inventory Red-Flag Growth Spreads

```formula
DSO Spike = (AR_t / Revenue_t) × 365 − (AR_{t−1} / Revenue_{t−1}) × 365
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** A sharp jump in DSO relative to revenue growth signals premature revenue recognition, bill-and-hold sales, or channel stuffing.

## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Detecting Channel Stuffing and Aggressive Capitalization via Ratio Divergence

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
Company Alpha grows reported Revenue by 25% (from $400M to $500M) and Net Income by 30% (from $40M to $52M). However, Accounts Receivable jumps by 75% (from $60M to $105M), and Operating Cash Flow (CFO) drops from $48M to $18M.

```formula
Cash-to-Income = CFO / Operating Income

Accruals = Net Income − CFO
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Year 1 DSO = (60 / 400) × 365 = 54.75 days; Year 2 DSO = (105 / 500) × 365 = 76.65 days (+21.90 days spike!). Year 1 CFO/NI = 48 / 40 = 1.20x; Year 2 CFO/NI = 18 / 52 = 0.35x. Total Accruals (NI − CFO) swung from −$8M to +$34M.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> The 21.9-day jump in DSO coupled with CFO/NI collapsing to 0.35x is a textbook forensic signature of channel stuffing (recognizing uncollected year-end credit sales).

### Worked Example 2: Evaluating Conservative vs. Aggressive Accounting Policy Choices

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
Firm B extends the estimated useful life of its fleet from 6 years to 10 years (Gross Fleet = $300M, zero salvage) and reduces its Bad Debt Allowance from 4.0% to 1.5% of $200M Receivables.

```formula
DSO Spike = (AR_t / Revenue_t) × 365 − (AR_{t−1} / Revenue_{t−1}) × 365
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Fleet Depreciation reduction = ($300M / 6) − ($300M / 10) = $50M − $30M = +$20M boost to EBIT. Bad Debt Expense reduction = (4.0% − 1.5%) × $200M = +$5M boost to EBIT. Total discretionary pre-tax earnings boost = +$25M with $0 increase in economic cash flow.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Manipulating subjective estimates (asset lives and credit loss allowances) inflates current EBIT by $25M while creating future write-down hangovers.

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** In the CFA curriculum's Financial Reporting Quality framework, the highest-quality corporate reporting combines:

- **[A]** High reporting quality (GAAP-compliant, decision-useful) and high earnings quality (sustainable, adequate return > WACC)

- **[B]** Conservative accounting biases that intentionally understate current earnings

- **[C]** Aggressive Non-GAAP pro-forma adjustments that smooth quarterly volatility

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** High overall quality requires both faithful, unbiased representation (reporting quality) and sustainable cash-backed returns exceeding the cost of capital (results/earnings quality).

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** Which combination of financial statement trends is the strongest warning sign of premature or fictitious revenue recognition?

- **[A]** Net Income growing 15%, CFO growing 18%, and Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) declining by 4 days

- **[B]** Net Income growing 20%, CFO declining 30%, and Accounts Receivable growing twice as fast as Revenue

- **[C]** Gross margin expanding while Inventory Turnover increases

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** When Revenue and Net Income surge while CFO declines and Accounts Receivable balloons relative to sales, booked revenues are not converting into cash.

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** If management improperly classifies routine store maintenance expenses as capital expenditures (PP&E additions), what is the immediate impact on the current period's statement of cash flows?

- **[A]** Operating cash flow (CFO) is overstated, Investing cash flow (CFI) is understated, and total cash change is unaffected

- **[B]** Both CFO and Free Cash Flow to the Firm (FCFF) are overstated

- **[C]** CFO is understated and CFI is overstated

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Shifting cash outflows from operating maintenance (CFO) to CapEx (CFI) inflates reported CFO and depresses CFI by the exact same dollar amount, leaving Free Cash Flow (CFO − CapEx) and total cash unchanged.
