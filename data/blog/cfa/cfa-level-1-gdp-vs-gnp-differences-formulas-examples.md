## Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

### Formula 1: GDP vs. GNP (Gross National Income) Identity

```formula
GNP = GDP + Net Foreign Factor Income (NFFI) = GDP + (Factor Income Earned Abroad by Residents − Factor Income Paid to Foreigners Domestically)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:**

- GDP measures output produced within a nation's geographic borders.

- GNP measures output earned by a nation's citizens/firms worldwide.

### Formula 2: Fundamental Macroeconomic Savings-Investment-Trade Identity

```formula
(X − M) = (S − I) + (T − G)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Proves that a trade deficit (X − M < 0) must be financed by either a private savings shortfall (S < I) or a government fiscal deficit (T < G).

## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Reconciling GDP and GNP for a Host Economy vs. Net Foreign Investor Economy

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
Country Alpha has Domestic Output (GDP) = $1,850B. Citizens and corporations of Country Alpha earn $95B in dividends, interest, and wages from overseas subsidiaries, while foreign multinationals operating inside Country Alpha earn $140B in profits and wages.

```formula
GNP = GDP + Net Foreign Factor Income (NFFI) = GDP + (Factor Income Earned Abroad by Residents − Factor Income Paid to Foreigners Domestically)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Net Foreign Factor Income (NFFI) = $95B − $140B = −$45B. Country Alpha's GNP (GNI) = $1,850B + (−$45B) = $1,805B.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Economies with heavy inward foreign direct investment (such as Ireland or Singapore) have GDP > GNP because foreign multinationals produce output inside the country's borders.

### Worked Example 2: Applying the Fundamental Macro Identity to Trade and Fiscal Balances

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
An economy reports Private Domestic Saving (S) = $900B, Private Domestic Investment (I) = $1,050B, Government Tax Revenue (T) = $700B, and Government Spending (G) = $820B. Calculate Net Exports (X − M).

```formula
(X − M) = (S − I) + (T − G)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Private Sector Balance (S − I) = $900B − $1,050B = −$150B. Fiscal Balance (T − G) = $700B − $820B = −$120B (fiscal deficit). Trade Balance (X − M) = (S − I) + (T − G) = −$150B + (−$120B) = −$270B (trade deficit / net capital inflow of $270B).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> To reduce a $270B trade deficit without cutting domestic investment I, the economy must either raise private saving S or reduce the fiscal deficit (G − T).

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** Profits earned by a German-owned automobile manufacturing plant located in South Carolina (United States) are included in:

- **[A]** US GDP and German GNP

- **[B]** German GDP and US GNP

- **[C]** Both US GDP and US GNP

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** Output produced physically inside South Carolina is part of US GDP (geographic basis), while the profit belongs to German factor owners and is included in German GNP (citizenship/ownership basis).

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** An economy has Private Saving (S) of $600 billion, Private Investment (I) of $520 billion, and a Government Fiscal Deficit (G − T) of $130 billion (so T − G = −$130 billion). Its trade balance (X − M) is:

- **[A]** A trade surplus of $50 billion

- **[B]** A trade deficit of $50 billion

- **[C]** A trade deficit of $210 billion

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** (X − M) = (S − I) + (T − G) = ($600B − $520B) + (−$130B) = +$80B − $130B = −$50 billion (a trade deficit of $50B).

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** Which of the following transactions is INCLUDED when calculating current-year Gross Domestic Product (GDP)?

- **[A]** Government Social Security transfer payments to retirees

- **[B]** The value of intermediate steel purchased by an automaker and embedded in cars sold this year

- **[C]** The dealer bid-ask commission charged on a secondary market stock trade

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option C.**
>
> **Explanation:** Financial brokerage services rendered in the current year are newly produced services included in GDP, whereas transfer payments (no output) and intermediate inputs (double counting) are excluded.
