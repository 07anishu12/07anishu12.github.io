## Introduction

DuPont Analysis Explained: How to Calculate Return on Equity is a core Level I Financial Statement Analysis topic (LM 10: Financial Analysis Techniques — 3-step and 5-step DuPont decomposition of ROE) focusing on decomposing Return on Equity into 3-factor (Net Margin, Asset Turnover, Leverage) and 5-factor (Tax Burden, Interest Burden, EBIT Margin, Asset Turnover, Leverage) drivers. This guide covers the governing equations (3-Step DuPont Identity and 5-Step Extended DuPont Identity), two verified numerical examples, a comparison table, and three original CFA-style practice questions.

## Concept Explanation & Institutional Framework

From first principles, decomposing Return on Equity into 3-factor (Net Margin, Asset Turnover, Leverage) and 5-factor (Tax Burden, Interest Burden, EBIT Margin, Asset Turnover, Leverage) drivers links theoretical valuation and risk mechanics to practical decisions in Financial Statement Analysis. 3-Step DuPont Identity (ROE = (Net Income / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity) = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Leverage) establishes the primary relationship (Separates profitability, asset utilization efficiency, and balance sheet financial leverage.), while 5-Step Extended DuPont Identity (ROE = (NI / EBT) × (EBT / EBIT) × (EBIT / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity)) governs Further decomposes Net Margin into Tax Burden (NI/EBT), Interest Burden (EBT/EBIT), and Operating Margin (EBIT/Revenue).

## Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

> [!NOTE]
> The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

### Formula 1: 3-Step DuPont Identity

```formula
ROE = (Net Income / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity) = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Leverage
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Separates profitability, asset utilization efficiency, and balance sheet financial leverage.

### Formula 2: 5-Step Extended DuPont Identity

```formula
ROE = (NI / EBT) × (EBT / EBIT) × (EBIT / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Further decomposes Net Margin into Tax Burden (NI/EBT), Interest Burden (EBT/EBIT), and Operating Margin (EBIT/Revenue).

## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: 5-Factor DuPont Decomposition of a Corporate Issuer

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
Income Statement ($M): Revenue = $2,000, EBIT = $300, EBT = $240, Net Income = $180. Balance Sheet Averages ($M): Average Total Assets = $1,600, Average Shareholders' Equity = $800.

```formula
ROE = (Net Income / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity) = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Leverage
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Step 1: Tax Burden = NI / EBT = 180 / 240 = 0.7500 (75%, implying 25% effective tax rate). Step 2: Interest Burden = EBT / EBIT = 240 / 300 = 0.8000 (80%). Step 3: EBIT Margin = EBIT / Revenue = 300 / 2,000 = 15.00%. (Check Net Margin = 0.75 × 0.80 × 15.0% = 9.00%). Step 4: Asset Turnover = 2,000 / 1,600 = 1.2500x. Step 5: Financial Leverage = 1,600 / 800 = 2.0000x. Step 6: ROE = 0.75 × 0.80 × 0.15 × 1.25 × 2.00 = 0.2250 = 22.50% (matching $180M / $800M = 22.50%).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> The 5-step DuPont reveals that a 15% operating margin is scaled by 0.80 interest burden and 0.75 tax burden into a 9% net margin, then multiplied by 1.25x turnover and 2.0x leverage to reach 22.50% ROE.

### Worked Example 2: Diagnosing a Multi-Year ROE Decline via DuPont Attribution

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
Between 2024 and 2026, a retailer's ROE fell from 18.0% to 14.4%. Over that span: Tax Burden stayed at 0.80, Interest Burden fell from 0.90 to 0.75, EBIT Margin stayed at 10.0%, Asset Turnover fell from 1.25x to 1.20x, and Leverage stayed at 2.00x.

```formula
ROE = (NI / EBT) × (EBT / EBIT) × (EBIT / Revenue) × (Revenue / Avg Assets) × (Avg Assets / Avg Equity)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

2024 ROE = 0.80 × 0.90 × 10.0% × 1.25 × 2.00 = 18.00%. 2026 ROE = 0.80 × 0.75 × 10.0% × 1.20 × 2.00 = 14.40%.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Core operating margin (10.0%) did not deteriorate; instead, higher borrowing costs (Interest Burden dropping from 0.90 to 0.75) and slower inventory turnover accounted for the entire 360 bps drop in ROE.

## Comparison & Reference Analysis

| DuPont Component | Ratio Definition | Economic Meaning | Effect of Increase on ROE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax Burden | Net Income / EBT (= 1 − Eff Tax Rate) | Fraction of pre-tax profit kept after tax | Higher ratio increases ROE |
| Interest Burden | EBT / EBIT | Fraction of EBIT kept after interest (= 1 if debt=0) | Higher ratio increases ROE |
| Operating (EBIT) Margin | EBIT / Revenue | Pre-financing operating pricing power | Higher margin increases ROE |
| Total Asset Turnover | Revenue / Average Total Assets | Sales generated per dollar of assets | Higher turnover increases ROE |
| Financial Leverage | Average Total Assets / Average Equity | Equity multiplier (1 + Total Liabilities/Equity) | Increases ROE if ROA > after-tax debt cost |

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** A company has a Tax Burden (NI/EBT) of 0.75, an Interest Burden (EBT/EBIT) of 0.80, an EBIT Margin of 12.0%, Total Asset Turnover of 1.50x, and a Debt-to-Assets ratio (Total Liabilities / Total Assets) of 50.0%. Its Return on Equity (ROE) is closest to:

- **[A]** 10.80%

- **[B]** 16.20%

- **[C]** 21.60%

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option C.**
>
> **Explanation:** If Total Liabilities / Assets = 50%, then Equity / Assets = 50%, so Financial Leverage (Assets / Equity) = 1 / 0.50 = 2.00x. ROE = 0.75 × 0.80 × 0.12 × 1.50 × 2.00 = 0.2160 = 21.60%.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** In the 5-step DuPont formula, if a company retires all of its interest-bearing debt, its Interest Burden ratio (EBT / EBIT) will:

- **[A]** Equal 0.00

- **[B]** Equal 1.00

- **[C]** Exceed 1.00

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** With zero interest expense, EBT equals EBIT, so the Interest Burden ratio EBT / EBIT = 1.00 (its theoretical maximum in the absence of non-operating interest income).

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** A firm's ROE increased from 14% to 17% over the past year while its Return on Assets (ROA = Net Margin × Asset Turnover) decreased from 7.0% to 6.8%. The increase in ROE was driven entirely by:

- **[A]** Improved operating cost control

- **[B]** Higher total asset turnover

- **[C]** Increased financial leverage (Assets / Equity rising from 2.0x to 2.5x)

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option C.**
>
> **Explanation:** Since ROE = ROA × Financial Leverage, if ROA declined (7.0% -> 6.8%) while ROE rose (14% -> 17%), Financial Leverage must have increased from 14/7 = 2.0x to 17/6.8 = 2.5x.

## Common Mistakes & Exam Traps to Avoid

- **Periodicity Mismatch: Failing to align compounding frequency in 3-Step DuPont Identity.**

- **Timing Errors: Mixing beginning and ending balances in 5-Step Extended DuPont Identity.**

- **Distractor Traps: Stopping at an unadjusted intermediate step.**
