## Core Formulas & Mathematical Equations

> [!NOTE]
> The following analytical equations govern problem-solving and numerical questions across this curriculum reading.

### Formula 1: WACC & Modigliani-Miller Proposition I with Taxes

```formula
WACC = w_d × r_d × (1 − t) + w_p × r_p + w_e × r_e

V_L = V_U + (t × D)
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** In the presence of corporate tax shield t×D, debt lowers WACC and increases levered firm value V_L until financial distress costs offset the tax shield.

### Formula 2: Economic Profit & Supplier Trade Credit Cost (EAR)

```formula
EVA = NOPAT − (WACC × Invested Capital)

EAR_TradeCredit = [1 + Discount / (1 − Discount)]^(365 / (Net Days − Discount Days)) − 1
```

**Institutional Context & Variable Breakdown:** Measures value creation above WACC and the effective annual cost of forgoing early-payment supplier discounts (e.g., 2/10 net 30).

## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Calculating the Effective Annual Cost of Forgoing Supplier Trade Credit (2/10, Net 40)

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A corporate treasurer buys $5,000,000 of raw materials on terms of '2/10, net 40' (a 2.0% discount if paid by Day 10, otherwise full payment due on Day 40). The company's short-term bank credit line costs 8.50% per year. Should the company take the discount?

```formula
WACC = w_d × r_d × (1 − t) + w_p × r_p + w_e × r_e

V_L = V_U + (t × D)
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Periodic 30-day rate of forgoing the discount = 0.02 / (1 − 0.02) = 0.02 / 0.98 = 2.0408%. Number of 30-day periods in a 365-day year = 365 / (40 − 10) = 365 / 30 = 12.1667. Effective Annual Cost (EAR) = (1 + 0.02 / 0.98)^(365/30) − 1 = (1.020408)^12.1667 − 1 = 27.86%!

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Because the 27.86% implied annual cost of stretching trade credit beyond Day 10 vastly exceeds the 8.50% bank line, the treasurer should borrow at 8.50% on Day 10 and take the 2% discount.

### Worked Example 2: Static Trade-Off Theory of Capital Structure: Tax Shield vs. Financial Distress

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
An unlevered firm has Market Value V_U = $400M. It issues $150M of permanent debt at a corporate tax rate of t = 25%. Investment bankers estimate the present value of expected financial distress and agency costs at $12.5M.

```formula
EVA = NOPAT − (WACC × Invested Capital)

EAR_TradeCredit = [1 + Discount / (1 − Discount)]^(365 / (Net Days − Discount Days)) − 1
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

PV of Tax Shield = t × D = 0.25 × $150M = +$37.5M. Levered Firm Value under Static Trade-Off Theory: V_L = V_U + (t × D) − PV(Financial Distress Costs) = $400M + $37.5M − $12.5M = $425.0M.

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Optimal capital structure is reached at the debt ratio where the marginal tax shield benefit of $1 more debt exactly equals the marginal present value of financial distress costs.

## Comparison & Reference Analysis

| Organizational Form | Owner Legal Liability | Taxation Treatment | Access to Public Capital |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietorship | Unlimited personal liability | Pass-through (personal income tax) | Very limited (owner wealth/bank) |
| General / Limited Partnership | GP: Unlimited; LP: Limited to capital | Pass-through taxation | Private LP commitments (PE/VC/Law) |
| Limited Liability Company (LLC) | Limited liability for all members | Pass-through (avoids double tax) | Capped member limits / private |
| Public Corporation (C-Corp / PLC) | Strictly Limited Liability | Double taxation (Corporate + Dividend) | Unlimited public equity & bond markets |

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** A company purchases inventory on trade credit terms of '1/10, net 30'. Using a 365-day year, the effective annual cost (EAR) of forgoing the discount and paying on Day 30 is closest to:

- **[A]** 18.43%

- **[B]** 20.13%

- **[C]** 37.24%

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** EAR = [1 + (0.01 / 0.99)]^(365 / (30 − 10)) − 1 = (1.010101)^(18.25) − 1 = 1.20132 − 1 = 20.13%.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** Under the Static Trade-Off Theory of capital structure, as a company increases its debt-to-equity ratio from zero toward high leverage, its Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):

- **[A]** Decreases continuously toward the after-tax cost of debt

- **[B]** Initially decreases due to the tax deductibility of interest, reaches a minimum at the optimal capital structure, and then increases as financial distress costs dominate

- **[C]** Remains completely constant at all leverage levels

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** At low to moderate debt levels, the tax shield (t×D) lowers WACC; beyond the optimal point, rising bankruptcy and agency risk push both r_d and r_e sharply higher, raising WACC.

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** Which corporate characteristic distinguishes a public corporation from a general partnership?

- **[A]** Pass-through taxation of profits directly to owners

- **[B]** Free transferability of ownership shares, separation of ownership and management control, and limited shareholder liability

- **[C]** Unlimited personal liability of shareholders for corporate debts

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Public corporations feature limited liability, separation of ownership (shareholders) and control (board/executives), and freely tradable shares.
