## Step-by-Step Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 1: Calculating Justified Price-to-Book (P/B) and Intrinsic Price per Share

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A bank has a Book Value per Share (BVPS) of $40.00, an expected ROE of 13.5%, a required return on equity of r = 9.5%, and a sustainable growth rate of g = 4.5%.

```formula
Justified P / B = (ROE − g) / (r − g) = 1 + [(ROE − r) / (r − g)]
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

Justified P/B = (ROE − g) / (r − g) = (13.5% − 4.5%) / (9.5% − 4.5%) = 9.0% / 5.0% = 1.80x. Intrinsic Value per Share V_0 = 1.80 × $40.00 = $72.00 (verified via Gordon Growth: E_1 = 13.5% × $40 = $5.40; b = 4.5%/13.5% = 1/3; D_1 = $5.40 × 2/3 = $3.60; V_0 = $3.60 / (0.095 − 0.045) = $72.00!).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> The Justified P/B formula (ROE − g)/(r − g) is algebraically identical to the Gordon Growth Model and is heavily tested on CFA Level I and Level II.

### Worked Example 2: Short Sale Proceeds, Margin Requirement, and Margin Call Trigger Price

**Problem & Given Scenario:**
A hedge fund sells short 2,000 shares of Stock K at P_0 = $100.00 ($200,000 short sale proceeds). The initial margin requirement is 50% ($100,000 deposit, so Total Margin Assets = $300,000), and the maintenance margin requirement is 30%. Find the stock price P_Call that triggers a short-position margin call.

```formula
EV = Market Cap_Common + Market Value_Preferred + Market Value_Debt − Cash & Short-Term Investments
```

**Step-by-Step Substitution & Calculation:**

For a short sale, P_Call = P_0 × (1 + Initial Margin %) / (1 + Maintenance Margin %) = $100.00 × (1 + 0.50) / (1 + 0.30) = $150.00 / 1.30 = $115.38 per share. (Check at $115.38: Short liability = $230,769; Equity = $300,000 − $230,769 = $69,231; $69,231 / $230,769 = 30.0%!).

> [!TIP]
> **Financial Interpretation & Exam Takeaway:**
> Notice the symmetry: Long Margin Call multiplies P_0 by (1 − IM)/(1 − MM), whereas Short Margin Call multiplies P_0 by (1 + IM)/(1 + MM).

## CFA Practice Questions & Detailed Solutions

### Practice Question 1

**Question:** A trader sells short 1,000 shares of a stock at $65.00 per share with an initial margin requirement of 50% and a maintenance margin requirement of 25%. A margin call will be triggered if the stock price rises above:

- **[A]** $75.00

- **[B]** $78.00

- **[C]** $86.67

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** For a short position, P_Call = P_0 × (1 + Initial Margin) / (1 + Maintenance Margin) = $65.00 × (1.50 / 1.25) = $65.00 × 1.20 = $78.00.

### Practice Question 2

**Question:** In Michael Porter's Five Forces framework for industry analysis, an industry is most likely to earn persistent returns above its cost of capital (ROIC > WACC) when:

- **[A]** Switching costs for customers are high, economies of scale create high barriers to entry, and few close substitutes exist

- **[B]** Exit barriers are high, products are undifferentiated commodities, and suppliers are concentrated monopolies

- **[C]** Industry capacity grows faster than customer demand

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option A.**
>
> **Explanation:** High switching costs, high entry barriers, and lack of substitutes suppress competitive rivalry and protect pricing power.

### Practice Question 3

**Question:** A bank has a Return on Equity (ROE) of 14.0%, a sustainable growth rate (g) of 4.0%, and a required return on equity (r) of 9.0%. Its justified Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio is:

- **[A]** 1.56x

- **[B]** 2.00x

- **[C]** 2.80x

> [!TIP]
> **Correct Answer: Option B.**
>
> **Explanation:** Justified P/B = (ROE − g) / (r − g) = (14.0% − 4.0%) / (9.0% − 4.0%) = 10.0% / 5.0% = 2.00x.
